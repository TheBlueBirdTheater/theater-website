# Create Add-Ons

Add-Ons don't have their own separate page within Ludus. We have built Add-Ons to attach easily to a specific Event, Collection, or Class. This makes things simple for both you and your patrons.

### Initial Set up

1.  Go to the Event, Collection, or Class to which you'd like to attach Add-Ons and click **Pricing** --> **Add-Ons**.
    
2.  To create a new Add-On, click the green **+Add-On** button. To copy from another event/collection, click **Copy from another event**. _Suggested Article_: [Copy Add-Ons from another event](https://support.ludus.com/copy-add-ons-from-another-event)
    
3.  Fill out the applicable settings for your Add-Ons. _Terms and definitions are found at the bottom of the page_
    
4.  Click **Done** to save
    

### User Terms and Definitions

**Name** - The title of the Add-On overall. (e.g. T-Shirt)

**Image** - An optional image that will be displayed above the Add-On to give patrons a visual of what they will be buying.

**Description** - An optional field where you can elaborate on the details of the Add-On.

**Assignment** - Which event times/class times this Add-On is available for.

_Suggested Article_: [Attach Add-Ons to specific event time(s)](https://support.ludus.com/attach-add-ons-to-specific-event-times)

**Variants** - Different variations of the same base Add-On. An example would be a T-shirt with multiple size options. Your Add-On will need one variant.

-   **Variant Name** - The title of your variant. (e.g. Small T-shirt)

-   **Price** - The cost of the Add-On.

-   **Inventory** - The total number of this specific variant that you have available to sell.

**Limit Per Patron** - A limit to how many of this specific Add-On a patron can purchase.

_Suggested Article_: [Set limits on Add-Ons](https://support.ludus.com/set-limits-on-add-ons)

**Access** - Who has access to purchase the Add-On.

_Suggested Article_: [Set limits on Add-Ons](https://support.ludus.com/set-limits-on-add-ons)

**Custom Fees** - Collect additional custom fees on this price type. _Suggested Article_: [Custom Fees](https://support.ludus.com/custom-fees "Custom Fees")

### Advanced Options

**Special Passcode** - A passcode that will protect the Add-On. Users will need to insert the code to purchase the Add-On.

**Attach to Ticket Price** - Connect this Add-On to a specific ticket price and only have it appear if they are choosing that ticket price. By default, patrons will be required to select an option for the Add-On that is attached to the ticket price. (Great for dinner theatre) There is an additional setting you can choose to make the attached Add-On optional.

_Suggested Article_: [Attach Add-Ons to a specific ticket price](https://support.ludus.com/attach-add-ons-to-a-specific-ticket-price)

**Disable Transfers** - Prevent this Add-On from being transferred to another patron should you allow that setting on the event in general. (Only present on Event Add-ons)

**Disable Pay Later** - By checking this box, you will prevent patrons from being able to request an invoice at checkout. (Only present on Add-Ons attached to Collections and Classes.)

**Form** - Collect additional information from purchasers aside from name, email, phone number, and address.

**Availability** - The window in which sales will be available for the Add-On.

**Tax Exempt** - Do not collect Taxes for this Add-On. (Only appears when Sales Tax is in use on the account)

_Suggested Article:_ [Taxes](https://support.ludus.com/taxes "Taxes")
