# Sort Add-Ons

If you have multiple Add-Ons, you may wish to customize the order in which they appear for you and your patrons.

To do so, navigate to the event or collection that houses your Add-Ons. Next to the title, click **Pricing** **\--> Add-Ons**. Then, click **Sort Add-Ons**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-a1dP9ImR_y9A7GjFmvr5_-20250328-182805.png?format=webp "Document image")

Drag them into the desired order and click **Save Sort**.

Add-Ons appear two per row, side by side. The item you place in the second position will appear in the first row on the right side.
