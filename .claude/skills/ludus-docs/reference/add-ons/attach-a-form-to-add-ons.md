# Attach a Form to Add-Ons

You may wish to ask additional questions of purchasers if they select one of the Add-Ons. To accomplish this, you will want to attach a Form to the Add-On. Just like attaching Forms to other areas within Ludus, this is incredibly simple.

Before attaching it, make sure you have built your Form.

_Suggested Article:_ [Create a Form](https://support.ludus.com/create-a-form)

1.  Locate the event/collection the Add-Ons are attached to.
    
2.  Next to the title, click **Pricing** --> **Add-Ons**.
    
3.  Click **Edit** next to the desired Add-On.
    
4.  Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Advanced Options**.
    
5.  For the section titled **Form**, choose the desired Form.
    
6.  Click **Save Changes**.
