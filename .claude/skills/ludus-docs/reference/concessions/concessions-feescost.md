# Concessions Fees/Cost

When using Concessions, the credit card processing fees are **2.9% + $0.30/Transaction.** You can view the fees by going to **Settings** (the gear at the top of your account) -> **Fees**.

Concession fees can be passed on or absorbed by your group under **Concessions** (at the top of your account) -> **Settings** -> **Fees**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/FPn1ctblLMy8ezSdaxqM4_screenshot-2026-08-05-at-123422-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Tips and donations fees _are always absorbed_ and are not passed on to patrons.

To see all of the Ludus fees and costs, please see the [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table) .
