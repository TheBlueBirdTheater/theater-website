# Selling a Concessions Item on the App

You must have the **Ludus Mobile App** on your device to sell a Concession Item.

Head over to [Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-mobile) to learn more about the app and how to download and sign into that.

### Sell an Item in the App

1.  Start by logging into your Ludus Box Office App.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/G7xqmndH3JIi7hqtySFEP_img-0787.jpg?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Once logged in, select **Concessions** at the bottom of your app (along the menu bar).
    
3.  On the **Concessions** Tab, you will be able to:
    
    -   Select the Group you are selling to
    
    -   Sell Items
    
    -   Connect to a Printer (if needed)
    
    -   Connect to the Mobile Reader (if using - [Mobile Readers](https://support.ludus.com/mobile-readers) )
    
    -   View Transactions
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/5zuCJ478-qOPI0heAG0H-_img-1047.jpg?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  To sell an **Item**, select **Sell Items.**
    
    From here, you can filter by the Tags you set up under [Concessions Settings](https://support.ludus.com/concessions-settings) (along the top) or search for the item you need.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/QET2JO0R1GWnPXAeEiBys_img-1054.jpg?format=webp "Document image")
    
    When selecting an item, if there are no variants, you can tap the item to add it to your cart (tap it as many times as needed).
    
    If the item has variants, tap the item to select a variant, then use the "+" next to it to add as many as needed to the cart.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/TGJYoLNIdCnRDNoj_i_Ib_img-1055.jpg?format=webp "Document image")
    
    If any items are sold out, you will see "Out of Stock" listed by the item(s) on the app (like with the Popcorn below):
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/1FW0mD99hHW5OuZ8r0pGj_img-0797.jpg?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  Once you have selected the items (including any **Options** or **Variations** of the Items), you can choose the cart icon (top right corner).
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/t3jhWR6czip9gj0XulbLm-20260422-141320.jpeg?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  When in the cart, you can:
    
    -   Add (Link) a Patron
    
    -   Add A Discount
    
    -   Add A Donation
    
7.  Select **Process Order** to bring up the payment screen.
    
8.  On the **Payment Screen**, you will see the final numbers for the sale, and you can run the sale as:
    
    -   Card (with the Mobile Reader or Manually)
    
    -   Cash
    
    -   Gift Card
    
    -   Comp
    
    -   Other (If those are set up on your account - [Other Payment Options](https://support.ludus.com/other-payment-options))
    
    If your Mobile Reader is not connected or you do not have one, you can enter the Credit Card information manually via the text at the bottom.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-y-qJRS-bn8KJY4OpQfwMD-20250226-190041.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    If a purchase is over $15, the patron will be asked to provide a signature.
