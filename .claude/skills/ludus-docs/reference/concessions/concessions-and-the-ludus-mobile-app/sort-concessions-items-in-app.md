# Sort Concessions Items in App

If you wish to have your retail inventory items appear in a specific order in the app, this can be easily accomplished through the sort function in your Ludus admin panel.

This could be incredibly helpful if you want to keep certain items together (such as snacks or apparel) in your inventory.

To **sort** those items and have that reflected in the [Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-mobile) App, go to **Retail** (at the top of your account) -> **Dashboard** -> **Sort Items**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-u-6FrNsDcxjkK-SxyHs3e-20250805-144035.png?format=webp "Document image")

From here, you can drag and drop the inventory items to the order you want them to show in the app. Be sure to click **Save Sort** once finished!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-67OpoqnlOIze7szffWwUQ-20250805-144402.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once saved, the sorting of your inventory will be reflected on your app!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/QET2JO0R1GWnPXAeEiBys_img-1054.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

The inventory sort is only reflected in the app and will not be present on your admin dashboard.
