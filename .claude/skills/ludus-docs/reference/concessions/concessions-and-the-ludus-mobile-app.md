# Concessions and the Ludus Mobile App

The Ludus Box Office App can be used to sell your Concessions Items and process those sales.

Before checking out how to, you will want to make sure you have downloaded and signed in to the Ludus Box Office App.

More information on the app and how to utilize it can be found here: [Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-mobile)

The Ludus Mobile app is available on both Android and iOS.
