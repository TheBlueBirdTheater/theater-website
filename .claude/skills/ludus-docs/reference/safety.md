# Safety

## What can this section help with?

This hub contains a list of resources and tools that you might find helpful should you need to create a socially distant event.

## Why use these resources?

Several features have proved useful during the pandemic, but can also be used for the cold/flu season or for setting policies you wish your patrons to agree to.

## What resources are available to me?

Ludus was built for the performing arts by people who care about them, and we want to make your life easier. The Safety portion of Ludus was created specifically with Illness in mind and provides items regarding:

-   [Safety Resources](https://support.ludus.com/safety-resources)

-   [Safety Checklist](https://support.ludus.com/safety-checklist)

-   [COVID-19 Templates](https://support.ludus.com/covid-19-templates)
