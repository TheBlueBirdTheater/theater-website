# Where can I share feedback and ideas?

At Ludus, we truly listen to our customers, and we always want an open door for ideas and feedback. Through Ludus Pulse, you can share feedback and react to other customers' feedback.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-a7tztJLDW1CjH7RukEhrl-20250429-181304.png?format=webp "Document image")

**Ludus Pulse** is a collaborative space where you can view recommendations, but you can also:

-   Keep track of your requests/ideas

-   View what's new in the product

-   See the status of recommendations on Pulse, including ->

-   What is **Open** (submitted, but not being looked at yet)

-   What is **Under Review** (being looked into)

-   What is **Planned** (on the product roadmap)

-   What is **In Progress** (currently being created)

-   What is **Completed** (the recommendation/idea has been added to the product)

-   What is **Closed** (the item was resolved)

## How do I access Pulse?

Accessing Ludus Pulse is simple, and there are three main ways to access and contribute ideas.

### Ludus Dropdown

The first way to access Ludus Pulse is when you are signed into your admin panel. If you go to the **Ludus** (top left corner) -> and **click**, you will see **Pulse** as an option in the dropdown. You can then select Pulse to be brought to the site.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV--ktKhVmRIlLOJuuGUA9Nn-20250429-180552.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Chat Widget

Another way to access the Widget is through the chat widget/box at the bottom right corner of your admin panel.

Once you open the widget, you will scroll through the available items to Ludus Pulse. When you select this, it will bring up Pulse for you!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-zBgumBPZQgN_H_SbeSpz3-20250429-180909.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Pulse Web Address

You can access Pulse through the URL: [http://www.ludus.com/pulse](http://www.ludus.com/pulse "http://www.ludus.com/pulse")

You MUST already be signed into your Ludus account to access the URL.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-a7tztJLDW1CjH7RukEhrl-20250429-181304.png?format=webp "Document image")

### **The “?”**

You will select the **“?” (top right)** → Select **Pulse**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-f1VPVc18zzHxZ9O1Nfqei-20250512-133053.png?format=webp "Document image")
