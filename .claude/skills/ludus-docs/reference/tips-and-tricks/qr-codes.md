# QR Codes

When setting up your account, there are a few locations where you can access and use QR Codes within Ludus. These **QR Codes** have already been created for you to use, so they simply need to be copied and saved as needed!

### Account QR Code

1.  Navigate to the "**?**" at the top of your Ludus account.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/vUr3W76FM3mXMKZjUYGs2_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-09t200534699.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Once on the "**?**" page, you will select **Guide Me** under **Go live with ticket sales**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/xS9QOU1ZDN96fBz8zri6T_qr-codes-archbee-document.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  From here, you can scroll down to the **QR Code,** which can be copied and used as needed.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/6-GLF3fIPszRkSBl0ji9m_screen-shot-2024-01-30-at-102904-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### Event QR Code

1.  Navigate to the **More** dropdown next to your **Event**. In this dropdown, you will select the **Share** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/QfaO2vIcQul7sJdFQ5Q4K_screen-shot-2024-01-30-at-103339-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Once on the **Share** page, you will be able to scroll down and access the **QR Code** for that event. This can be copied and used as needed.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/0zzglB00xg9eADYd1FFFo_screen-shot-2024-01-30-at-103626-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### Collection and Class QR Codes

1.  Navigate to the **Options** dropdown next to your Collection or Class. In this dropdown, you will select the **Share** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/U77xrEzvkKsqmYTrALzDA_qr-codes-archbee-document-1.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Once on the **Share** page, you will be able to scroll down and access the **QR Code** for that Collection or Class. This can be copied and used as needed.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/7THpWp06XgZHUYv6xAHt0_qr-codes-archbee-document-2.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### Fundraiser QR Code

1.  Navigate to **Fundraising** (at the top) --> **Fundraisers**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/b2Yzvt7d2KRwxcCkrA8Ui_qr-codes-archbee-document-3.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  From here you will select Share next to the **Fundraiser**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/nIRaeDnUm_rfhQVd0t9bw_screen-shot-2024-01-30-at-104650-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Once under the **Share** tab, you will be able to scroll down and access the **QR Code** for that Fundraiser. This can be copied and used as needed.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/-i7meGMQEAk07wZR5dsb0_qr-codes-archbee-document-4.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### Passes QR CODE

1.  Navigate to **More** (at the top of your admin panel) --> **Passes**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/0GL0wAzzJ_x8oYHdxQc22_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-09t201205670.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  From here, you will select **Options** (next to the Pass) --> **Share**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/3kBmfTgvpeQch7fx6WXWQ_screen-shot-2024-01-30-at-105816-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Once under the **Share** tab, you will be able to scroll down and access the **QR Code** for that Pass. This can be copied and used as needed.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/bQ__sUcqfCfgbdG2IJ8A-_qr-codes-archbee-document-5.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### Volunteer Management QR Codes

1.  Navigate to Volunteers by clicking **More**, on the top navigation bar and select **Volunteers**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/qbkmRM4lXVeANTcn77qC2_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-09t201416069.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Select **Settings** and then click on the **Share** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/HcWyhScoapVJrfuBTHcaK_qr-codes-archbee-document-6.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    To obtain a QR code for a specific opportunity click the **Options** button next to the opportunity and click **Share**.
