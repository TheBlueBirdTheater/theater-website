# Speed Up Box Office Lines

In this article, we'll review some tips to speed up lines at the box office before the event. All of the following tips are merely suggestions. You can choose which you'd like to use for your program/organization.

### Box Office View

The Box Office View offers a quick checkout process and allows you to complete credit, cash, and comp sales (similar to the **New Order** sales process). This can be helpful with in-person sales as the sale can be completed all on one screen. We recommend utilizing this view if you aren't planning on capturing patron information such as name and email. Also recommended for general admission events.

### Skip the Printing

There is no additional equipment needed to operate Ludus. All you need is a device with an internet connection to make ticket sales. While printing tickets for patrons can be nice, it can definitely take up some time from a box office perspective.

**Send tickets digitally**: You can send patrons their tickets digitally via email or text. Especially if you entered their patron information when selecting the tickets, it is as simple as one click to send tickets digitally. Just choose **Digital** from the retrieval screen. _Suggested Article_: [Overview of retrieval options](https://support.ludus.com/overview-of-retrieval-options) (Bonus, you save paper)

**Utilize the Ludus App for Check-In**: The ticket scanning app available on iOS and Android allows you to scan tickets that patrons bring to the event. Additionally, there is a manual list that you can use when patrons don't have a printed or digital ticket. From the **Patrons** tab on the app, simply search for the patron by name and check them in with the click of a button.

### Look Up/Skip Patron Information

When making a new order from the admin panel, all fields for patron information are completely optional. We recommend at least a name and an email for easy tracking of orders later on; however, you can complete a purchase while leaving this section blank

**If they've been a patron with you before**: You can click the green **Magnifying Glass** button (🔍) and look up their name/email. Then, click **Select** and all of the information that is on file will autofill and you can add/update as needed.

### Utilize Payment Terminals

While there is no additional equipment needed to operate a sale within Ludus, we do have mobile readers and full-standard terminals available for purchase. This can speed up card transactions by saving the time of manually typing in card information for each purchase.

[Mobile Readers](https://support.ludus.com/mobile-readers)

[Standard Terminal BBPOS WisePOS](https://support.ludus.com/standard-terminal-bbpos-wisepos)

### QR Codes in the Lobby

We have seen many organizations and programs have success with placing QR codes for online ticket sales in their lobby. This allows patrons who plan on paying with a card to select their seats from the convenience of their own mobile device. They can simply scan the QR code and be taken directly to the event on your Ludus page to purchase tickets. Everything updates in real time so you don't have to worry about double-selling the same seats. This will drastically cut down the number of people in your box office line while still allowing that option for those who prefer.

To get a downloadable QR code for your event, go to the main **Events** tab and next to the event title, click **More** --> **Share**. To learn more, check out this article.

_Suggested Article_: [Share an Event](https://support.ludus.com/share-an-event)

### Incentivize Online/Early Sales

We have programs that see 95%+ of their sales done online, which drastically cuts down box office lines and makes the day-of much smoother. How do they do this? They charge more at the door.

For example, we have a high school theatre program that sells tickets for $15 online. If you wait to purchase tickets at the door, they charge $5 more ($20). They also advertise their online ticketing website's URL everywhere so patrons know they can purchase online.
