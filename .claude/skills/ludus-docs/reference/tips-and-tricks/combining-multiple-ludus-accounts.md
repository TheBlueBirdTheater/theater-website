# Combining Multiple Ludus Accounts

If you have multiple Ludus accounts, while they cannot be merged into one, you can move information from one to the other.

**A few keys to making this a successful transition:**

-   Inform your patrons of the move or merger to another Ludus account.

-   Share the new subdomain and any pertinent information.

-   Check out [Marketing](https://support.ludus.com/marketing) for ways to reach all of your patrons.

-   Keep the old Ludus account and import patrons over to the new Ludus account.

-   By doing this, you will not lose any data and can transfer information at your convenience.

-   Import any other data that is historical (donation data, ticket sales, etc).

-   For help with this, reach out to the [Live Chat Support](https://support.ludus.com/live-chat-support) .

-   Be sure to check out [Import Templates and Instructions](https://support.ludus.com/import-templates-and-instructions) as well, for what you can transfer over.

Ludus Support is here to help, so if you have any questions or need additional help reach out directly to the team so we can assist!
