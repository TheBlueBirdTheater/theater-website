# Process a Donation

When you are processing a sale for an Event, Collection, Class, or Pass, you are able to tack on a donation.

_Suggested Article_: [Add a donation to a purchase](https://support.ludus.com/add-a-donation-to-a-purchase)

However, there may be times when you'd like to record/process a stand-alone donation. This is great for recording checks or cash donations to ensure that your reporting gives a complete view of your fundraising totals.

To process a donation:

1.  Navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Donations**.
    
2.  Click **New Donation**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/4iRe2S0Lb9Kv184MutBHw_screen-shot-2023-09-01-at-15746-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Choose whether this is a **Credit** transaction or a **Cash** transaction from the tabs at the top. (If you have custom payment options set up, such as "Check", you will see a tab for **Other**.)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/PK5vndlspfwOpGetlan9S_screen-shot-2023-09-01-at-24138-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Enter the donation amount and fill out the patron details.
    
    If the patron is already in your Ludus patron database, you can autofill all their information by looking them up in the search bar.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/mvyQznaYkOXgz19Y_5wyE_screen-shot-2023-09-01-at-25033-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  If you have donation categories set up, you can choose one from the drop-down menu. Then, insert a memo (optional).
    
    For non-credit payment options, you can enter the date the donation was made. This helps ensure your reporting reflects the date the donation was actually made, Not the date you entered the donation into the system. Leave this section blank to use the current date.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/yKRGsyqwe1YN_yRMCWYuG_screen-shot-2023-09-01-at-25444-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  De-select the checkbox if you would not like to send a confirmation email to the patron entered above.
    
7.  For credit transactions, click **Proceed to payment details** and collect payment.
    
8.  Click **Collect** to finish.
