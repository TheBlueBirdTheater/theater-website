# Can I turn Cash Sales Off?

While there is a "_**Cash**_" sale option on the admin site, this will not appear on the patron site. This is ONLY an option for your account: Sellers and Full admin. There is currently no way to turn off cash sales, as they are exclusive to the admin site.

In addition to Cash sales, Other payments, Comp orders, and Invoicing are only available on the admin site as well.
