# Overview of payment options

## Online Sales (Patron Self-Serve)

When patrons purchase tickets online themselves (self-serve), they can select from the following payment methods:

-   **Card (credit or debit)** — all major card brands including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.

-   **Apple Pay** — Patrons will be given an Apple Pay option if their browser/device supports Apple Pay

-   **Google Pay** — Patrons will be given a Google Pay option if their browser/device supports Google Pay

## At the Box Office (In-Person)

If you are processing sales in person or over the phone at your box office, you'll use your Ludus admin panel. Patrons can pay by:

-   **Card (credit or debit)** — All major card brands including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.

-   **Cash** — As an admin, you and your sellers have the ability to mark sales as "Cash".

-   **Contactless** — A Ludus Payment Terminal is required for contactless payments like Apple Pay, Google Pay, and tap-to-pay methods in-person. A Payment Terminal can be purchased in your admin panel under **More** -> **Supplies Shop**.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Payment Devices](https://support.ludus.com/payment-devices)

-   **Custom Payment Methods** - Set custom fields for payments such as "Check"

-   _Suggested Article_: [Add Other payment options](https://support.ludus.com/add-other-payment-options)

-   **Invoice** - Admins can issue an invoice for payment for Events, Collections, or Classes. through the admin panel so payment can be taken later either online or in person.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Create/Send an Invoice for an order](y4T6T6CHi8LLzJQbFePO7)
