# View/Sort Students

Once students have been added to your Ludus account (either through a Class, individually, or through a bulk upload) you can view a full list of students.

You will want to go to **More** (at the top of your admin panel) -> **Students** -> and from here you will be able to view all currently registered students on your account!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/ki9ObGabkQ9hYvnN4Zu6d_screen-shot-2023-08-29-at-25412-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### **At this point, you can also break down your sorting by Adult:**

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/wQ8v1lHVqP3fInovTNcx2_screen-shot-2023-08-29-at-25418-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### **You can also sort by Under 18:**

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/IyCdkrBQMlNbTWG1iFmon_screen-shot-2023-08-29-at-25437-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

This can be particularly useful if you have a large student database with a wide variety of students on your account.
