# Export Students from the Students Feature

If you need to export your students from Ludus at any point, you can do this through the Students feature.

## Export Students

1.  To export Students, go to More (at the top of the admin panel) -> Students.
    
2.  From here, navigate to More (at the top of the Students page) -> Export Students.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/qEdp07FjPxI74vD2pJh4W_screenshot-2024-06-24-at-113413-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Once exported, you can save or utilize your student data as needed.
