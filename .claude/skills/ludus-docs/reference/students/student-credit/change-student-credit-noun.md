# Change "Student" Credit noun

## Change "Student" Credit noun

If for some reason you do not wish to use the term "student" when referring to the credit system, this can easily be changed to something like "cast/crew" or anything else that might fit your group better.

To do this, you will go to **More** (at the top of your admin panel) -> **Students** -> **Settings**. From here you can choose to edit the name from "students" to whatever works best for your group.
