# View Student Credit Ledger

Once students start receiving credit, you will likely want to pull a report to view how many sales each student has credit for. This information is readily available in the student ledger. Before you can view your student ledgers, you will first want to add students to your Ludus account and enable the student credit option.

_Suggested Articles_: [Add Students](https://support.ludus.com/add-students), [Enable Student Credit](https://support.ludus.com/enable-student-credit)

To do this, you will go to **More** (at the top of your admin panel) -> **Students** -> **Credit Report**. From here, you can adjust the date range, select the types of sales associated with that student, and generate/export the information.

The amount found in the "Ledger Total" column will reflect the dollar amount of tickets that were attributed to the student. How you track or reward sales is up to you. Ludus does not provide any additional financial reward for student credit.
