# Turn off Student Credit Self-Sign Up

It is possible to disable the public-facing Student Credit Self-Sign Up option within the Students Settings area.

To do so, open the Students Settings screen located under **Students**.

Change the setting **Student Credit Self-Sign U**p to No.

Click **Save** at the bottom of the Settings screen.
