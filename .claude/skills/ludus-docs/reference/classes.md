# Classes

As an extension of our Collections feature, Classes allows you and your patrons to easily register for classes, workshops, camps, and more online.

Bring your class registrations to the next level by storing crucial information (date of birth, grade, allergies, emergency info, photo permission, etc.) year after year and allowing parents to manage it themselves.

Registrants can easily be viewed, messaged, and managed in one centralized location.

## Video Overview:

Classes is a premium add-on to your Ludus account. When you upgrade to Classes, we'll add an additional $0.05 to your Ludus convenience fee, which you can choose to pass on to registrants or absorb.

_Suggested Article_: [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table)

## What can this feature help with?

Create and set up Classes; they are the perfect way to offer time slots for individual lessons, small classes, and so much more!

## Why use this feature?

We have seen so many groups successfully set up and run Classes via this Collection feature. This is the perfect way to set up classes, private lessons, masterclasses, and other needs you might have.

## What makes this feature so special?

Ludus was built for the performing arts by people who care about them, and we want to make your life easier. The Classes feature in Ludus has so many amazing extras, including:

-   Specific Student Forms (only for Classes)

-   Customization to Forms

-   Class Rosters

-   The ability to create Invoices for Patrons

-   And so much more!

## So how does this feature work?

When you set up your first Class, you will be guided through the steps to create and configure it. Classes will be your patrons' (students') go-to space to sign up for your available classes and pay those fees. Including every possible detail for your students not only makes your life easier but also eases their minds!

## How do I create my first class?

To create a new Class, navigate to **Collections** --> **Classes**.

For more information about each field, check out the descriptions found in [Add a Class](https://support.ludus.com/add-a-class) .

Then, click **+Add Class**. Fill out the Basic Info, Files, Form, and Additional Options fields as needed.
