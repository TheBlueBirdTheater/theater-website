# AnywhereSeat

## What can this feature help with?

The easiest way to sell tickets to your virtual event!

Broadcast live streams, pre-recorded videos, and other linkable events all within one platform.

## Why use this feature?

If you want to offer multiple ways to view your events, this is the best way to do so.

## What makes this feature so special?

AnywhereSeat was built for the performing arts by people who care about them, and we want to make your life easier. The AnywhereSeat portion of Ludus was built to enable you to share your events in a variety of ways beyond the physical performance space. This includes:

-   **Livestreams**

-   **Recorded Events**

-   **Other Platforms** (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.)

## So how does this feature work?

AnywhereSeat is a separate site from Ludus, so you will need to set up your AnywhereSeat account separately to create these virtual events.

## How do I enable AnywhereSeat?

To enable AnywhereSeat, you will want to reach out to Ludus customer support via the chat widget located in the lower right-hand corner of your Ludus account.
