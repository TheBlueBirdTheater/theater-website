# Copy a Form

If you need to copy an older Form, perhaps you like the foundation of it but wish to update it without changing the original, that can easily be accomplished with the Copy function.

### To Copy a Form, you will want to:

1.  Click **More** at the top of your account → and select **Forms.**

2.  Select the 3 dots next to the Form you wish to replicate -> **Copy**.

3.  Enter the name of the duplicate form and click **Create Form**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/hvaBvqLPL3RTSTDADGKlT_screenshot-2026-06-30-at-33238-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once copied, all original questions will be copied over to the new form and you will be able to edit the form as needed. This will not affect any of the previously collected data for the original Form.
