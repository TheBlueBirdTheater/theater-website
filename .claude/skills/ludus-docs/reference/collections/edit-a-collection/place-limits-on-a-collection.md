# Place limits on a Collection

Within a Collection, you are able to place limits on the number of purchasers before a Collection becomes unavailable. Additionally, you can limit how many of each fee are purchased.

### Place Overall limits on a Collection

1.  To set a limit, navigate to **Collections** --> **Standard**.
    
2.  Then, next to the name of the desired Collection, click **Options** --> **Settings**.
    
3.  Scroll down to the section titled "Additional Options".
    
4.  For Registration Limit, click the check box that says "Limit total registrations" and insert the desired cap.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/gW0-Sk91b0qpUfDNZ50Nf_screen-shot-2023-08-15-at-32446-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  Click **Save Changes**.
    

### Limit the number of Fees per patron

1.  To set a per-fee limit, navigate to **Collections** --> **Standard**.
    
2.  Then, next to the name of the desired Collection, click **Pricing** --> **Fees**.
    
3.  Next to the desired fee, click **Edit**.
    
4.  Next to the "Limit Per Patron" section, click the checkbox.
    
5.  Insert the desired cap and click **Save Changes**.
    

### Limit the total inventory for Fees on a Collection

1.  To set a per-fee limit, navigate to **Collections** --> **Standard**.
    
2.  Then, next to the name of the desired Collection, click **Pricing** --> **Fees**.
    
3.  Next to the desired fee, click **Edit**.
    
4.  At the bottom of the page, click **Advanced Options**
    
5.  Next to the "Total Inventory" section, click the box that says "Set total inventory for this price" and insert the desired cap.
    
6.  Click **Save Changes**.
