# View Collection submissions

Once the sales start pouring in, you may wish to see a list of all of the purchases that have been made on a particular Collection.

To do so, navigate to **Collections** --> **Standard**. Then, next to the Collection deadline, click the **Submissions** button.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/KCbjkAoztgn1ULIARFkef_view-collection-submissions-archbee-document.png?format=webp "Document image")

This report will include the purchaser's information such as name, email, phone number, and street address (if entered). Additionally, it will break down each fee that was purchased along with responses to any forms that were attached to the Collection or Fee. You can easily export this data by clicking **Export to Spreadsheet** at the top of the page.
