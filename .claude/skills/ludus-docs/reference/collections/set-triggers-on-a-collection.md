# Set Triggers on a Collection

Triggers allow you to automatically turn public sales on or off. For your overall Collection, you can choose the exact date and time when the fees will go on sale to the public. Remember that sales will be turned off by the collection payment deadline. You can always sell via your admin panel, regardless of whether your Collection is on/off.

To set a Trigger for a Collection, navigate to **Collections** --> **Standard**. Then, next to the desired Collection name, click **Options** --> **Triggers**.

To turn on the Trigger, click the toggle so the bar is green and the circle is on the right-hand side. You will then have boxes where you can enter the date and time you'd like this Collection to go live for public sales.

Click **Save Trigger** to complete the setup.
