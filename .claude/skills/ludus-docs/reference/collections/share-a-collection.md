# Share a Collection

Whether for an email blast, a social media post, or marketing graphics, you may wish to include a direct link or QR code that takes patrons to your Collection(s).

### Types of links

-   **Public Share Link** - An open link that directs people to the listing. This will only function when the event is made public.

-   **Private Share Link -** A hidden link that gives patrons access to an event that is otherwise turned off publicly. This is a great way to provide early access, in addition to Access Codes.

-   **QR Code** - A Scannable image that will take patrons directly to the desired page in a web browser. Only for public links.

### Get a direct link/QR code for an individual Collection.

To do so, navigate to **Collections** --> **Standard**. Next to the desired Collection, click **Options** --> **Share**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/KGzSLv19Y8Fy-paIr36Zr_share-a-collection-archbee-document.png?format=webp "Document image")

The proceeding screen will have three ways to share access.

-   **Public Share Link**: This is a direct link that will take patrons to this Collection only. It is best for email links and social media posts.

-   **QR Code**: A scannable code that can be added to any material. Click the image to download the QR code. It is best for posters and other graphics.

-   **Private Share Link**: This is a direct link that can be used to provide early access to patrons while the Collection is publicly turned off. If patrons use this link, they can make a purchase even if the Collection is set to "Off." It is best to provide early access via a special email or social media push.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/4BtlDSgpGEIPNp6tk-3Z-_share-a-collection-archbee-document-1.png?format=webp "Document image")

It is important to share all Links as they are shown on your Share page. Adding the "www" prefix to a link in Ludus will prevent your link from working.

When you use the QR code for a collection, it is a direct link to that collection. This means that when you scan your patrons, they will be taken to this collection and will not see other collections, events, or classes unless they click your navigation buttons to view your other pages. If you prefer to use one QR code that is for your overall account (Splash Page, or Ludus Events page) an Account QR code is available to use. Instructions to get your account QR code can be found [here](https://support.ludus.com/qr-codes "here").
