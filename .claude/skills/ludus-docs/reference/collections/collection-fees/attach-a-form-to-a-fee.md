# Attach a Form to a Fee

Forms are found throughout Ludus and are a way to collect additional information from purchasers beyond name, email, phone number, and address. Check out this article to learn more about Forms and the various fields you can set up.

_Suggested Article_: [Forms](https://support.ludus.com/forms)

While you can attach a Form to a Collection overall, there might be times when you only want to ask the questions on a form if they choose a specific fee (e.g., you have a VIP price and a standard price, and you only want to ask special questions for people who pick the VIP price and not the standard price). Check out the steps below to learn how to achieve this.

Before attaching it, make sure you have built your Form. To learn how to do this, check out our article.

_Suggested Article_: [Create a Form](https://support.ludus.com/create-a-form)

### Attach to a Collection Fee

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **Collections** --> **Standard**.
    
2.  Next to the desired Collection, click **Pricing** --> **Fees**.
    
3.  Find the fee to which you'd like to attach a form and click **Edit**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/QuCjRXCIXzhAloETLYXc3_screen-shot-2023-08-30-at-83031-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Click **Advanced Options** and then scroll down to the section titled "Form"
    
5.  From the drop-down menu, select the form you'd like to attach.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fetC4qm9D4qn3Qcm_WBwc_screen-shot-2023-08-30-at-83209-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  Click **Save Changes**.
    

If any forms are attached to the Collection as a whole, patrons will also be asked to complete them at checkout.

When adding forms to a fee, be sure to check that "**Require a form entry for each fee selected"** is not enabled on the collection, as this will cause only the fee's form to appear.
