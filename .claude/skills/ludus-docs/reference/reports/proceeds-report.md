# Proceeds Report

The Proceeds Report will display a breakdown of your proceeds from Ludus. There are two variations for the proceeds report depending on whether you elected to receive paper check payouts or direct deposit. See a breakdown with additional details below!

## View the Proceeds Report

To view the proceeds reporting hub, click **Reports** (at the top navigation bar) --> **Proceeds**.

### Paper Checks versus Direct Deposits

**For paper checks**, each check sent will be listed separately. The row will show the check number and amount, and a tag will display what the check is for (Donations, event tickets, classes, etc.). You can click each row to review the complete details for the check, see a digital preview, and track the check. _Suggested Article_: [Reconcile paper check proceeds](https://support.ludus.com/reconcile-paper-check-proceeds)

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/lHuMhvUhk82BJgFMTTvib-20260610-132116.png?format=webp "Document image")

**For direct Deposits,** whether you elected daily, weekly, or monthly direct deposits, each deposit will appear as a separate item on your proceeds report. On the main reporting screen, each line will display the deposit date and amount. Then, you can check as many boxes along the left-hand side as desired and reconcile deposits individually or in bulk. _Suggested Article_: [Reconcile direct deposits](https://support.ludus.com/reconcile-direct-deposits)
