# Reporting Dashboard

Whether you are viewing an account-wide overview or a specific Event, Collection, or Class, each reporting area within Ludus has a Dashboard. The dashboard is best for a super high-level overview of sales.

At the top of the dashboard, you will see quick stats for the specified area (account-wide, events, classes, etc.) and an area graph showing sales totals over time.

## Global Reporting Dashboard

To view your account-wide reporting Dashboard, click **Reports** (at the top of your admin panel) -> **Dashboard**.

### Quick Stats found on the Global Reporting Dashboard

The four main stats on the Global Dashboard are:

-   **Revenue** - The total income after discounts, fees, etc. (Includes cash sales)

-   **Tickets** - The total number of tickets sold in the specified time.

-   **Donations** - The number of donations made in the specified time.

-   **Sold Online** - The percentage of tickets purchased online by patrons versus your Admin Panel/Box Office.

## Event Reporting Dashboard

Click **Events** from the top navigation bar to view your reporting Dashboard for a specific event. Then, next to the desired event, click **Reports** --> **Dashboard**.

### Quick Stats found on Event Reporting Dashboard

The five main stats on an Event Reporting Dashboard are:

-   **Revenue** - The total income after discounts, fees, etc. (Includes cash sales)

-   **Tickets** - The total number of tickets sold.

-   **Add-Ons** - The total number of add-ons sold.

-   **Sold Online** - The percentage of tickets purchased online by patrons versus your Admin Panel/Box Office.

-   **Most Popular Time** - The event time with the most tickets sold.

## Collection Reporting Dashboard

To view your reporting Dashboard for a specific Collection, click **Collections** --> **Standard** from the top navigation bar. Then, next to the desired Collection, click **Reports** --> **Dashboard**.

### Quick Stats found on the Collection Reporting Dashboard

The four main stats on a Collection Reporting Dashboard are:

-   **Revenue** - The total income after discounts, fees, etc. (Includes cash sales)

-   **Registrations** - The total number of base fees/items sold in the collection.

-   **Add-Ons** - The total number of add-ons sold.

-   **Sold Online** - The percentage of tickets purchased online by patrons versus your Admin Panel/Box Office.

## Classes Reporting Dashboard

To view your reporting Dashboard for a specific Class, click **Collections** --> **Classes** from the top navigation bar. Then, next to the desired class, click **Reports** --> **Dashboard**.

### Quick Stats found on the Class Reporting Dashboard

The four main stats on a Class Reporting Dashboard are:

-   **Revenue** - The total income after discounts, fees, etc. (Includes cash sales)

-   **Registrations** - The total number of registrations sold.

-   **Add-Ons** - The total number of add-ons sold.

-   **Sold Online** - The percentage of tickets purchased online by patrons versus your Admin Panel/Box Office.

-   **Most Popular Time** - The event time with the most tickets sold.
