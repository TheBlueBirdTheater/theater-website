# Ask My Data

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/L3LyG2FC11Vc6M5-SoTAc_image.png?format=webp "Document image")

**Ask My Data** answers plain English questions about your box office, patrons, fundraising, marketing, volunteers, and more. Every answer comes with the numbers, charts, insights, and exports to back it up.

You can find this under **Reports** -> **Ask My Data** in your top navigation. Currently, this feature is invite-only and will be generally rolling out to all in August 2026. In the meantime, you can join the Wait List and get early access to the private beta before August.

Check out: [**Best Sample Questions**](https://support.ludus.com/ask-my-data#best-sample-questions "Best Sample Questions") to get started with Ask My Data

## FAQ

### Can I ensure that my patrons' data is not used to train models and is secure?

Privacy and preventing models from training on patron data was one of the first musts we had to address before we decided to move forward on Ask My Data. Anthropic is running on the backend for the data processing, and our specific model and corporate agreement with Anthropic prevents training on Ludus data, so it only stays within our own system, scoped per account. Also, just like all data in Ludus, everything complies with data privacy laws and requirements we have to abide by as a company.

This ends up being safer than exporting your Ludus data and importing into your own chat-based system.

### Are there usage limits?

During the private beta, we are monitoring usage and the cost to run a feature like this. Right now, everyone gets 50 questions per day but this may increase or decrease after the private beta. We will roll out additional options for advanced usage down the road, but everyone will always get free usage and questions regardless.

### Can the Ask My Data delete or change our data?

Nope! Ask My Data is read-only, so it can only pull data and give you answers at this time.

### Is this replacing customer support?

No! This is simply for chatting with the data in your account. Ludus' top notch _human_ customer support will always be there for you in the bottom right corner of your admin panel.

### Where are the conversations stored?

In our own Ludus database, scoped to the account, just like any other customer data. We built this whole thing in-house and did not outsource the chat interface. Standard retention and deletion policies apply (GDPR/CCPA compliant).

### **What can't it do?**

-   Predictions: "Who will buy next month?" (no crystal ball)

-   Profit margins: we don't have cost of goods data

-   Modify or act on anything: can't refund, can't email, can't update records

-   Schedule reports: no "email me this every Monday" (yet!)

### Can I share results with my team?

Yes! You can copy a link to any conversation and any admin user who is given the link can access it. You can also export to PDF, download tables to CSV, and save graphs as PNGs.
