# Daily Summary Email

The Daily Summary Email is a great way to receive daily sales information directly in your email. This feature can be enabled by going to **Settings** (the gear at the top of your account) -> **Notifications**.

_Suggested Article:_ [Notifications Center](https://support.ludus.com/notifications-center)

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/k5BSu5bqrKzypaVA7KIcH-20250926-143151.png?format=webp "Document image")

### What the Summary Email Includes:

1.  **Event Specific Sales**
    
    -   **Tickets Sold** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Revenue** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Total Tickets Sold** (for that entire event)
    
    -   **Total Revenue** (for that entire event)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/7dyhDXEUpQbbERLoHVkWY-20250926-143216.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  **Collection Specific Sales**
    
    -   **Items Sold** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Revenue** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Total Items Sold** (for that entire collection)
    
    -   **Total Revenue** (for that entire collection)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Jh03RikiiVuCJls4HVCc6-20250926-143354.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  **Class Specific Sales**
    
    -   **Class Spots Sold** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Revenue** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Total Class Spots Sold** (for that entire class)
    
    -   **Total Revenue** (for that entire class)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/kI3bzRezYCH8HLKKvSacH-20250926-143758.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  **Passes**
    
    -   **Passes Sold** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Revenue** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Total Passes Sold** (for that pass category)
    
    -   **Total Revenue** (for that pass category)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Ao0_7yEOi-QaA0Y_ZLbwX-20250926-143824.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  **Donations**
    
    -   **Total Donations** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Revenue** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Total Donations** (for that donation category)
    
    -   **Total Revenue** (for that donation category)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/2jU2nSgRABruZXQpMiQEk-20250926-143948.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  **Gift Cards**
    
    -   **Total Gift Cards Sold** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Revenue** (from the day before)
    
    -   **Total Gift Cards Sold** (for that gift card category)
    
    -   **Total Revenue** (for that gift card category)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/XS_oLAeiwuHmZ5WDkilAj-20250926-144239.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
7.  **Itemized**
    
    -   **Item ID**
    
    -   **Type of Item** (Ticket, Add-On, etc...)
    
    -   **Category** (Event, Donation, Class, etc...)
    
    -   **Method** (how it was sold)
    
    -   **Revenue**
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/wAALPfa8ERplatB1UpFVf-20250926-144442.png?format=webp "Document image")
