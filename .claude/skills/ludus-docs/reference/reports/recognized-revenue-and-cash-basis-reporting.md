# Recognized Revenue and Cash Basis Reporting

# What is Recognized Revenue and Cash Basis in Reporting?

-   **Recognized revenue** is money a business records as **earned** because it has delivered the product or held the event.

-   **Cash basis reports** show money coming in and going out **only when it’s actually paid or received**. It doesn’t count anything that hasn’t been paid for yet.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/h0y3HnJ6GdPBhti3gw-gd_image.png?format=webp "Document image")

Recognized Revenue Reporting can be found under **Reports** (next to your event) -> **Revenue** OR **Reports** (next to your event) **-> Summary.**

From here, you can select **Recognized Revenue**.

## Recognized Revenue Report

This report shows when revenue is _earned_, not just when money is collected.

-   **Flex Pass Redemptions**: When a Flex Pass is used to redeem a ticket, the _assigned redemption value_ is reported under _Flex Redemptions_ for that event.

-   **Custom Redemption Values**: You’ll be able to manually set the value of each flex redemption price per event, giving you full control over how redemptions are valued. For example, if a $10 ticket is worth $8 in the flex pass, you’ll set this up during the flex pass setup.

-   **Flat $ Season Passes:** This will show the recognized value of the individual tickets within a flat $ season pass.

The original Flex Pass purchase is _not_ reported at the event level.

**So, what is the result of all of this?** You will see a more accurate view of revenue per event, even when tickets are redeemed with a pass.

## Cash Basis Report

This report reflects when _cash is actually received_, regardless of when the event occurs.

-   **Flex Pass Redemptions**: Shows **$0** for redeemed tickets (since no new cash was collected at redemption).

-   **Flex Pass Purchases**: Reported in aggregate at the time of purchase, not tied to specific events.

**So, what is the result of all of this?** You will see a clear view of actual cash flow and deposits.

## Why does this matter?

-   **Recognized Revenue** helps assess the full value of events, including redemptions.

-   **Cash Basis** supports cash flow tracking and reconciliation.

You can toggle between both views depending on your reporting needs.
