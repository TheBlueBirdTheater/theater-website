# Where to Find the Report You Need

In reporting, there are various levels of reports. These start at the Global Level and progress through Event and Event Times. When searching for the report you need, it's essential to go to the correct level of reporting.

### Global Reports

Global Reports are found under Reports (at the top of your admin panel). These reports automatically show the last 30 days on your account.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/E42K90gXZc1gk0BstdL3I_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-07t100648386.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Event/Collection/Class Reports

These show all sales for the Event/Collection/Class to which they are attached.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/FKyKzraiUDRyQ2zAIby0O_screenshot-2024-05-15-at-35048-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Event Time/Class Time Reports

This will show all sales for the specific Event Time/Class Time to which they are attached.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/rkJU_rQj8qbGKg7tEH8To_screenshot-2024-05-15-at-35111-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Before jumping into reporting, it's best to know which level of reports you will need. From there, you can view the various reports available!
