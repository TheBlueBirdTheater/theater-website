# Volunteers Report

The Global Volunteer Report provides a look into your volunteers and the shifts they have signed up for on Ludus (through the [Volunteer Management](https://support.ludus.com/volunteer-management) feature).

_This report includes:_

-   The Volunteer's **Name**

-   The Volunteer's **Email**

-   The Volunteer's **Phone Number** (if provided/required)

-   The **Opportunity** the Volunteer signed up for

-   The **Shift** the Volunteer signed up for

-   The **Role** the Volunteer signed up for

-   When that volunteer position was **Approved**

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-8O9wSrimz3pacjFmrl7c5-20241101-190442.png?format=webp "Document image")

_Additionally_, when searching the **Volunteer Report**, you can search by patron name/email/phone number, and the criteria for the search can be set:

-   Volunteer Role

-   Date Range

The Volunteer Report can be exported as a CSV anytime.
