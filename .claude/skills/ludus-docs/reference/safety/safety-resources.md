# Safety Resources

### Seat Buffering

For reserved seating events, create an automatic seat buffer between groups without having to permanently alter your seating chart. Ludus will smartly space out groups using socially distant seats that are marked as patrons buy tickets!

[Enable Seat Buffering](https://support.ludus.com/enable-seat-buffering)

### Arrival Times

Reduce crowding and control the flow of patrons with arrival time groups.

[Enable Arrival Times](https://support.ludus.com/enable-arrival-times)

[Set Up Arrival Times](https://support.ludus.com/set-up-arrival-times)

### Patron Policies

Have your patrons agree to a "COVID-19 Social Contract" along with other general policies you may have.

[Set Patron Policies](https://support.ludus.com/set-patron-policies)

[COVID-19 Templates](https://support.ludus.com/covid-19-templates)

### AnywhereSeat

Sell tickets to a streamed or prerecorded version of your event.

[AnywhereSeat](https://support.ludus.com/anywhereseat)

### Safety Checklist

A list of safety action items to check off before, during, and after your event. You are free to use, share, and edit!

[Safety Checklist](https://support.ludus.com/safety-checklist)

### Resources

Email templates and best practices for canceled or postponed events.

[Rescheduling/postponing or canceling an event](https://support.ludus.com/reschedulingpostponing-or-canceling-an-event)
