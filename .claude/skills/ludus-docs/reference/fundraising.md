# Fundraising

Welcome to the Fundraising hub. This will be a centralized location from which you can find any information you could ever need about the tool.

## Video Overview:

## What can this feature help with?

The fundraising tab in Ludus is home to Donations and can set up Fundraisers. If you are looking to raise some money for a specific item or your group in general, this is the place!

## Why use this feature?

This feature has been used by a multitude of groups, all different, and all successfully. It's a great way for patrons to show their support for your group, venue, or whatever else you might need to fundraise for.

## What makes this feature so special?

Ludus was built for the performing arts by people who care about them, and we want to make your life easier. The Fundraising feature in Ludus has so many amazing extras, including:

-   Collect Donations

-   Create Fundraisers

-   Create Specific Categories for Patron Donations

-   Set up Fundraising Levels (IE - $100 is a Gold Donor, etc.)

-   Create Custom Messages with Tax Information

-   And so much more!

## So how does this feature work?

When you enable Donations under the Donations Settings, you will be able to allow patrons not only to donate during checkout but also to visit the Donate tab on your patron site and give at any time.

## How do I enable donations/fundraising?

If you would like to accept donations from patrons on your Ludus page, you will need to turn on the donation feature.

To do so, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Settings**. Then, under "Allow Donations," click **Yes**. Be sure to scroll to the bottom and save your changes.

## FAQ's

### What are the fees?

Donations are different from ticket sales. By law, we're not allowed to pass fees onto patrons when they make a donation. If a patron makes a $10 donation, then their card will be charged a flat $10.

For your program, the donation fee is simply what we're charged for credit card processing, which is: 3.5% + $0.50 / transaction. For example, if a patron makes a $10 donation, you'll receive $9.15.

### Can patrons be asked to cover the fees for Donations?

Donations made through the Fundraising page ask patrons whether they would like to cover the processing fees.

Donations made as part of another order (such as a donation during a ticket order) do not currently offer this option.

### How do we collect donations?

Once Donations are turned _on_ for your portal, you are able to collect donations on both the patron side and the admin side.

_Suggested Article_: [Fundraising Settings](https://support.ludus.com/fundraising-settings)

Donations can be collected at checkout, or a separate transaction (by selecting the "Donate" tab).

### How do we receive our donations?

This depends on how you choose to receive ticket sales proceeds. 

If you're receiving direct deposits, then donations will simply be deposited into your bank account on the schedule you set, along with your ticket sales.

If you're receiving paper checks at the end of your events, we send you a separate donation check at the beginning of every month for the previous month's donations.

_Suggested Article_: [Check payout schedules](https://support.ludus.com/check-payout-schedules)
