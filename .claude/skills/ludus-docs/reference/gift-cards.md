# Gift Cards

Our Gift Cards feature can be enabled for any account simply by writing in chat and requesting that Gift Cards be enabled.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/z9lTSSgbTZlzmSvx8IieV-20260114-164310.png?format=webp "Document image")

Gift cards are capped at $2,000. You are not able to create one larger than that and would need to do separate gift cards.

## What can this feature help with?

The Gift Cards feature in Ludus is a great way for your patrons to purchase Gift Cards for friends or family that can then be redeemed on your Ludus account.

## Why use this feature?

If your group or patrons are looking for a way to give the gift of an event or the ability to attend one, this is a fabulous way to do so!

## What makes this feature so special?

Ludus was built for the performing arts by people who care about them, and we want to make your life easier. The Gift Cards feature in Ludus lets your patrons enjoy your group to the fullest. This feature includes the ability to:

-   Set Expiration Dates

-   Create and send Complimentary Gift Cards

-   Make the feature admin-only

-   Monitor Gift Card usage through reporting

## So how does this feature work?

Once enabled by the Customer Support team (via the chat in the lower-right corner of your account), you can start sending Gift Cards or make them available for your patrons to purchase. For patrons, this feature will be available on the patron site under the Gift Cards tab.

## How do I start selling Gift Cards?

Our Gift Cards feature **can be enabled for any account simply by reaching out to** [Support](https://support.ludus.com/support) !

Follow these three steps to issue a gift card from the admin panel:

1.  Navigate to gift cards by clicking **More → Gift Cards.**

2.  Click **New Gift Card** to start the process of creating your gift card.

3.  Select the payment type from the tabs at the top. Then, enter the information for the gift card and collect payment.'

When finished and payment is collected, the patron will receive an email with the digital gift card and related information!
