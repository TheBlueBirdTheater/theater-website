# Volunteer Sign Up - Patron Site

The Volunteer Sign Up on the Patron site will function similarly to the Admin Panel, with the Patron site serving as the backdrop. Additionally, they can sign up multiple volunteers in that process (instead of signing each volunteer up individually).

Patrons will go to the **Volunteers Tab** on your Patron site and select **Register**, next to the Opportunity they wish to register for.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-yzhliyQf5UtJom0c1NFLm-20250721-155816.png?format=webp "Document image")

From here, the patron will be able to select the roles that they and anyone else they are signing up will like to fill, as well as the number of those roles they will be filling.

Once those roles are selected, the volunteer will then enter the primary contact for this opportunity.

If the main contact is John Doe, they will fill in their own information and not that of anyone else they are signing up to volunteer with at this point.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-pNKvmm2efcMcz38OjD7g_-20250721-160119.png?format=webp "Document image")

After this information has been entered, the volunteer will be able to Assign Roles to themselves and any other volunteer they have signed up in the past. They can also choose to set up a new volunteer!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-PDMWDR5WmNjb2uXEkmLT8-20250721-160531.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once these roles have been assigned, the volunteer (patron) can proceed to the next page to review, complete any required forms, and finalize their volunteer details.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-dBhHautZvSXDdpyeY8e53-20250721-160707.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once these section is finalized, they will be brought to a confirmation page and they will also receive a confirmation email.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-l_MI34t0RnW6BSPUEGnPY-20250721-160815.png?format=webp "Document image")

If a patron manages another patron in Volunteer Management, they will receive all volunteer reminders for an opportunity, even if they themselves are not signed up for that opportunity.

Additionally, the patron's profile will show all volunteers they manage.
