# Share Links for Volunteer Opportunities

All Opportunities can be shared, or you can share just the link to a single Opportunity if desired.

### Share All Opportunities:

1.  Go to **More** (at the top of your account) -> **Volunteers.**
    
2.  On the **Opportunities** page, select **Share** at the top.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/kTx1jPrLSIfpnXb1La-7Q-20251120-154955.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Once selected, that will generate a link and QR code to share with your patrons.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/SMUqtbvjefvaQH3J6cQEu-20251120-155038.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### Share Specific Opportunities:

1.  Go to **More** (at the top of your account) -> **Volunteers.**
    
2.  Next to the Opportunity you want to share, select **More** -> and in the dropdown click **Share**.
    
3.  From here, you will be able to share that specific Opportunity.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Q2BZKOJuxCfBBLERSzNJT-20251120-155243.png?format=webp "Document image")
