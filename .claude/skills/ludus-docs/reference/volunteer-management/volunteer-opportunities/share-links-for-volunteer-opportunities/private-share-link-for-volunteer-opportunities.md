# Private Share Link for Volunteer Opportunities

Sometimes, you may want to share an opportunity with specific volunteers without making it available to everyone. Similar to the Private Share Link for events, Volunteer Opportunities also have a Private Share Link.

To access this link, your Volunteer Opportunity must be turned off. To do this, navigate to **More** (located next to the Opportunity) -> select **Turn Off**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/z7_a4sYNEDRtIyr8Vr0sB-20251002-155807.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once that is off (or if it is already off), you can go to the same **More** dropdown -> **Select Share**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/ncUrGsxWnOKaTPrqSwt7V-20251120-150740.png?format=webp "Document image")

From here, you can copy and share the _**Private Share Link**_. Only those with the link will be able to access that Opportunity.

While you can also copy the Public Share Link or QR code, these will not allow sign-ups unless you turn the Opportunity on.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/c--1rPmyERbKDWuglCd8H-20251002-160101.png?format=webp "Document image")
