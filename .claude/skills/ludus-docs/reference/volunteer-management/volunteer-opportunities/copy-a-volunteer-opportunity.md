# Copy a Volunteer Opportunity

If you have an Opportunity you wish to copy (similar to how Events can be copied), you can easily do so on the Volunteer Opportunities page.

### Copy an Opportunity

1.  Go to **More** (at the top of your account) -> **Volunteers**.
    
2.  From here, select the **Opportunities** page, and find the Opportunity you wish to copy.
    
3.  Go to **More** (next to the **Opportunity**) and select **Copy Opportunity.**
    
    ![Document image](https://app.archbee.com/api/optimize/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/p1JfTJ5m4j86fO2173DH6-20251120-155753.png "Document image")
    
4.  From here, you can confirm the information being copied or adjust it as needed. Then you can simply save, and the Opportunity will be copied and created!
