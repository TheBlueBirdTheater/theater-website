# Volunteer Reporting

Through the reporting available under the **Opportunities** tab of Volunteers, you can access specific information about the volunteers and opportunities available to them.

### Opportunity Reports

Like event-specific reports, you can also get a report specific to the individual opportunities offered. The report is located on each opportunity in the same place as an event-specific report.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/b15rTl2Z44NJY30lQl-vp-20251120-154355.png?format=webp "Document image")

**This type of report will give you information specific to this opportunity, including:**

-   Who is working the shift

-   Email

-   Phone Number

-   Shift Date and Time

-   Hours Worked

-   Role

-   Approval date

-   Registration Date

-   Additional Details

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/YREqY51dRhFelSVTu1iq--20251020-131815.png?format=webp "Document image")

This can be exported to CSV for printing.

You can set this for specific roles and a date range before generating the report.

### Global Volunteer Report

While the **Opportunities Report** is incredibly helpful when looking into specific Opportunities, there may be a need for an overall Volunteer Report. More information on this report can be found under [Volunteers Report](https://support.ludus.com/volunteers-report) .

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/ZOLriC4UuL0bkCfabDGXl-20251020-131835.png?format=webp "Document image")

Volunteer Reports will include Manual and Imported hours as well!
