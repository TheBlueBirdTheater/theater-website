# Volunteer Settings

The Settings page in Volunteer Management contains several options. These included Registration and Access, Messages, and Approval Tracking.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/BK37GQNIeLaux8pw_jDvM-20251120-153349.png?format=webp "Document image")

## Volunteer General Settings

This page allows adjusting the following:

-   **Allow Public Registrations:** Adds the Volunteer tab to your public Ludus pages and allows volunteers to sign up for shifts.

-   **Require Application & Authentication:** Volunteers must fill out the volunteer Application and sign in to their volunteer account before registering for shifts. When disabled, a new volunteer can select the shift and click to register and sign up for it all at once, rather than going through the sign-up process first.

-   **Allow Volunteers to Cancel Shifts:** Allow volunteers to cancel their shift registrations. If this setting is enabled, volunteers will be able to cancel their shift registrations.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Allow Volunteers to Cancel Shifts](https://support.ludus.com/allow-volunteers-to-cancel-shifts "Allow Volunteers to Cancel Shifts")

-   **Require Phone Number:** Requires volunteers to fill out their phone numbers to register.

-   **Require Address:** Requires volunteers to fill out their address to register.

-   **Require Date of Birth:** Requires volunteers to fill out their date of birth to register.

-   **New Volunteer Forms:** Allows you to set a form to collect additional information when a new volunteer registers.

-   **Admin Approval Access:** All Full Admins can approve volunteers by default. Emails are sent to all selected admins in this field whenever a new volunteer signs up. This allows additional admins who are not at the Full Admin level to access the approvals tab.

-   **Volunteer Signup CTA Message:** Used to create a Call to Action message on the Signup page.

For this CTA to be visible:

-   Users must not be logged in.

-   _**Allow Public Registrations**_ must be turned on.

-   _**Require Application & Authentication**_ must be turned on/

-   **Unapproved Volunteer Message:** Create a standard reply to volunteers who are not approved.

## Messages

**Volunteer Signup CTA Message:** This message will appear on the public Volunteers page in the main volunteer signup CTA.

**Unapproved Volunteer Message:** This message will appear below the shift signup form for unapproved volunteers.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/i88bphnhPFGcrlbKSpUMn-20251120-153924.png?format=webp "Document image")

## Approvals & Tracking Settings

Also located on the General settings tab, Approvals and Tracking enable the automation of various aspects of the approval process.

**Here you can set the following:**

-   **Enable Hours Tracking:** Track the hours your volunteers complete. Approved hours will appear in reports and volunteer profiles.

-   **Automatically Approve:**

-   New Volunteers

-   Volunteer Roles

-   Hours

-   **Automatic Approvals for Shift Signups:**

-   Approve all volunteers

-   Approve only pre-approved volunteers

-   Do not auto-approve (admin review required)
