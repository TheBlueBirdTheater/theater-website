# Direct Link to Passes

You may wish to share either a Private or Public Link to a pass, or you might need a QR code for that pass. If so, those can easily be accessed and used within Ludus.

## Links to Pass

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/vV5n2kfd0sggT41DcnqQD_screenshot-2024-04-16-at-113557-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Public Share Link

A Public Share Link can be used when the pass is turned on. This link will bring the patron directly to that Pass (whether it's a Flex Pass, Season Ticket, or Membership).

To get to the Public Share link, go to **More** (at the top of the screen) -> **Options** (next to the Pass) -> **Share**. From here you can copy and share the Public Pass link.

### Private Share Link

A Private Share Link can be used when the pass is turned off. This link will bring those patrons directly to the pass, while it is turned off for the general public (this includes Flex Passes, Season Tickets, and Memberships).

To get to the Public Share link, go to **More** (at the top of the screen) -> **Options** (next to the Pass) -> **Share**. From here you can copy and share the Private Share link.

It is important to share all Links as they are shown on your Share page. Adding the "www" prefix to a link in Ludus will prevent your link from working.

### QR Code for a Pass

The QR Code is a great option to share a specific pass via a poster or advertisement. This can be copied, saved, and printed as needed.

To get to the Public Share link, go to **More** (at the top of the screen) -> **Options** (next to the Pass) -> **Share**. From here you can copy and share the QR Code.
