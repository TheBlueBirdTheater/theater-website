# Share a direct link to Passes

It might be helpful to have a direct link specifically to passes if you are looking to push sales. This is easily accessible through Ludus.

## Public Share Link

To get your direct public share link, go to **More** (at the top of the admin panel) -> **Passes** -> **Options** (next to the pass) -> **Share**. From here, you will have the option to share the direct link to that pass, as well as a QR code for that pass.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/xPjcsWpG1MWbE6d9E4UUT-20260109-183031.png?format=webp "Document image")

## Private Share Link

If you are interested in sharing a private link (while the pass is still **turned off**), you can do that by going to **More** (at the top of the admin panel) -> **Passes** -> **Options** (next to the pass) -> **Share**. From here, you will have the option to share the private share link to that pass. This is a great way to share a pass before it goes public!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/CY6ecjuqP1KAyOxEgbB5_-20260109-183137.png?format=webp "Document image")

It is important to share all Links as they are shown on your Share page. Adding the "www" prefix to a link in Ludus will prevent your link from working.
