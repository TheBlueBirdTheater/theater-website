# Pass - Patron Confirmation Email

Once a pass is purchased, the patron will receive a confirmation email at the address they used to purchase the pass.

This email will include a button labeled "view my pass". Whether they have purchased a Season Ticket, Flex Pass, or Membership, this will be the same.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/AdQs5cEQzmaFgOdjN96HI-20260417-173907.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once opened, and the patron is logged in, they can either see their season tickets or access their Flex Pass or Membership information.
