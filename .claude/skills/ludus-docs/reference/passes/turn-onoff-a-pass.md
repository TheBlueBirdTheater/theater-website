# Turn On/Off a Pass

At any point during your season or event's run, you can choose to turn a pass on or off, depending on what you need.

To do this, go to **More** (at the top of your admin panel) -> **Passes** -> **Options** (next to the pass) -> **Turn On/Off** (depending on what is needed).

Once this is selected, the pass will then be reflected as on or off, depending on your choice. Regardless, you will still be able to sell an active pass on your admin panel, whether it is turned off or not.

Turning a Pass off prevents the sale of the pass online. It doesn't prevent the redemption of already sold passes.
