# Create Special Pricing for Memberships

One of the perks of membership is special pricing for event tickets, as well as reduced fees for collections and classes.

Setting up pricing for members requires just three steps:

1.  Click **Pricing** and create a **New Price** for your event (Fee for collections & classes). Proceed to define the price like any other price. The price field is the Price you want members to pay (Discounted or otherwise).
    
2.  Under Access, select Pass Holders.
    
3.  Check the box(es) next to the Memberships that this particular price should apply to, and save your price.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/qRM1lAZpSzZKiQX6Kkmkt_ludus-manual-admin-2024-04-29t113827554.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

Once completed, active membership pass holders will be presented with the membership pricing to purchase tickets.

Members would log in using the Member and Pass Holder log-in field at the top of your ticketing page. Alternatively, they can log in via the **My Account** link and view their membership to purchase tickets.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/nHpFywYe5AIQb-XQCajX-_ludus-ludus-manual-8.png?format=webp "Document image")

The membership pricing will be applied automatically if you are signed in as a member.
