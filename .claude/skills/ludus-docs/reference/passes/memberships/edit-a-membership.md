# Edit a Membership

Up until your first sale of the specific pass at hand, you can edit any details you'd like. Once sales have begun on the membership, the "Type" of the pass and its "Pricing" cannot be changed. All other settings can be edited.

To change the settings on a Membership:

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **More** --> **Passes**.
    
2.  Next to the pass you'd like to edit, click **Edit**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/APL2ejjKuFCw9QWasFDEP_screen-shot-2023-09-13-at-122603-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Change the applicable settings.
    
4.  Click **Save Changes** to save.
