# Season Ticket

## Overview

A Season Ticket is the Ludus term for any pass or subscription where the patron chooses all their tickets at the same time they purchase their pass. When you set up a Season Ticket, you can control which events, event times, and ticket prices they have access to choose from.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/EVI6x180PhD2nBIpHRyky_screen-shot-2023-09-13-at-33114-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Unlike Flex Passes or Memberships, Season Tickets do not come with a pass holder code or additional perks/benefits.

Season Ticket will only apply to tickets selected at the time of sale.
