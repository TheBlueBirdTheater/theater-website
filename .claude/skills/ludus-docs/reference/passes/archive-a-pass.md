# Archive a Pass

Once your season or event has passed and you no longer need a pass, you might want to archive the pass to simplify your view. To archive a pass:

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **More** --> **Passes**
    
2.  Next to the desired pass, click **Options** --> **Archive**.
    

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/tfZ-1V9XRZbGydho_DFQP_screen-shot-2023-08-28-at-44257-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Archived passes can always be recovered later if needed by going to **View Archived Passes** at the bottom of the Passes tab.
