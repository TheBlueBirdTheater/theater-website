# Schedule an Automated Email for Ticket Holders

One of the perks of our [Marketing](https://support.ludus.com/marketing) feature is the ability to send automated messages to ticket holders for an event centered around the time they are attending. For instance, if they have a ticket for Friday at 7:00 p.m., you can schedule an email that will send hours or days before or after they attend. This is great for post-event surveys and makes sending reminder emails a breeze. Additionally, this uses our drag-and-drop email designer, so you can ensure these emails are visually appealing to your patrons.
