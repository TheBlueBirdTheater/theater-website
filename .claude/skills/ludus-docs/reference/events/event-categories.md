# Event Categories

Whether you have two events or twenty-seven, you may wish to use event categories to help your patrons navigate through the listing of your various events. This is particularly great if you have different types of events. For instance, choir vs. band vs. orchestra, or musicals vs. plays vs. opera.

### How it Appears to Patrons

When patrons are on your ticketing portal, there will be different tabs for each event category with an active show. They can click each tab to pull a list of that specific category of events.
