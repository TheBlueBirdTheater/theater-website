# Change the Seating Chart or Capacity for a Specific Event Time

Whether you are using reserved seating or general admission, there may be times when your seating chart or capacity differs slightly for one event time compared to another. You may have a greater capacity on Thursday nights than on other nights, or something else may affect your seating chart for a specific event. Check out the drop-down menu items below to learn how to amend your seating chart or capacity for individual event times.

### Change the total availability of tickets for the general admission event

When setting up a general admission event, you will set the total number of seats you can sell per event time. To alter this on an event time basis rather than the event overall, navigate to the event time you wish to edit. Then, next to it, click **Options** --> **Edit Time**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/l-dMUOjAanWHGYbzzOVDm_screen-shot-2023-08-28-at-55907-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

In the pop-up menu, click the settings wheel icon (⚙️) and then enter the new total you have under "Total Seats". Click **Save** to finish.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/3uxf6-x2DkRN2OkiJoVr8_screen-shot-2023-08-28-at-60049-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

You will now see the new capacity for the event time listed on your main Events page.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/NidHzpHlHQFTL98K3wryO_screen-shot-2023-08-28-at-60350-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Use a different seating chart for an event time

You might have slight variations in your seating chart from event to event. To change the seating chart on an event-time basis, check out the steps below.

1.  Build a secondary seating chart. If the chart is just a slight variation of an existing chart, we recommend making a copy of an existing seating chart and then just making adjustments.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Copy a seating chart](https://support.ludus.com/copy-a-seating-chart)
    
2.  Navigate to your main **Events** tab.
    
3.  Next to the desired event time, click **Options** --> **Edit Time**.
    
4.  Click the settings wheel icon (⚙️). Then, under the section labeled "Seating Chart", select the new chart.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
