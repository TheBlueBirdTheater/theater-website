# Preview Your Event

If you would like to see your event in preview before it goes live to the public, here is how you can do so!

### Preview Your Event as a Patron

1.  Click **More**, next to your event.

2.  Select **Share**

3.  **Copy** the **Private Share Link**

4.  **Paste** the **Private Share Link** to your favorite browser

You can now use that link to view your event and walk through the purchase process, just as your patrons will.

_**Do not process an actual credit card sale**_ to test the process. Credit card fees are non-refundable.
