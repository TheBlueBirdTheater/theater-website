# Edit/delete and ad from tickets

Once you have added an ad/graphic to your tickets, you may need to make changes or delete it altogether.

1.  On the main **Events** tab, click **More** --> **Ad Manager** next to the desired event.
    
2.  To change the ad graphic, click **Change Ad** and upload the new graphic.
    
    To delete the ad completely, click **Delete Ad**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/ri1_1I0tJKQeVjO5y0fV8_screen-shot-2023-10-10-at-90117-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

Deleting and changing ads are permanent actions. To restore a previous ad, you will need to reupload the graphic.
