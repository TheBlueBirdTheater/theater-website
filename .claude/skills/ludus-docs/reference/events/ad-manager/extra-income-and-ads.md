# Extra Income and Ads

Add custom ads and graphics to tickets, which are perfect for earning additional income from sponsors and local businesses by maximizing the space on your tickets!

Are you looking for ideas on how to generate more income with ads? Here are a few:

-   **Partner with local businesses**

-   For example, a local café might offer a discount to ticket holders, and in return, they pay for the ad space on the ticket.

-   **Sponsorship on the Physical/Online Ticket**

-   **Print Ads**: Sell space on the bottom of your printed tickets for local businesses or event sponsors to place ads.

-   **QR Codes**: Include QR codes that link to sponsor websites, promotions, or special offers. You can charge sponsors for this service.

-   **Future** **Event Ads**

-   Sharing information and the QR code for upcoming events in the ad section. This can generate more interest and income in future events!
