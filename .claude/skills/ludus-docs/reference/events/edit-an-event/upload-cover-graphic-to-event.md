# Upload Cover Graphic to Event

Cover graphics allow you to spice up your ticketing page with eye-catching images. To add one to your event, check out the steps below.

1.  Navigate to your event settings (**Events** --> **More** --> **Settings**).
    
2.  Next to "Cover Graphic," click the green **Select Image File**.
    
3.  Choose the desired Image from your computer
    
    The recommended size for cover graphics is 600 x 250 pixels.
    
4.  Once you have uploaded the image, you can hover your cursor over it to crop, replace, or delete it.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/nJ5_HVO7twKu2GrWol4Jy_screen-shot-2023-08-25-at-40124-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

When your patrons now view your event, they will see the graphic at the top.
