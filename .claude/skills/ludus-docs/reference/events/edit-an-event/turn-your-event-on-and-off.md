# Turn Your Event On and Off

Once your event is set up, ticket prices are added, and event times are listed, you are ready to go live with ticket sales. As an admin, you can control when you make ticket sales available to the public by turning your event On and Off.

Regardless of whether your event is on or off or in coming soon mode (or not), you can always sell tickets via your admin panel. On and Off is a status for public sales.

### Turning the event on

When you initially create your event, it will be set to "Off" so you can make any adjustments and finish setting it up before pushing it live. You can easily view the status as an event that is "off" will have the title appear in red, and you will see a red **Off** button next to the title.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/UKJZ6AOR2EikGYwkMZNIU_screen-shot-2023-08-29-at-25025-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

To turn the event on, there are two options:

1.  Click the red **Off** button to toggle sales "On"

2.  Next to the event name, click **More** --> **Turn On**.

### Turn off the event

To remove an event from public view (i.e., turn it "Off"), go to the event listing on your admin panel and click **More** --> **Turn Off**. The event title will now appear in red, and you will see a big red "Off" button.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fqmDjnw0VrD2hbk9Dbpw0_screen-shot-2023-08-30-at-100225-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Turn the event on automatically at a specified time

You can also automate turning your event "On" to the general public. For this, you will want to utilize Triggers on your event. Check out this article to learn more. _Suggested Article_: [Set Triggers on an Event](https://support.ludus.com/set-triggers-on-an-event)
