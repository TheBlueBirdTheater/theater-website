# Recover Deleted Ticket Prices

If you've deleted a ticket price but now want to recover that price, you can, and all of the same settings the price originally had will remain intact.

To recover a ticket, go to **Pricing** \-> **Ticket Prices** next to your event -> scroll to the bottom of your ticket prices list -> and click **Recover** next to the price you'd like to bring back.
