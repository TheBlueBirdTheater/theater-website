# Name Your Own Price Tickets

A "Name your own price" or "Pay what you can" event is a great way to eliminate financial barriers and encourage participation.

For example, you can have a free event where the base price is $0, but if someone is willing to pay for their tickets, they can do so. You'd be surprised how many people will pay for a ticket to support your group!

## Set up a name-your-own-price ticket

1.  Like adding any other ticket price, go to **Pricing** -> **Ticket Prices** next to your event and click the **\+ Ticket Price** button.
    
2.  In the "Basic Info" section next to the "Price" field, check the **Allow patron to enter custom price** checkbox to convert this price into a "name your own price."
    
3.  Set the minimum that they can pay.
    
    Example: If your event is free, but you want people to have the option to pay for their ticket, set the minimum to _0\._ That way, tickets will default to $0 unless they decide to enter a higher price.
    
4.  **Save** the ticket price when you're done adding any other info or options to the ticket price.
