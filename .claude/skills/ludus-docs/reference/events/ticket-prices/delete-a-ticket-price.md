# Delete a Ticket Price

Deleting a ticket price will not affect reports or existing orders and can always be recovered. When a ticket price is deleted, it will no longer be accessible.

To delete a ticket price, go to **Pricing** -> **Ticket Prices** next to your event and click **Delete** next to the price.

Once deleted, it'll be added to a section beneath your active prices so you can see all deleted prices and recover if necessary. _Suggested Article_: [Recover Deleted Ticket Prices](https://support.ludus.com/recover-deleted-ticket-prices)
