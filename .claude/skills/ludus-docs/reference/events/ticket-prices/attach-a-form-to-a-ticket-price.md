# Attach a Form to a Ticket Price

Forms are found throughout Ludus and are a way to collect additional information from purchasers beyond name, email, phone number, and address. Check out this article to learn more about Forms and the various fields you can set up.

_Suggested Article_: [Forms](https://support.ludus.com/forms)

While you can attach a Form to every ticket sold for an Event, there might be times when you only want to ask the questions on a form if they choose a specific ticket price. (i.e., you have a VIP price and standard price, and you only want to ask specific questions for people who pick the VIP price). The steps below outline the process of attaching a Form.

Before attaching the Form, make sure you have built your Form. To learn how to do this, check out our article. _Suggested Article_: [Create a Form](https://support.ludus.com/create-a-form)

### Attach to a specific ticket price

1.  Ensure you are on the **Events** tab from the top navigation bar.
    
2.  Next to the desired Event, click **Pricing** --> **Ticket Prices**.
    
3.  Find the ticket price to which you'd like to attach a form and click **Edit**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-mfP89dNo9TSnLHaFOU3Yp-20250328-181746.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Click **Advanced Options** and then scroll down to the section titled "Form."
    
5.  From the drop-down menu, select the form you'd like to attach.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/_zqRqI5mu1_rm93w1VCH-_screen-shot-2023-08-30-at-85442-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  Click **Save Changes**.
    

If any forms are attached to the Event, patrons will be asked to complete them at checkout. Additionally, because you have connected the Form to a specific ticket price, they will be asked to fill out multiple Forms equal to the number of tickets they select.
