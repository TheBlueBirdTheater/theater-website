# Set up Early Bird Pricing

Below, we'll cover how to create an Early Bird ticket price that automatically expires at a specific time, immediately making a higher-priced ticket available.

If you already have ticket prices created for an event, you can edit those prices instead of creating new ones.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-HLiiOyZeOeY8sH9exSVcC-20250328-181951.png?format=webp "Document image")

## Set up your Early Bird ticket price

The Early Bird ticket price is the lower/discounted price available to patrons until a specific time. At that point, your higher-priced ticket will become available.

1.  Go to **Pricing** -> **Ticket Prices** next to your event -> and click the **\+ Ticket Price** button to create a new ticket price.
    
2.  Enter "Early Bird" for the name (or whatever you want to name the ticket), enter a price, and configure any other settings you'd like.
    
3.  Click **Additional Options** at the bottom of the page to open a new panel of options.
    
4.  Using the "Availability" option under "Sale Ends," select _**On a specific date/time**_ and set the date/time you'd like the Early Bird price to _turn off_ (become unavailable).
    
5.  Save the ticket price to proceed to setting up your higher-priced ticket next.
    

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/VS7SjSCjgX13ugiQ3QZ7w_image.png?format=webp "Document image")

## **Set up your higher-priced ticket**

Once your Early Bird ticket price expires, you'll need to set a higher-priced ticket that will become available after the early bird pricing window ends.

1.  Go to **Pricing** -> **Ticket Prices** next to your event -> and click the **\+ Ticket Price** button to create a new ticket price.
    
2.  Enter the name for your higher-priced ticket price (such as "General"), enter a price, and any other settings you'd like to set.
    
3.  Click **Additional Options** at the bottom of the page to open a new panel of options.
    
4.  Using the "Availability" option under "Sales Start," select _**When sales end for another price**_ and select the "Early Bird" ticket price in the dropdown.
    
    This means the higher-priced ticket price will become available once the Early Bird ticket price ends.
    
5.  Click **Save** to save the ticket price
    

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/7T0FCybLC3R1YR-RxJY-O_image.png?format=webp "Document image")

**You are all set!** Your higher-priced ticket will become available once your Early Bird price becomes unavailable.

You can also create multiple prices to turn on once Early Bird sales end (maybe an Adult and Student-priced ticket- just repeat the "Setup your higher-priced ticket" steps above).

You can also expand on the concept of Early Bird pricing and sequence ticket prices to change as the event date approaches, such as going from $10 four weeks out to $15 three weeks out to $20 two weeks out. This feature is intended to be flexible to your needs!

Regardless of whether a price is available, all prices will always be selectable via your admin panel when selling tickets using the **New Order** button or Box Office View. As an admin, you always have access to all your ticket prices unless you delete them.
