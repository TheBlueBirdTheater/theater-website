# Exchange an order

Whether you permit exchanges is at your organization's discretion. Within Ludus, you can exchange tickets for a different seat at the same event time, for a different event time within the same event, or for a completely different event. Check out the steps below to learn how to make an exchange.

1.  To look up the order, navigate to the **Search Bar/Magnifying Glass** and enter the patron's name.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Q2wyg5HaGejiPk35cpycY_screen-shot-2024-01-22-at-41208-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    Next, **select** the correct order to open and access the exchange function.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/1tnsdA5RXkTVZqk6e7cMl_screen-shot-2024-01-22-at-41432-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Look Up a Past Order](https://support.ludus.com/look-up-a-past-order)
    
2.  Once the order is selected and opened, scroll down to the section titled "**Tickets**" and select the ticket(s) you wish to refund.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/sHGlGqMKwTKgr_RL0LQGP_exchange-an-order-archbee-document.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click **Exchange**.
    
4.  Choose the new event/event time from the list of options.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/DGEDhJTPVetGRrMNLQcTs_exchange-an-order-archbee-document-1.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  Choose the new seats from the seating chart. (This step will disappear for general admission events)
    
6.  Use the dropdown menu at the bottom to verify the ticket type. For example, if you originally had an adult ticket and are exchanging it for a senior ticket, you can change using the dropdown menu.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/dbCS0IKrcmDJnBOLZfhRR_exchange-an-order-archbee-document-2.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
7.  Verify the exchange details to ensure everything looks correct, then click **Confirm Exchange** to finalize.
    
    _**Optional:**_ Insert order details as to why the exchange was made
    
    If you went up or down in price value during step 6, you might have a refund due or need to collect payment. For cash payments, it will show the amount owed/due in cash. For card orders, the refund will be made to the card used at checkout. For card orders that require additional payment, card information must be captured again.
