# What happens if I request to refund a patron's fees?

While purchases can be refunded from your Ludus account (see [Refund or Release an Order](https://support.ludus.com/refund-or-release-an-order) ), fees are not automatically refunded to the patron.

When patrons purchase tickets, they agree to our refund policy before completing their order: _"__**Refunds and exchanges are at the discretion of (your group's name). All Ludus application/convenience fees are non-refundable.**__"_

If you wish to refund those fees, **your organization will be absorbing those fees** (covering them) instead.

To refund those fees, you can reach out to [Live Chat Support](https://support.ludus.com/live-chat-support) and request that. Again, if that is requested, those will be absorbed by your organization and you will be covering the fees.
