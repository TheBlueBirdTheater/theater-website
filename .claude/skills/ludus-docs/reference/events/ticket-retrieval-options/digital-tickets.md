# Digital Tickets

There are three main ways that patrons can manage their tickets. To stay digital and save time and paper, you and your patrons can use the "Digital" ticket option. Digital tickets can be sent via email to the address on file for the order or via text to a cell phone.

Of the three ticket printing options (Print, Digital, Will Call), you can choose which options are available to your patrons. You and your box office admins will have access to all three options.

_Suggested Article_: [Limit Ticket Retrieval Options for Patrons](https://support.ludus.com/limit-ticket-retrieval-options-for-patrons)

### Emailed Digital Tickets

When a patron purchases tickets, they must enter their name and email before completing the purchase. This ensures that we can send them order confirmations and that you can keep track of your patrons.

After they have completed their purchase, if you have the digital ticket option available, patrons can click **Digital** to have their tickets sent to their email. The tickets will arrive via email with scannable codes. Patrons can select **View Tickets** to access those tickets.

### Texted Digital Tickets

If you or your patrons enter a mobile phone number on the order, they can also have a link to their digital tickets texted to them. They can click the link in their text messages and view their digital tickets. They can easily add their tickets to their Apple Wallet if desired.

### Apple Wallet Tickets

Patrons using an iOS device can click a handy link on their digital tickets to add them directly to their Apple Wallet. This will make it incredibly simple to pull up their tickets at your event.

### Google Wallet Tickets

Patrons using an Android device can click a handy link on their digital tickets to add them directly to their Google Wallet. This will make it incredibly simple to pull up their tickets at your event.
