# Will Call Tickets

Will Call is a retrieval option for your patrons that allows them to pick up their tickets at the door. Instead of them printing tickets themselves or having them emailed or texted, Will Call means your organization will manage their tickets for them. Whether you cross them off a list or print each order individually to hand out, Will Call removes a step for your patrons.

Regardless of whether you use Will Call, you will always have a complete list of patrons attending your event in our check-in app and patron lists.
