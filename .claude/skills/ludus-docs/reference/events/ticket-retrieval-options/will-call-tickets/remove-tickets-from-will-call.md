# Remove Tickets From Will Call

Sometimes you will need to remove a patron from Will Call. Whether you are changing the delivery method or a patron accidentally selected Will Call and wants to receive their ticket another way, removing them from the list is simple.

1.  Look up the order. _Suggested Article_: [Look Up a Past Order](https://support.ludus.com/look-up-a-past-order)
    
2.  Click **Retrieve** from the top toolbar.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-y7cjGBzdEthcWoytJ4l_Q-20250508-142952.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click **Remove** in the "Will Call" section.
