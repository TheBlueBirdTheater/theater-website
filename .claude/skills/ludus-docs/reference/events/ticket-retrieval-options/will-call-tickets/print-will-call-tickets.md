# Print Will Call Tickets

Printing Will Call tickets is at your organization's discretion. If you wish to print all of the Will Call tickets instead of just checking patrons off the list, follow the steps below:

1.  Next to the desired event time, click the yellow box that displays the number of pick-ups. (This same list can also be viewed by clicking **Options** next to the event time and selecting **Pickups**.)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/nWTy9neoQKy6_C0oghFHw_screen-shot-2023-08-28-at-11114-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Select the ticket(s) you'd like to print by clicking the checkbox. To easily select all, click the green **Select All** from the top toolbar.
    
3.  On the right side of the toolbar, use the dropdown menu to select **Print selected tickets**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/CuUg0hBjabkTHMDgjBWKU_screen-shot-2023-08-28-at-12356-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Click **Submit**.
    
    If you have enabled thermal printing, those tickets will be printed to your thermal printer. Printing off of standard 8 1/2 X 11 paper will take you through your computer's standard printing prompt.
