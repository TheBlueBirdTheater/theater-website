# Additional Devices & App

Since Ludus is a web-based platform, the only thing needed to manage your Ludus account is a device with an internet connection such as a laptop or computer. However, there are additional apps available for both iOS and Android devices that can help speed up things like ticket scanning or enable additional equipment such as our [Mobile Readers](https://support.ludus.com/mobile-readers).
