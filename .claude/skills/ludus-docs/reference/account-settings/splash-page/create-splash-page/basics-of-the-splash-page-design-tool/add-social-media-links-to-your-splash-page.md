# Add Social Media links to your Splash Page

Adding your social links is easy in the splash page editor.

### Add Social Links to your page:

1.  In the splash page editor, under **Content,** select the **Social** content option and drag and drop it where you would like to add the content.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-BSvJ30hPC6nTZpigJv_cU-20241030-163602.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Click on the Field you just added to open the sidebar.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-5Eg6IsrCaGUA4EH0O1GR6-20241030-163903.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  On the right side of the screen, click on the icons you would like to add. As you do this, you will see new fields appear which allow you to add your URL info for that icon.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-607NU0dWnN5j-zUugBnG4-20241030-164147.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Once you have added the URLs to your selected items, you can also choose what type of Icon you would like to show. As well as click and drag the icons into the order you would like to display them in. You can additionally adjust the sizing of the icons themselves in the side panel.
    
5.  Click to **save** when you are done.
    

Your new social media links will now appear on the splash page!
