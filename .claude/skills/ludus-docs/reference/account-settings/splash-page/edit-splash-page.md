# Edit Splash Page

Splash Pages can be edited at any point in time. To edit, click the **Settings Wheel Icon** (⚙️) and choose the **Splash Page** tab. Then click **Edit Splash Page** and make any desired changes.

Be sure to click **Save** in the upper right-hand corner once you are done editing!

For a refresher on what design options are available, check out this article.

_Suggested Article_: [Basics of the Splash Page design tool](https://support.ludus.com/basics-of-the-splash-page-design-tool)

### Troubleshooting

If you try to edit your Splash Page and nothing happens when you click **Edit Splash Page**, click on **Version History** and recover the latest version of your Splash Page. That will restore the ability to edit your Splash Page.
