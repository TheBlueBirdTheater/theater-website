# Turn Splash Page on or off

### Turn Splash Page On

Once the Splash page has been completed, you will want to turn it on. To do so, click the **Settings Wheel Icon** (⚙️) and choose the **Splash Page** tab. Then click **Turn Splash Page On**.

### Turn Splash Page Off

If you would like to disable the Splash Page, click the **Settings Wheel Icon** (⚙️) and choose the **Splash Page** tab. Then click **Turn Splash Page Off**.
