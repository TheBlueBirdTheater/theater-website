# Account fees breakdown

With Ludus, there is never any confusion or surprises when it comes to the ticketing fees on your account. We pride ourselves on being 100% transparent and never having monthly fees, hidden costs, or surprise charges. The only time a fee is charged is when a patron pays with a card.

_Suggested Articles_: [How much does Ludus cost?](https://support.ludus.com/how-much-does-ludus-cost) and [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table)

If you have added any premium features to your account and want to view the fee breakdown on your account:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Navigate to the **Fees** tab.
    
3.  Under the **Pricing Breakdown** section, you can view the base fee and any premium features that you have added to your account.
    
    Premium features do not affect the pricing for donations, micro fees, or macro fees. Those will always stay consistent. _Suggested Article_: [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table)
