# What are the premium features within Ludus?

### There are three premium features within Ludus:

-   [Marketing](https://support.ludus.com/marketing) - Build and send custom emails to your patrons that promote your organization and boost ticket sales

-   [Classes](https://support.ludus.com/classes) - Manage registrations for classes and workshops while maintaining all relevant information about students on their profiles.

-   [Embed Widget](https://support.ludus.com/embed-widget) - Plug the Ludus ticket-buying process directly into your own website, allowing patrons to select and purchase without ever leaving your site.

There are no monthly fees or subscriptions to utilize any of the above features. If you choose to enable one of the features, 5 cents will be added to the base Ludus fee (5% + .75).
