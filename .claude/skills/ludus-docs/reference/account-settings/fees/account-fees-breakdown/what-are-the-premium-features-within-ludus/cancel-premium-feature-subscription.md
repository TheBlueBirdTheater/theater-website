# Cancel premium feature subscription

There are three premium features within Ludus. There are no monthly subscriptions or minimum requirements when it comes to these features. They simply add 5 cents to your Ludus ticketing fee.

_Suggested Article_: [Fees](https://support.ludus.com/fees)

You can enable/disable these features at any time from your admin panel. No tricky cancellation screens attached.

### To cancel a premium feature subscription:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Click the tab titled **Fees**.
    
3.  Scroll to the section titled **Manage Premium Features**.
    
4.  If you are utilizing a feature, you will see a green **On** light underneath the name. Then, there will be a **Cancel Subscription** button on the right side. If you are not utilizing a feature yet, you will have a button that says **Learn More**.
    
    To cancel an active subscription, click **Cancel Subscription**.
    
    Cancellation takes effect immediately, and your fee will automatically be adjusted, and the functionality of the premium feature will be disabled.
    
    _**Any reporting for previous items sold in a premium feature like Classes will remain on your account for accurate reporting.**_
