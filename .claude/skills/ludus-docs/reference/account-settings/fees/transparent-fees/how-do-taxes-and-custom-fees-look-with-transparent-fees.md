# How do Taxes and Custom Fees look with Transparent Fees?

While the service fees show up with the price of your item when selecting that item (ticket, collection, etc), Taxes and Custom Fees appear a bit differently.

### How Taxes will show on an order:

When your patron goes through the ticket-buying process, they will see the taxes applied before paying for their items. Those will show up as a yellow banner below the "Proceed to Payment".

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-kHVrgxQ6wju_FxTp_3PNY-20250514-152751.png?format=webp "Document image")

See [Taxes](https://support.ludus.com/taxes) for more information on how taxes work in Ludus.

### How Custom Fees will show on an order:

The Custom Fees will show in 2 locations during the patron checkout process.

1.  The first location is on the page where they select seats/items, and before they are added to the cart.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-oQJatWefySfRUSyztUkAS-20250514-153127.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  The following location is where those are added to the cart. Those fees will show in a pop-up next to the price (labeled with whatever they were originally added as).
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-xS-KXsJkA8XG8eBWgDIFm-20250514-153305.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

Please see [Custom Fees](https://support.ludus.com/custom-fees) for more information on how these work and how to add them to your account.
