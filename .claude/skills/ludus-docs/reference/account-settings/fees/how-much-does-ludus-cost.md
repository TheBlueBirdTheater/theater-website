# How much does Ludus cost?

There's no need to budget out Ludus. When we say everything is free to your program, we mean it. A $10 ticket is $10 to your program.

## So, how does Ludus make money?

We pass a **5% + $0.75/ticket convenience fee** on to your patron when they pay using a credit card. Cash, comp, and $0 tickets have no fees. (If you'd like to absorb the convenience fee instead of passing it on to your patrons, this is possible.)

For tickets $3 or below, we implement a flat Micro Fee of $0.50/ticket that overrides the default ticket fees. This means fees will be significantly lower for lower-priced tickets.

There are no additional charges such as setup costs, annual fees, or hidden charges.

## Is Ludus actually getting the full 5% + $0.75 per ticket?

Nope! We actually use this to cover the credit card processing fees we're charged. Whatever is left over is what we make as a company (so we can keep the lights on).

Feel free to take a look at our [**pricing**](https://ludus.com/product_pricing.php "pricing") page, view our [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table), or play around with our handy [**fee calculator**](https://ludus.com/product_fee_calculator_widget.php "fee calculator").

### Examples

1.  _**A purchase without premium features, custom fees, or taxes:**_
    
    If a patron buys a $10 ticket using a credit card, they'll pay $11.25. We collect the $1.25, cover credit card processing fees, and you're left with the full $10. There are no additional fees you have to pay.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/98faWM0QRvGLKHMNoY_ZR-20260313-194945.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  _**A purchase with 2 premium features enabled (+$.10 added to the $.75 portion fee):**_
    
    If a patron buys a $10 ticket using a credit card, they'll pay $11.35 (instead of $11.25, as $.10 is added for the premium features enabled). We collect the $1.35, cover credit card processing fees, and you're left with the full $10. There are no additional fees you have to pay.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/S1e5_1t6CMO1Ps9aoj_Qo-20260313-194740.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  _**A purchase with a custom fee ($1 per order) and 2 premium features (+$.10 added to the $.75 portion of the fee):**_
    
    If a patron buys a $10 ticket using a credit card, they'll pay $12.40 ($1 of which is the custom fee). We collect the $1.40, cover credit card processing fees, and you're left with the full $11 (the $10 ticket plus the $1 custom fee added by your group). There are no additional fees you have to pay.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/on-Pw14yz8nO6DhHTMBFv-20260313-200123.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  _**A purchase with a 6% sales tax and no premium features or custom fees:**_
    
    If a patron buys a $10 ticket using a credit card, they'll pay $11.88. We collect the $1.28, cover credit card processing fees, and you're left with the $10.60 (the $10 ticket plus the tax added, which equals $.60 for this ticket). There are no additional fees you have to pay.
    
    Reminder - Ludus does not provide tax advice; please consult a tax professional.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/wwfmkfN67BYrPdI0uurQ2-20260313-200109.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

**Micro Fees:** If a patron buys a $3 ticket using a credit card, they'll pay $3.50. We collect the $0.50, cover credit card processing fees, and you're left with the full $3. There are no additional fees you have to pay.
