# Add a Custom Fee

Once you've enabled Custom Fees on your account, you're ready to add your fee(s).

### To add Custom Fees:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Navigate the the **Fees** tab.
    
3.  Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **+Custom Fee**.
    
4.  Choose whether you'd like to add an **Order Fee** or a **Ticket Fee**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Ktrg7_OJA6u6fhlQuQjI-_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-102242-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  \-For Order Fees, give your fee a name, choose whether it is a flat fee or a percentage fee, and insert the amount
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/MKxEbfzC6NuxwjktVqL1I_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-102557-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    \-For Ticket Fees, Give your fee a name, choose whether it is a flat fee or a percentage fee, insert the amount, and click which event(s) and subsequent ticket prices the fee applies to.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/MDOXoWHprgzHQNqjvIML__screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-103147-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  Click **Add Custom Fee**.
