# Edit/Delete Custom Fee

Once you have a Custom Fee added to your account, you might have the need to edit the settings (like which tickets it applies to), or delete the fee altogether.

### To edit/delete a Custom Fee:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your screen.
    
2.  Navigate to the **Fees** tab.
    
3.  Scroll down to the "Custom Fees" section.
    
4.  Next to the desired fee, click **Edit** to change settings, **Delete** to remove the fee, and **Recover** to reactive a previously deleted fee.
