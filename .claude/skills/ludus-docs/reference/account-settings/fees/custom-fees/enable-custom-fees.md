# Enable Custom Fees

If you'd like to utilize custom fees on your account, you will first need to enable the feature.

### To enable custom fees:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Navigate the the **Fees** tab.
    
3.  Scroll down to the **Custom Fees** section and click **Enable Custom Fees**.
