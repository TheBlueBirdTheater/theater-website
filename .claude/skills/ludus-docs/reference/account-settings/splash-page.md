# Splash Page

Splash Pages allow you to customize the first page patrons see when they arrive at your Ludus ticketing page (_yourorganization_.ludus.com). The Splash Page designer utilizes drag-and-drop design tools that allow you to build a page that showcases your organization loud and clear. Whether it is sleek and simple or bold and intricate, the tools are in your hands.
