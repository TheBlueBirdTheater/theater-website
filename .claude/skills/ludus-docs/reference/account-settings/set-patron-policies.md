# Set Patron Policies

When a patron makes a purchase on your page, you can have a list of policies that patrons must agree to before paying for their order.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/o1JpOeYwJpRGDTeDdIYJt_screen-shot-2023-09-03-at-81243-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Customize/enable policies

To enable and set policies for your patrons:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner.
    
2.  Click the **Policies** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/R_0XxioJjij88-aUsYuzX_holland-community-theatre-admin-1.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  There are three sections within policies: **COVID-19 Social Contract**, **Refund and Exchange Policy**, and **General Policies**.
    
    There is sample text preloaded for you within the boxes. Click the text box and edit as necessary to fit your organization.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/mRksSE4KppNg2VcPf1jxE_screen-shot-2023-09-03-at-82339-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  To enable the policy on the patron purchase page, click the slider to move the circle to the right side and turn the bar green.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/DZRBqOzmKa0HvxTSJoaW8_screen-shot-2023-09-03-at-82249-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
