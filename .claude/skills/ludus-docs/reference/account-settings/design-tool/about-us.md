# About Us

When you use Ludus and your address, phone number, and social media details are listed under **Settings** (gear at the top right) -> **General Settings**, that information will appear in the "About Us" card.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-Qlv3P_Z8OKBfw_bFqTKNn-20250626-201354.png?format=webp "Document image")

From **Settings** (the gear at the top) -> **Design Tool** ->**About Us**, you can update whether you want this card to appear on your site and decide whether to change the wording of the "About" portion at the top, as well as the description of your organization.
