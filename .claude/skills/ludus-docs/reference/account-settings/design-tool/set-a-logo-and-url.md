# Set a Logo and URL

The branding tool within Ludus enables you to prominently display your brand's logo throughout the ticket-buying experience, as well as integrate a home site URL to the patron page if needed. Whether on the top banner of your main ticketing page or at the top of your confirmations, you can ensure that your branding is visible.

### Upload a Logo and URL

To upload your account logo and URL:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** in the upper right-hand corner of your screen.
    
2.  Click the **Design Tool** tab and navigate to the **Header** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/XA3sTGILzyQ0DXLTa-DrQ_screenshot-2026-08-21-at-111543-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  To add the **Header Logo,** click **"Choose File"** and select the image from your computer. Additionally, to add a **Logo URL**, paste that link into the **Logo URL** location.
    
    The **Logo URL** is optional for use with the Header Logo. It's there as an option if needed!
    
4.  Click **Save Design** to update.
    

Some logos may look better on a wider banner. To change the width of your header, click the drop-down menu next to **Header Size**. Click **Save Design** to preview the new width.

### Where does this logo appear?

**Header**: The logo you set within the design tool will appear in the header of your Ludus ticketing portal. This header remains in place throughout all pages while your patron navigates through the process.

Additionally, if patrons click on the logo, they will be able to access the URL if it has been added to the header logo.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/WMw3HZzO6W3FpQK8q2D2P_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-41906-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

**Emails**: Additionally, this logo will appear on transaction emails set from your Ludus account. Whether it's a confirmation email, an email about abandoned carts, or donation notifications, they will all feature your logo along with the color you've set for your header.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/9N7eoUulr2q5oRU4xn7kI_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-42412-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

**Digital Tickets**: When a patron chooses the digital ticket retrieval option, they will receive an email that contains their scannable tickets. Additionally, if they click the link to view the tickets in a browser, your logo will be displayed at the top of the page.
