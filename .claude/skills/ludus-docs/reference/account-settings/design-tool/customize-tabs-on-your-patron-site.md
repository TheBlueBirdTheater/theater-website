# Customize Tabs on your Patron Site

Each page of your Ludus site will have a designated tab at the top of the screen (e.g., Tickets, Donations, Passes, etc.).

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-FCUGQblNnI8tS6m9WV2kA-20250520-153638.png?format=webp "Document image")

To customize the text for each tab OR to hide that tab from public view, click the **Settings Wheel Icon** (⚙️) --> **Design Tool**. Then, navigate to the tab titled "Tabs". From there, type in the desired name for each section and click **Save Design**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/M4ckMetzgZgM3jTDeEtBE_screenshot-2026-08-21-at-111314-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

When the tab is either customized to your preferred wording or hidden, those changes will be reflected on your patron site.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-DO-gHv8UKIk9VQJgea76s-20250521-164104.png?format=webp "Document image")
