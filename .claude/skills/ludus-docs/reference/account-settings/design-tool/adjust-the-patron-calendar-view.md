# Adjust the Patron Calendar View

This section of the design Tool can be used to adjust the Calendar view on the Patron site.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/d9gJLnVj18aX2SpnRFDpg_screenshot-2026-08-21-at-111858-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/woN2W8QqJT0tUOh0W6mTV_screenshot-2026-08-21-at-111914-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

For the calendar view, you can adjust:

-   What day the week starts on (Sunday or Monday)

-   The _Default view_ (Calendar or List)

You can also access the Embed code if the [Embed Widget](https://support.ludus.com/embed-widget) is enabled for your Ludus account.

Once saved, you will be able to view your updates on your updates on your patron page.
