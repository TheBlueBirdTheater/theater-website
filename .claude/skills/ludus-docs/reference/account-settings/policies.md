# Policies

# Policies

Policies in Ludus are a great way to let patrons know and give them the opportunity to agree to your venue's policies (whether those are group, venue, health, or whatever else you might need).

# Enable/edit Policies

There are 3 types of Policies available in Ludus - **General Policies**, **Refund and Exchange Policy**, and **COVID-19 Social Contract.** Once toggled to "on" these policies will show up for patrons and they will be required to acknowledge them prior to check-out.

In order to edit these policies you will want to go to **Settings** (⚙️ at the top of your admin panel) -> Policies. From here you can edit the default language in the **Refund and Exchange Policy** and **COVID-19 Social Contract** or add your own information and guidelines to any of them.
