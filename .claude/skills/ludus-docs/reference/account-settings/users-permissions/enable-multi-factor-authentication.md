# Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (also known as MFA or 2FA) is an additional step you can add to the login process for your Ludus account. When this feature is enabled, Admin users will be required to verify their login via phone number.

This is a great way to add an extra step of security for your account and the login process.

### Enable/Disable Multi-Factor Authentication

1.  From the top navigation bar, click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) and navigate to the **Users** tab.
    
2.  At the bottom of the page, you'll see a section for "Multi-Factor Authentication". You can view if it is currently enabled or disabled, and click **Edit** to alter.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-ZlnnWSQneNwY3yOd4-UQV-20241021-200107.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-YyxwncxXMN5q1__UrwVvI-20241021-200235.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Choose whether you'd like it Disabled or Enabled and click **Done**.
    
    Once enabled, you may have to log back in with the MFA.
    

### Log in with Multi-Factor Authentication

Once enabled on your account, when users go to log in, they will be given the following screen:

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/tBjUhEJSeLIJCIBGTKzcf_screenshot-2024-04-26-at-113809-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

At that point, they can enter their number and they will have the ability to enter the code sent to their phone.
