# General Settings

The **General** Settings tab is the home for all of the basic information, such as your program name and contact information. Additionally, you can enable/disable features from this page. Check out the listing below to learn what each setting means.

### Options and Terms:

-   **Program Name** - The name of your organization/program. This will appear on tickets and throughout the Ludus ticket-buying experience.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Change your Program Name](https://support.ludus.com/change-your-program-name)

-   **Subdomain** - Where your patrons can access your Ludus page. (e.g., **manual**.ludus.com) \*Please contact support via live chat or support@ludus.com to change your subdomain.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Change your account subdomain](https://support.ludus.com/change-your-account-subdomain)

-   **Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, and TikTok** - Link to your social media pages on your events screen.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Create links to your social media accounts](https://support.ludus.com/create-links-to-your-social-media-accounts)

-   **Contact Email** - An email for patrons to contact you at. This will also be the default reply email for any manual messages sent.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Event Messaging](https://support.ludus.com/event-messaging)

-   **Phone Number** - A phone number at which patrons can contact you.

-   **Address** - Address for your organization.

-   **Default Country** - The country in which your organization exists.

-   **Default State -** The state location of your organization.

-   **Default City** \- The city location of your organization.

-   **Timezone** - Timezone for your organization. You will want to use your local time and ensure proper use of Triggers and availability.

When choosing a subdomain, it is recommended to use the name of your group (or an abbreviation of your group's name). This is preferable over the event name, as that might change throughout your time using Ludus.

### **Features:**

For each feature listed below, click **Yes** to enable and **No** to disable.

-   **Display tooltips** - Choose to show or hide tooltips when you hover over items in your admin panel.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Enable/disable Tooltips](https://support.ludus.com/enabledisable-tooltips)

-   **Alumni Info** - Ask patrons if they are alumni when checking out.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Collect alumni information](https://support.ludus.com/collect-alumni-information)

-   **Cash Tender Calculator** - Display a cash tender calculator when accepting cash payments through your admin panel.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Disable/enable cash tender calculator](https://support.ludus.com/disableenable-cash-tender-calculator)

-   **Accessibility Notes at Checkout** - Users and admins can add accessibility notes to the patron's profile while checking out an order.

-   **Invoices** - Enable/disable the Invoicing feature.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Invoicing](9W67EbgHL7ATKvxuMf7LI)

-   **Partial payment Fee -** Pass the Ludus Convenience fee on or absorb them for partial payment of Invoices. $1.00 per partial payment.

-   **Require Phone Number** - Require patrons to fill out their phone number to purchase tickets and passes, register for classes, and make donations. Name and Email are required by default.

-   **Require Address** - Requires Patrons to fill out their address to purchase tickets and passes, register for classes, and make donations. Name and Email are required by default.

-   **Seats Display** - Choose between Seats Left or Seats Sold to view real-time availability for your shows on the Admin panel.
