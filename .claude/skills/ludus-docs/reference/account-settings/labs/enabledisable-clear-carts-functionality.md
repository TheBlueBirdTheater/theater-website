# Enable/disable Clear Carts functionality

Everything in Ludus updates in real-time. This ensures that two people can not add the same tickets to their cart and have double-sold seats. Once a patron has added tickets to their cart, they will be held to give them adequate time to complete checkout. After a bit of time, if they do not complete their purchase, they will get an abandoned cart email, and their tickets will be released back into the available pool for other patrons.

As an admin, you can clear tickets from a patron's cart. Let's say it is minutes before a show, and a patron has tickets sitting idly in their cart. You can clear that cart to ensure those tickets are available for another patron to purchase.

### Enable Patron Cart Clearing

To enable the ability to clear a patron's cart:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Click the tab titled **Labs**.
    
3.  For the section titled **Clear Carts**, click **On**.
    
4.  Click **Save** at the bottom of the page.
    

### Clearing a Cart

Once the ability to clear carts has been enabled in your settings, you can easily do so from your admin panel.

1.  On the main **Events** tab, navigate to the event time for which you'd like to clear the cart. (i.e., If you are trying to clear the cart of a patron buying tickets to the September 22nd show, go to that event time)
    
2.  Click **New Order** next to that event time.
    
3.  At the top of the ticket selection page, there will be a blue box that shows the number of active carts for that event time. To view, click the **blue box**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/9vfjjSO5-qMu-J_DpoBeH_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-124107-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  To clear the cart, click **Clear** next to the desired cart.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/4o5lD7Ov3U8-tFE44appk_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-124348-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    Clearing a patron's cart is a permanent action. If you do so, they will have to start over to complete their purchase. The tickets they originally selected might not be available by the time they go back to purchase, as they will become available to the public as soon as the cart is cleared.
