# Prevent orphan/gap seats

We understand that, with certain events/event times, you need to sell every possible seat. Fortunately, we have a way to ensure that each party selects seats that do not leave gaps!

Orphan/gap seats are individual seats that are left between parties or at the end of a row. Without this feature, a patron could choose to place a singular buffer seat between their party and another existing reservation. If you'd like to prevent this and maximize your seating, you will want to enable the **Check for orphan/gap seats** feature.

### Example of Gap Seat

Without the feature enabled, a patron might choose a set of three seats as seen below.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/vlmIFZMeDIj8osez20Ns5_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-115725-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

This leaves two single seats in a row instead of accommodating a group of three and a group of two that would like to sit next to each other.

When the feature is turned on, the patron will see a message like this when they try to add tickets to their carts that leave a single seat between parties or at the end of an aisle.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/op_Sfkx3A3KRVEwHz22Ub_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-115814-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

Before proceeding to checkout, they will have to adjust their seating selection to remove single-seat gaps.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/9fVUZxHv7NKTFE63LKt7O_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-115844-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Prevent Orphan/Gap Seats

To enable the check for orphan/gap seats:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Click the tab titled **Labs**.
    
3.  For the section titled **Check for orphan/gap seats**, click **On**.
    
4.  Click **Save** at the bottom of the page.
