# Create links to your social media accounts

On your main events page, there will be a box that displays general information about your program/organization. If you'd like, you can also set a direct link to your Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, and/or TikTok Pages.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/ISxL00DE9rSt1Ygm5otnr_screenshot-2026-08-28-at-103147-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

**To do so:**

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Under the **General** tab, type in the rest of your URL for your Facebook or Twitter page under the appropriate sections.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/W-DpWtN6bpBqrrAJVxw5V_screenshot-2026-08-28-at-102441-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the page.
