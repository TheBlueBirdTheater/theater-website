# Enable/disable Tooltips

Tooltips are a handy way to learn what specific tabs and buttons mean within Ludus. When you hover over an area, there will be a little description in the bottom left-hand corner that will give you a bit more detail about the button/area. (e.g., If you hover your cursor over the **Users** tab, you will see this message:

_"Add and manage who can access your admin panel."_

This feature will be on to start. To disable/enable this in the future:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Under the **General** tab, scroll down to **Display tooltips**, and click **Yes** or **No** accordingly.
    
3.  Click **Save Settings**.
