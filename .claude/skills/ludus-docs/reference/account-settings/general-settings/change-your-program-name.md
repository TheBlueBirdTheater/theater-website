# Change your Program Name

Your program name is used as the main display name on tickets and throughout the ticket-buying experience. This will also be the name that is plugged into confirmation email subject lines, such as "Order Confirmation for **Ludus Manual**".

You will choose your program name when you first set up your account. To change the name:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  From the **General** tab, insert the desired text under **Program Name**.
    
3.  Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the page.
