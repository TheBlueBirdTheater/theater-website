# Disable/enable Invoicing

Invoicing allows you to create, customize, and send itemized invoices to patrons for items within Ludus, such as tickets and class registrations.

_Suggested Article_: [Invoicing](9W67EbgHL7ATKvxuMf7LI)

**You can enable/disable this feature at any time from your admin panel. To do so:**

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  From the **General** tab, scroll to the section titled **Invoices** and click **Yes** or **No** accordingly.
    
3.  Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the page.
