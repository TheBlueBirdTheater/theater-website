# Collect accessibility notes during checkout

Accessibility needs are never one-size-fits-all. Within Ludus, you can enable a box where patrons and admins can manually enter the needs of a specific patron. These notes will be saved to the patron's profile and can be edited on future orders.

### Turn off Accessibility Notes

To start, you will want to enable this feature. To do so:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  From the **General** tab, scroll to the section titled **Accessibility Notes on Checkout** and click **Yes** to enable.
    
3.  Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the page.
    

## Input/Edit Accessibility Notes for a Patron

Once the feature is enabled, both admins and patrons themselves will be able to type accessibility notes on an order.

### How patrons can insert notes:

There will be a box where they can type in any notes they may have.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fD1k8Qp5W2_SpuI9M0v75_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-111743-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

### How Admins can add notes:

There are two ways to add accessibility notes to a Patron's order/profile.

**During checkout**:

When you are creating a new order, scroll to the bottom of the checkout page and click the box for **Accessibility** on the right-hand side.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/atd3z5_-aQOEBDhjPpem-_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-112144-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

The notes will automatically save as you edit.

**To add notes directly to a patron's profile:**

1.  Search for the patron using the search bar on your admin panel.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [View/Edit patron profiles](https://support.ludus.com/viewedit-patron-profiles)
    
2.  Scroll to the section titled **Accessibility Details**.
    
3.  Type to update the details.
    
    The accessibility details will automatically display on the ticket lists for your check-in. To remove these from the display, uncheck the box for **Show Patron Accommodations on Ticket List**.
