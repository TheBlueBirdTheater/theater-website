# Set public contact information

On your main events page, you can set contact information. This will give patrons an address and/or phone number at which they at connect with your organization. Additionally, you can choose the contact email that will be used as the default reply email for any messages that you send out through Ludus on an event or Marketing emails.

_Suggested Article_: [Event Messaging](https://support.ludus.com/event-messaging)

To edit this section:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Under the **General** tab, type in the email, address, phone number, country, state, and timezone as desired.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/6NMNVGn41xDSfLGt3f7Z6_screen-shot-2023-09-04-at-105215-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click **Save Settings** at the bottom of the page.
