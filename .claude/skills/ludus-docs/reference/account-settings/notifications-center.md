# Notifications Center

The Ludus Notification Center lets you set which items you and your team are notified about.

You can find this under **Settings** (gear icon at the top right corner) -> **Notifications**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/DUKD0bmKU28W-4S2zF9yX_screenshot-2026-06-30-at-125955-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

_This includes:_

-   **Tickets Sold**

-   When to Notify (Immediately or in a Daily Summary)

-   Who Gets Notified (Default Recipients or Custom)

-   **Passes Sold**

-   When to Notify (Immediately or in a Daily Summary)

-   Who Gets Notified (Default Recipients or Custom)

-   **Donations Made**

-   When to Notify (Immediately or in a Daily Summary)

-   Who Gets Notified (Default Recipients or Custom)

-   **Class Registrations**

-   When to Notify (Immediately or in a Daily Summary)

-   Who Gets Notified (Default Recipients or Custom)

-   **Collection Payments**

-   When to Notify (Immediately or in a Daily Summary)

-   Who Gets Notified (Default Recipients or Custom)

-   **Events Sold Out**

-   When to Notify (Immediately or in a Daily Summary)

-   Who Gets Notified (Default Recipients or Custom)

-   **Daily Summary Email**

-   Who Gets Notified (Default Recipients or Custom)

If you select Custom email recipients for any of the above notification options, you can enter the email(s) that should receive those notifications.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/6J40jpOHHfgMgYzeyFULn_screenshot-2026-06-30-at-11248-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

You can also select the **Preview** button next to each option to see what it will look like.
