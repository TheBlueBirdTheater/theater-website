# Taxes

If you are required to collect sales tax in your state, county, or city, this page lets you set your sales tax percentage. Sales Tax is added to tickets, fees, Add-Ons, and Passes.

If specific fees or tickets are exempt from taxes, simply go to **edit** those -> **advanced options** (in the edit) -> make "**Tax Exempt**".

Once you have set up **Sales Tax** for your account, the appropriate percentage of tax will be collected for your sales and included with your payouts. To see the amount of sales tax collected for a payout, navigate to **Reports** _**→**_ **Proceeds**. Select the payout you wish to view, and then click **Reconcile**.

Tax is applied after the Ludus Fees.
