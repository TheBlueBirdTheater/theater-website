# Collect Sales Tax

Ludus cannot give tax advice or tell you whether or not you should collect sales tax. Please consult a tax expert for any questions related to sales tax and your organization/program.

## **Activate Sales Tax**

1.  Click the **Settings Wheel Icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your screen.
    
2.  Choose the **Taxes** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/5zq9YcL4PYSEVwtRNA6HF_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-09t172850197.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Read the message at the top of the page. Once you have done so, click **ON** to begin collecting sales tax.
    
4.  Insert the percentage and click **Save**. Tax will be collected for your sales and included with your payouts.
