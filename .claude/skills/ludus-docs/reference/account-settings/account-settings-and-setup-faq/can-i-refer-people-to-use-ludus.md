# Can I refer people to use Ludus?

Your recommendation of Ludus to other users and organizations is the highest praise we can receive! To show you how much it means, we have the Ludus Refer and Earn program. Once an account you refer to Ludus goes live with ticket sales, we'll issue a $25 gift card for both you and the group you referred. You can send your share link to friends and colleagues, post to Facebook Groups, and anywhere else you connect with others!

### To start referring:

1.  Log in to your admin panel using the designated login.
    
2.  In the bottom left-hand corner of your screen, click **Refer and Earn**.
    
3.  From the referral hub, use the link, email, Facebook, SMS text, or Messenger option to share your referral code.

4.  Check out the "Referral Status" section to view any account that has started the signup process!
