# Why is my Ludus account not appearing in Google searches?

When you create a Ludus account for your group or have the domain name changed on your existing account, it will take some time for Google to index the new site and add it to the listing.

According to Google, for sites with fewer than 500 pages, it can take several hours up to 3/4 weeks. On average, it is generally about 1 week.
