# Other Payment Options

Sometimes there may be special payment methods outside of the standard credit or cash that you wish to collect. For example, you might frequently receive checks and want a payment method just for those. Ludus offers you full flexibility with the ability to create your own customizable Other payment option.

Other payment methods will appear as such within reports, allowing you to keep track of methods that fall outside of credit, cash, and complimentary sales.

Ludus does not collect the funds that you mark under an Other payment option. These methods are treated similarly to cash in the system in that they will appear on your reporting but they will not appear in the proceeds that are sent from Ludus.
