# Edit/Delete Other payment options

Maybe you want to change the details of an Other Payment option or you simply need to delete the payment option altogether. Regardless of the motive, it is easy to do within Ludus.

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Navigate to the **Payments** tab.
    
3.  Scroll down to the section for **Other Payment Options**.
    
4.  Click **Edit** to alter details.
    
    Click **Delete** to delete the entire option.
