# Add Other payment options

### To add an "Other" payment method:

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Navigate to the **Payments** tab.
    
3.  Click **+Add Other Payment Option**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/oz_5eLQAg_t6XosoRRPWH_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-72710-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  At a minimum, enter the **Name** of your other payment option. If you have additional fields you'd like to capture with additional fields, type those into the **Custom Fields**. (e.g, you could set up an Other Payment option titled check and want to have a custom field for check number and a custom field for memo)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/kt2QMWdNgTXRkn4ReneEr_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-73121-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  Click **+Add** to save.
