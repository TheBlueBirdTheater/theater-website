# Check payout schedules

### Schedule for paper checks

-   **Tickets** revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once your event is complete

-   **Collections** revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once the set payment deadline is hit

-   **Classes** revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once the last class time has passed

-   **Donation** proceeds are mailed on the 1st of every month

-   **Pass** proceeds are mailed on the 1st of every month

-   **Gift Card** proceeds are mailed on the 1st of every month

-   **Retail** proceeds are sent on the 1st of every month (This is in a separate check from the Event)

To learn more about requesting paper checks for an event, collection, or class, check out this article.
