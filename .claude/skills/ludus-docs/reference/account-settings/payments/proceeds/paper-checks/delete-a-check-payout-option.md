# Delete a Check Payout Option

You may find that you no longer need one of the payout methods that you created in the past. These could be old groups that have previously used your space, or perhaps you are restructuring your Ludus account. This article will show you how to remove any payout methods you no longer need.

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) in the upper right-hand corner of your admin panel.
    
2.  Select the **Payments** tab.
    
3.  Next to the desired check option, click **Delete**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/i8VB_a3nqIGink0fkXtxz_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-62627-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    You can't delete your primary check option. If you'd like to switch your primary check option, click the star next to the corresponding option.
