# Request paper check payout

Some checks within Ludus are sent automatically or on a monthly basis

**Paper check schedule**

-   **Ticket** revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once your event is complete

-   **Collections** revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once the set payment deadline is hit

-   **Classes** revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once the last class time has passed

-   **Donation** proceeds are automatically mailed on the 1st of every month

-   If needed, an early payout for donations CAN be requested.

-   **Pass** proceeds are automatically mailed on the 1st of every month

-   If needed, an early payout for passes CAN be requested.

-   **Pass Renewals** proceeds are sent out with the event proceeds when completed and requested.

-   **Invoicing** proceeds are automatically mailed on the 1st of every month

-   If needed, an early payout for passes CAN be requested.

-   **Gift Card** proceeds are automatically mailed on the 1st of every month

-   If needed, an early payout for gift cards CAN be requested.

### Requesting a check for Events, Collections, and Classes

For events, collections, and classes, you will have a blue banner that appears once the last time/deadline has passed. Whenever you are ready to receive those proceeds:

1.  Verify that the information is accurate for your payout method. To view your check payout methods, click the **Settings wheel icon** (⚙️) --> **Payments**.
    
    If you have multiple check options on your account, you will want to verify which option is attached to the particular event/collection/class.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Set alternative check methods per event/donation/etc.](https://support.ludus.com/set-alternative-check-methods-per-eventdonationetc)
    
2.  Click **Request Funds** from the blue banner below your event/collection/class.

### Request an early payout

There may be certain circumstances where you'd like to receive proceeds before the event is over. To request an early check payout for any current proceeds, please contact customer support by emailing support@ludus.com or using the live chat button in the bottom right-hand corner of your screen.
