# Add or Remove Team Members to your Direct Deposit Account.

Once you have a direct deposit account created, you can invite an additional user to be an administrator of the direct deposit account. You may wish to set this up with a secondary email/phone number to have a backup method should the main admin no longer be active with your group.

### Add a team member to your direct deposit account

1.  Log in to your direct Deposit account either through the Settings -> Payment page on Ludus by clicking to update your account. Or log in directly at [https://connect.stripe.com/express\_login](https://connect.stripe.com/express_login "https://connect.stripe.com/express_login")
    
2.  Click the person Icon at the top of the page
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/iSTHK8FgFmZ2iLrX2P8JH_image-11.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click on **Ludus** under **Platform Settings.**
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/mSYOo8tDKLLVTsr6Wrbvd_stripe-express.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  On the modal that opens, select **Invite and Remove** under the **Team Members** section
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/zPDPqbjmz-Exm0orE_F6n_stripe-express-1.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  Click **Add Team Member.** You will then be asked to enter the email of the team member to add to your account.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/680yhQ2THo38rtO6NMJkg_stripe-express-2.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

The invited team member will get an email to accept the invitation. When they accept, they will be asked for their phone number for verification. Once they have provided a phone numbr they are added to the account and have full access to edit settings such as your payout schedule and direct deposit account settings.
