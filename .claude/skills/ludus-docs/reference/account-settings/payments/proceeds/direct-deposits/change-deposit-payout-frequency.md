# Change deposit payout frequency

You can also choose a frequency when you choose direct deposit as your proceeds option. You can decide if you'd like to receive your funds daily, weekly, or monthly. This allows you to set a frequency that meets your cash flow needs. The default when you first set up your proceeds will be daily. If you'd like to change that check out the steps below.

If you wish to receive proceeds **AFTER** an event is concluded (instead of daily, weekly, or monthly) please contact [Live Chat Support](https://support.ludus.com/live-chat-support) to have Manual Deposits set up for your account. It is recommended this be done **BEFORE** sales are live.

1.  Click the **Settings wheel icon** in the upper right-hand corner of your screen.
    
2.  Navigate to the **Payments** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/428YNdr8HNpzEah2ks3Tl_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-43429-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Under the details for your direct deposit, click **Change Payout Frequency**.
    
    If you have yet to set up your direct deposit information, you will want to do that first. To see what information is required, check out this article.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Information needed for direct deposits](https://support.ludus.com/information-needed-for-direct-deposits)
    
4.  From the pop-up menu, click the drop-down and select your desired frequency.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/EE_EfidTPY7GPHdJyZHiR_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-43747-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    If you choose **Weekly** or **Monthly,** you will be prompted to choose the day of the week or day of the month accordingly.
    
5.  Click **Save**.
    

For Monthly Direct Deposits - Payout will be initiated a day before the last day of the Month in attempt for the funds to be allocated to your account by the last day of the month.
