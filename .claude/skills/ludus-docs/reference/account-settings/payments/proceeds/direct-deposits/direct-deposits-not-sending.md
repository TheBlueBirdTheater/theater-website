# Direct Deposits Not Sending

If you have direct deposits set up (either daily, weekly, or monthly) and you notice you have money that has been collected but not been deposited, please reach out to [Live Chat Support](https://support.ludus.com/live-chat-support) .

Most likely, what has happened in this situation is that Stripe requires more information from your group for your account. Once that is submitted, your deposits will continue to go through.
