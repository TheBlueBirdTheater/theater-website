# Accessing your Stripe Express Account (Direct Deposit)

The admin who sets up direct deposit and any team member added to the Stripe Express account for direct deposits can log in directly to Stripe to update information and view or edit certain account settings.

### How to log in to Stripe to access your direct deposit account.

Admins on the Stripe Express account can access the account by using the link below: [https://connect.stripe.com/express\_login](https://connect.stripe.com/express_login "https://connect.stripe.com/express_login ") . Users do not have to be an Admin in Ludus to access the Stripe account with this method.

Additionally, if an admin does have a Ludus Username / Password, they can access the Stripe Account via the Settings screen by selecting Payments and then choosing Update

Log in to your Stripe Express account either through the Settings -> Payment page on Ludus by clicking to update your account or to Change Payout Frequency.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/i9WwuxQGfltFlC8hJmouv_ludus-studios-2-admin-3.png?format=webp "Document image")

Any Full Admin who navigates to the Payments Tab will see any alerts from Stripe if information is needed. These types of alerts also trigger an email to the Stripe Express users for your account directly from Stripe with a link to provide any required information.

**Only Stripe Express users can log in to the Stripe account from the Payments Screen.**
