# Payment Descriptors

When a patron purchases an item through your Ludus account, depending on your payout method, they will see one of 2 things for their payment descriptor:

1.  **Paper Check payout accounts** - These will show as LUDUS.COM \* \[Your group's name\] for the descriptor.

1.  Example: LUDUS.COM \* STAGE PLAYERS

2.  **Direct Deposit payout accounts** - These will show _just_ your account's name as the statement descriptor

1.  Example: STAGE PLAYERS
