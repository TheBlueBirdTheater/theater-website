# Proceeds

Ludus is a 100% automated system, meaning that as sales come in, your proceeds are collected and sent to you instantly, allowing you to sit back and relax.

_**You do not have to manage a separate payment account — we handle everything for you.**_

## How can I receive my proceeds?

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/pohBfRqSBDO8wPRrewWW7-20260508-163817.png?format=webp "Document image")

### There are two ways for you to receive your proceeds:

**Direct Deposit**: Have your proceeds deposited daily, weekly, or monthly into a bank account. (No additional fees. We cover CC fees using the Ludus convenience fee, so you always receive the full face value of your tickets.)

**Paper Checks**: Have a paper check sent to you at the end of your event.

**Note** - Regardless of which method you choose, there are never any fees to receive your proceeds—no hidden fees or surprises. You choose the method that works best for you and let Ludus take care of the rest.

## When will my proceeds be sent?

### If you're receiving direct deposits

Your proceeds will be deposited per the schedule you chose (daily, weekly, or monthly). To see your direct deposit option, go to **Reports** -> **Proceeds** in your admin panel, and it'll give you an estimate of when to expect your next proceeds payout.

### If you're receiving a check at the end of your event

**For Events/Ticket Proceeds:** When ready, click the **Send Funds** button beneath your event to request a payout. Your proceeds check will then be processed and mailed via USPS First Class mail.

To check information on and status of your paper check, click the **Reports** dropdown within your admin panel -> **Proceeds** -> view check information listed and click for more details.

**Paper check schedule**

-   Ticket revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once your event is complete

-   Collections revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once the set payment deadline is hit

-   Classes revenue is mailed _**upon request**_ once the last class time has passed

-   Donation proceeds are mailed on the 1st of every month

-   Pass proceeds are mailed on the 1st of every month

-   Gift Card proceeds are mailed on the 1st of every month
