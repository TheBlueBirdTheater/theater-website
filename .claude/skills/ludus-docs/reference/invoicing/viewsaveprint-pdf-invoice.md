# View/Save/Print PDF Invoice

If you or your organization prefers to mail physical copies of invoices or simply needs to print them out, you can easily save/print a copy of the invoice from the details page.

To view, save, and or print a PDF copy of your invoice:

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **More** --> **Invoicing**.
    
2.  On the main Invoicing page, locate the invoice for which you'd like to collect payment and click **"View"**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/6VzATL29EHfgVFzEXlwN2-20251001-191114.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    You can sort invoices by status from the top bar or use the search bar to search by name, email, or invoice number.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-KYF4rVw_jjdP_SZAKq9oT-20250826-154542.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  At the top of the page, click the **PDF Page** symbol.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/WkwetgzSfgrsa290m6KqD-20251001-191250.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  The invoice will open in a new tab. You can then use your browser to print, download, or view it.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/KrzE2Lrkn49HgtLR6mdnJ-20251001-191509.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

Once an invoice has been paid, you can still view, save, and print a PDF version. After payment, the invoice will display a zero-dollar balance and reflect a paid status.
