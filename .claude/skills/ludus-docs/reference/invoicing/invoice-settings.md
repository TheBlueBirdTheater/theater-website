# Invoice Settings

Invoice Settings enables you to set defaults for your invoices, including branding, default due dates, and messaging.

When editing your defaults, be sure to click Save Changes before exiting.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/mjaw7SRkpK8e35JfkV4wT-20251215-175417.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Choose defaults

To set the defaults,

1.  Navigate to **More** --> **Invoicing** from the top navigation bar.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-18krHSFq3-Vba59Tj0Np5-20250618-191216.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  From the main Invoicing screen, select the **Invoice Settings** button.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Kh0WZNCzfwQk4mLZqQfNC-20251215-175247.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Please complete each section according to your preferences. In the next section of this article, you will learn more about each setting.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/DnEeZ0LWzGqgJPi1uCdXC-20251215-175310.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Click **Save Settings** to save any progress.
    

### Settings and Terms

-   **Invoice Logo** - The logo that will appear at the heading of the invoice, payment page, and email.

-   **Sender Name** - The name that will appear on emails when the invoice is sent.

-   **Reply-To Email** \- The email from which an invoice will be sent. (e.g. yourname@gmail.com)

-   **Sender Address** - The address that will be listed on the invoice.

-   **Invoice Prefix** - The text portion of your numbering scheme on Invoices. (e.g. INV)

-   **Starting Number** - The starting number for your invoice numbering scheme. (Note: Changing this number after initial setup may cause you to have duplicate invoice numbers.

-   **Increment** - The amount the numbering scheme will increase for each new invoice.

-   **Default Invoice Term** - The default time period after the invoice is created that it will be due. This can be changed for individual invoices when creating/sending.

-   **Invoice Fee Settings** - For every payment made via credit card, a fee of 5% + $1.25 is charged. Choose whether to absorb the fee or pass it on to the patron.

-   **Memo and Footer Defaults** - Default text that will appear on the invoice and email.
