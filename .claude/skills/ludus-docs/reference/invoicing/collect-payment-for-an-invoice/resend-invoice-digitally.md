# Resend Invoice Digitally

If you have updated the email associated with the invoice or the patron has mistakenly deleted the original and needs it resent, you can quickly send another digital copy of the invoice.

### To resend an invoice:

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **More** --> **Invoicing**.
    
2.  On the main Invoicing page, find the invoice for which you'd like to collect payment and click **View**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-05OGglZv6k65lVbbHv3cb-20250829-135819.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    You can sort invoices by status from the top bar or use the search bar to search by name, email, or invoice number.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-170mAOYMV9Ei5J1L4ZDGt-20250829-135909.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  At the top of the page, click **Resend Invoice**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-dftmZbHCKYwHkFINKrv2M-20250829-140028.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

You can also resend an invoice after it has been paid. Then, the patron will receive a second copy of the email that shows the invoice has been paid. The steps are the same as above.
