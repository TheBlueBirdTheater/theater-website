# Add-Ons with Invoice Me

When using Invoice Me, there are no changes in the ability to sell Add-Ons with your Collections or Classes.

However, you can prevent an Add-On from being available when using Invoice Me, if desired.

To prevent an add-on from being available or sold with a fee, select the option labeled **Disable Invoice Me** in the **Advanced Options** section of the Add-On Settings.
