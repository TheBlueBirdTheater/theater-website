# Create Fees for Invoice Me

When your Collection or Class is set up with the Invoice Me option activation, your fees (pricing) will automatically be set to allow Invoice Me as an option. The only change to setting up fees for your Collection or Class is if you want to disable the Invoice Me Option for a specific fee.

_**Suggested Articles:**_

[Add Fees to a Collection](https://support.ludus.com/add-fees-to-a-collection "Add Fees to a Collection")

[Add Fees to a Class](https://support.ludus.com/add-fees-to-a-class "Add Fees to a Class")

### Preventing a Fee from being selected by the Patron for Invoice Me

To set a specific fee to prevent Invoice Me, check the **Disable Invoice Me** box in the fee's settings when creating or editing the fee.
