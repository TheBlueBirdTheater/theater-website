# Add images and videos to your Ludus page

If you are familiar with the [Notices](https://support.ludus.com/notices) feature within Ludus, you will know that you can display custom messaging throughout the Ludus page and ticket-buying experience. These notice fields also allow you to insert videos and images.

### Adding an Image

When editing a notice (_Suggested Article__**:**_ [Add Global Notices](https://support.ludus.com/add-global-notices)), follow the steps below to add an image.

1.  Click the image icon from the toolbar.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/w9_sy5mbspsMAcLfTGecd_screen-shot-2023-08-01-at-42309-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Choose the desired image from your files.
    
3.  Click **Submit**.
    

You can click the image itself to add a caption, change alternative text, and adjust the alignment.

### Adding a Video

When editing a notice (_Suggested Article__**:**_ [Add Global Notices](https://support.ludus.com/add-global-notices)), follow the steps below to add a video.

The video will need to be hosted on a site such as YouTube or Vimeo.

1.  Click the three vertical dots on the toolbar.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/6Yt53i-Q-zGisO2o41tMW_screen-shot-2023-08-01-at-43054-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Click the **Insert Media** button.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/lYNf8ZzQ4_ZyeQatj2bWM_screen-shot-2023-08-02-at-81651-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Add the URL of the video to the box and click the **checkmark icon** (✅).
    
4.  Click **Submit**.
