# Patrons CRM

Patrons CRM is the home of your patron database as well as other customer relationship management (CRM) tools within Ludus.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/xeAlaWeshxTllqPih19Pv-20260114-135158.png?format=webp "Document image")

## What can this feature help with?

The Patron feature in Ludus was created to make tracking and updating your patron database as simple as possible.

## Why use this feature?

Every time a patron interacts with your site, they are added as a new patron or their patron record is updated. So if they purchase a ticket, that will show on their patron profile. If they want to purchase a pass or maybe donate, those will appear on their patron profile as well!

## What makes this feature so special?

Ludus was built for the performing arts by people who care about them, and we want to make your life easier. The Patrons feature in Ludus has so many amazing extras, including:

-   The **Database** and the ability to import patrons.

-   The **Audience** ability, which lets you create Marketing Audiences.

-   The **Properties** that allow adding additional fields to patrons.

-   **Tasks** is a CRM tool that lets you create and assign interactions to your patrons.

-   Using **Patron Tags** lets you track and manage patrons by unique tags (which is especially helpful for _Marketing_).

-   The **Households** feature lets you view a household's spending habits.

## So how does this feature work?

The Patrons Feature provides an overview of all your patrons. You can select specific patrons and dig into their records while also managing which marketing campaigns they receive via tags. Overall, this feature will give you an overarching view of your patron data.

## How do I get started in the Patrons Tab?

To start, if you are new to Ludus and do not have any patrons in the system yet, a great place to start would be to [Import Patrons to Ludus](https://support.ludus.com/import-patrons-to-ludus) . By doing this, you are setting up your patron dashboard for the best success!

If you have been using Ludus for a while and are new to using the patron dashboard, digging into [Patron Profiles](https://support.ludus.com/patron-profiles) and their history can be incredibly helpful!
