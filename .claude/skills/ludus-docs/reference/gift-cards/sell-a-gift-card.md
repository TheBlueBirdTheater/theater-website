# Sell a Gift Card

Gift cards can be sold/issued via the admin panel or by patrons online.

### Issue a Gift Card from the Admin Panel

Follow these three steps to issue a gift card from the admin panel:

1.  Navigate to gift cards by clicking **More → Gift Cards.**

2.  Click **New Gift Card** to start the process of creating your gift card.

3.  Select the payment type from the tabs at the top. Then, enter the gift card information and collect payment.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-IOOsXIAEcDwFO4a4Y4lG0-20250428-191117.png?format=webp "Document image")

When finished and payment is collected, the patron will receive an email with the digital gift card and related information!

You can view all purchased/issued gift cards under **Reports** (at the top) → **Gift Cards**

### Purchase a Gift Card from the Patron Site

Patrons can navigate to the Gift Card tab to purchase a gift card. Once there, they can enter their information (or the information of the person they are buying for), and the gift card will be sent digitally via email.
