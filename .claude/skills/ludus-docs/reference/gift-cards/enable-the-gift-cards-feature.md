# Enable the Gift Cards feature

Our Gift Cards feature **can be enabled for any account simply by reaching out to** [Support](https://support.ludus.com/support) !

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-iSaWMZCqYQ1k-9er07EgM-20250428-185951.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once enabled, your patrons can purchase and send Gift Cards to friends and family. On your end via the admin panel, you can issue Gift Cards via credit, cash, or complimentary, _and_ keep track of all outstanding balances/cards.
