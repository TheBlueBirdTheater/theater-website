# Hide Gift Cards From Public Pages

You can hide the ability to purchase a gift card from the public if desired.

Follow these steps to hide that option:

1.  Navigate to Gift Cards by clicking **More** at the top navigation bar -> select **Gift Cards.**
    
2.  Click on **Settings**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-FY4KpLse-Wh_So_AxGcLv-20250618-200224.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Adjust the **Hide from the Public** option to say _Yes,_ and then click **Save Changes**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-NJ_x0QzgNGNyZN9reJhUN-20250428-193436.png?format=webp "Document image")
