# Direct link to purchase Gift Cards

If you are trying to promote gift cards or perhaps would just like to share a direct link with patrons, this can easily be accomplished through the Gift Card Settings.

Simply go to **More** (at the top of your admin panel) -> **Gift Cards** \-> **Settings** (next to +New Gift Card). Once there, you can copy the **Gift Card Share Link,** which will direct patrons to Gift Card purchases.
