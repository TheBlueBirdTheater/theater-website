# Refund a Gift Card

If needed, Gift Cards can be refunded to the original method of purchase. The following steps will explain how to refund the purchase of a Gift Card.

1.  **Locate** the gift card to be refunded. You can do this via **Reports-> Gift Cards** or the Gift Card Screen under **More**, top Navigation, and then **Gift Card**.
    
2.  **Select** the gift card to refund by clicking the code OR searching the code using the search bar/magnifying glass.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-FY4KpLse-Wh_So_AxGcLv-20250618-200224.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-aD0YPeOvI_UD_r5dfEMtE-20250428-193111.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click the **Refund** button on the order details.
    
4.  A new window will open up to process the refund. From here you can:
    
    -   Enter the reason for the refund in the drop-down
    
    -   Enter the amount of the refund
    
    -   It can be in fully or partially refunded
    
    -   Enter optional details of the refund
    
    -   Enter an email address to send the confirmation to.
    
    All refunds are sent back to the original purchase method.
    
5.  Click **Refund** to process the refund.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/q8BxbtPcKNEOq6vZoAM7x_ludus-manual-admin-17.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

Credit Card refunds will be processed automatically; cash or Check refunds must be issued by your group.

**Note** - You cannot refund any more than the current balance on the gift card. If any of the balance has been used, that cannot be refunded unless the tickets/items purchased with the gift card are also refunded.
