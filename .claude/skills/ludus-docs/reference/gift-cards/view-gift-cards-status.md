# View Gift Cards Status

# View outstanding Gift Cards

When Gift Cards are enabled, it might be necessary to see how many outstanding gift cards are still unclaimed. This could be useful for reporting or just general knowledge.

To view the outstanding gift cards: go to **More** (at the top of your admin panel) -> **Gift Cards.** From here, you will be able to select using the top filters and see:

-   All Cards (active and not)

-   Active Cards

-   Empty Cards

-   Expired Cards

-   Disabled Cards.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-FY4KpLse-Wh_So_AxGcLv-20250618-200224.png?format=webp "Document image")

By selecting each of those filters, you will narrow the entire list of gift cards to the ones you wish to see.
