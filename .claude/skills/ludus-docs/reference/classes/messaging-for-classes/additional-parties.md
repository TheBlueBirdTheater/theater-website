# Additional Parties

When a patron goes to register for a class, they will insert their contact information (and the contact information for the attendee if they are registering someone else such as their child). Both the registrant and attendee can be messaged about any updates or notices to the class. However, there may be additional people who the registrant would like to keep afloat of such information. Maybe Grandma is going to be picking up the attendee every other week. Grandma can be added as an additional party for the student and you can choose to include them in all future messaging.
