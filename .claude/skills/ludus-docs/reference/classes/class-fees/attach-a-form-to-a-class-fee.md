# Attach a Form to a Class fee

Forms are found throughout Ludus and are a way to collect additional information from purchasers beyond name, email, phone number, and address. Check out this article to learn more about Forms and the various fields you can set up.

_Suggested Article_: [Forms](https://support.ludus.com/forms)

Student profiles are the main way to ask your registrants questions. Check out this article to learn more about the difference between Forms and Student Profiles.

_Suggested Article_: [Ask questions of Class registrants (Student profiles vs. Form submissions)](https://support.ludus.com/ask-questions-of-class-registrants-student-profiles-vs-form-submissions)

While you can attach a Form to a Class overall, there might be times when you only want to ask the questions on a form if they choose a specific fee. (i.e., You have a Pre-K price and an elementary price, and you only want to ask special questions for people who pick the Pre-K price and not the elementary price). Check out the steps below to learn how to achieve this.

Before attaching it, make sure you have built your form. To learn how to do this, check out our article.

_Suggested Article_: [Create a Form](https://support.ludus.com/create-a-form)

### Attach to a Class Fee

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **Collections**\--> **Classes**.
    
2.  Next to the desired Class, click **Pricing** --> **Fees**.
    
3.  Find the fee to which you'd like to attach a form and click **Edit**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/7KggqfivQCbOAwferpj47_screen-shot-2023-08-30-at-84754-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Click **Advanced Options** and then scroll down to the section titled "Form."
    
5.  From the drop-down menu, select the form you'd like to attach.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fetC4qm9D4qn3Qcm_WBwc_screen-shot-2023-08-30-at-83209-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  Click **Save Changes**.
    

If any forms are attached to the Class, patrons will also be asked to complete them at checkout. Registrants will also have to fill out questions on the Student Profiles.
