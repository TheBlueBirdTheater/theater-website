# Patron Messaging and Notifications for Payment Plans

Patrons will receive messaging and notifications regarding their payments and the plan. These emails and notifications will vary based on the information needed and will help keep your patrons up to date on their progress.

### Enrollment Confirmation Email

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/UQB7s9H7wyGlbHR6TCZqd-20260507-161913.png?format=webp "Document image")

## Failed and Missing Payment Method Emails

### Failed Payment

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/GanB5ZG-CjLPQ2B8xuCNt-20260507-164112.png?format=webp "Document image")

The system will attempt to charge the card 3 days in a row before ceasing further charges. Each charge will be followed by an email noting how many more attempts it will make to charge.
