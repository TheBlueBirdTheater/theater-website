# View a Specific Payment Plan

To open a specific patron payment plan, simply go to **Collections** (at the top of your account) -> **Payment Plans** -> select **View** next to the plan you wish to open.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/h5v7eyErBhCdF5DAvNoaR-20260507-165756.png?format=webp "Document image")

**Once opened, you will be able to access:**

1.  The Payment Plan Summary and Patron Details.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/3z_XEEa1o_Zg8yJcHY60g-20260507-165930.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Payments and Items included in the Payments.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/dkA_8KaYZJhrK8jIkKUXX-20260507-170037.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Form Submissions (if any were filled out), and Payment Plan activity.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Wzx0xETJbf5rKxdDIm-bB-20260507-170115.png?format=webp "Document image")
