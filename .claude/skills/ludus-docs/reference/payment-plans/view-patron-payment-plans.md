# View Patron Payment Plans

Similar to Invoicing, you can see all Payment Plans and their status.

To view these plans, simply select **Collections** (at the top of your account) -> **Payment Plans**. From here, you can see and access all payment plans.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/XdBysnTqVm4ThMVa8h-iO-20260507-164934.png?format=webp "Document image")

### This page will include:

1.  **Plan ID**

2.  **Status of the Plan**

1.  In Progress

2.  Paid

3.  Payment Failure

4.  No Payment Method

5.  Void

3.  **Class**

1.  Which class is the plan for?

4.  **Recipient**

1.  Who is paying for the plan?

5.  **Email**

1.  The email of the recipient.

6.  **Payments**

1.  How many payments have they made? For example, if there are 4 payments and they have made 1, it will show as 1/4.

7.  **Due**

1.  Amount still due.

8.  **Paid**

1.  Amount paid to date.

Additionally, you can select **View** to [View a Specific Payment Plan](https://support.ludus.com/view-a-specific-payment-plan) .
