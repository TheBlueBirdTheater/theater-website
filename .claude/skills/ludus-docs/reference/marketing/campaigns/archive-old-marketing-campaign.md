# Archive old Marketing Campaign

Once you finish a marketing campaign and want to remove it from your everyday view, you can archive it. This will allow you to go back and view a history (an archive, if you will) of prior campaigns while keeping your list of active campaigns clean and organized.

Archive a campaign:

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**.
    
2.  For the desired campaign, click **Options** --> **Archive**
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/IquLU9ey8paJUe6sDTXYn_screen-shot-2023-09-19-at-14508-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### View Archived Campaigns

To view an archived campaign, navigate to **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**. Then, scroll to the bottom of the page and click **View Archived Campaigns**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/iMrSPNom5Ti-Li9X-_vvk_screen-shot-2023-09-19-at-15203-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Recover an Archived Campaign

To recover an archived campaign, follow the steps above to find an archived campaign. Then, next to the campaign, click **Options** --> **Recover**.
