# Campaign Examples and Ideas

Looking for ideas and items to add to your Campaign? Here are a few to get you started:

1.  ### [](#merge-tags)Merge Tags
    
    Using [Merge Tags & Campaigns](https://support.ludus.com/merge-tags-and-campaigns) is a fantastic way to make your Campaign more individualized to your patrons, by simply adding their names to the message!
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-ZQi_-8UL6NxQXDntTqcHm-20250812-144138.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  ### [](#upcoming-events-the-ability-to-purchase)Upcoming Events & the Ability to Purchase
    
    When crafting your email, whether it's a reminder or information about an upcoming performance, consider including any upcoming events. Additionally, adding a "buy" button creates a fantastic way for patrons to easily access those events!
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-eTn8RxNEWBsEsdtn9R6Uj-20250812-144720.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  ### [](#social-media-links)Social Media Links
    
    Adding your social media links is a fantastic way to engage your patrons on other platforms and keep them up to date on your group.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-3LKze-lsebH6SLhgV2vDm-20250812-145324.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  ### [](#donations)Donations
    
    Whether you are thanking your donors or adding a "donate button" to your campaign, this is a great way to reach out to your patrons. A simple donate button in a campaign could be the perfect way to get patrons to support your group, while a thank you to all donors is another way to show your appreciation!
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-OJo8LpI8yhnaxeAx_TpdU-20250812-145928.png?format=webp "Document image")
