# Send a test email

You might wish to send a test email before going live with your campaign. To do so, navigate to **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**.

Then, next to the desired email campaign, click **Options** --> **Send Test**.

From there, you can either insert the email(s) to which you want to send a message or automatically send it to your Test Audience.
