# Place Videos in Marketing emails

Adding videos to your marketing emails is a great way to engage with your audience. Here's how to achieve that within Ludus.

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**.
    
2.  Next to the desired campaign, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Scroll down to the "Content" section and click **Open Email Designer**.
    
4.  From the panel on the right-hand side, drag the **Video** box to where you'd like in the body of the email.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/_2eXLVq_3Zmo1plT2fdmg_screen-shot-2023-09-18-at-81719-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  Click on the video box within the body of the email and paste the link to your YouTube or Vimeo video.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/uUJCTw0SOCh4ca5PQ9ONA_screen-shot-2023-09-18-at-81953-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

You can change the play button for the embedded video using the type, color, and size options in the right-hand menu.
