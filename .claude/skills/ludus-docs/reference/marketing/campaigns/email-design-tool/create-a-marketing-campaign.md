# Create a Marketing Campaign

Within Campaigns, you can customize the title, "from" name, recipients, content, and timing of your next Marketing blast.

To start creating a Campaign, navigate to **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**.

## Steps to setup

1.  If this is the first Campaign, click **Create your first campaign**. Otherwise, select **New Campaign**.
    
2.  **Choose a name for your Campaign**. This is for internal use and is different than the subject line for your email blast.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-NLjIaSyA4CL-BQ0XkpKXz-20250228-183718.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  **Choose a Template for your email design**: You can choose from one of our preset options, a blank canvas, or use the base from an email you have sent in the past using **My Campaigns** (seen above).
    
4.  **Choose the recipients:** Under the section titled "To", select who will be receiving the blast. You can send it to all patrons in the database or a segmented group utilizing Audiences. _Suggested Article_: [Audiences](https://support.ludus.com/audiences)
    
    If a patron in your audience has a primary and secondary email on their account, both emails will receive this Campaign.
    
5.  **Choose "From" information**: Insert a Friendly-From name and an email that will receive any responses to the Campaign.
    
6.  **Choose the subject line**: In the box provided, type the subject line you would like for the campaign. This will be what patrons see in their inboxes.
    
7.  **Edit the content of your email:** Click **Open Email Designer** and edit the design of your Campaign.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Basics of the Marketing email design tool](https://support.ludus.com/basics-of-the-marketing-email-design-tool)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/DBX-mYcozqo3xAnpPLt1__screen-shot-2023-08-10-at-35045-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
8.  **Save/Send**: If you still need time to edit the overall Campaign, simply click **Save as draft**. When you are ready to send, choose the send time. You can send it immediately (an email will be sent within the next 10 minutes) or at a custom date/time in the future. Once you click **Set Live**, the email will be sent on the date selected.
