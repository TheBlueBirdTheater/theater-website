# Share a Marketing Campaign

After you have sent your marketing campaign to your subscribers you might want to share the message elsewhere like on social media or your website.

With the share link you can do just that.

Once your campaign has been sent out, A share button appears on the campaign that allows you to share a link or QR code outside of your mailing list.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-5rkJg_7YwvHq6LfegSYn--20240820-194637.png?format=webp "Document image")

Simply click the share button to obtain a link you can use or to obtain the QR code.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-XM064569h8RzCjZiE4S2Z-20240820-194746.png?format=webp "Document image")

When the link or QR code is used patrons are directed to a copy of your email on your Ludus ticketing site.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-Hy4wX-u-d8VmTR2XV5J7V-20240820-194927.png?format=webp "Document image")
