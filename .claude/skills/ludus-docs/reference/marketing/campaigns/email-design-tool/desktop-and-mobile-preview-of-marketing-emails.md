# Desktop and mobile preview of Marketing emails

In today's world, patrons may view your content in desktop or mobile browsers. Our marketing feature automatically optimizes your design to ensure patrons have a good viewing experience. Here is how to view each format before hitting send.

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**.
    
2.  Next to the desired campaign, click **Edit**.
    
3.  Scroll down to the "Content" section and click **Open Email Designer**
    
4.  In the bottom right-hand corner of the designer, use the **desktop and mobile icons** to toggle between the two views.
    

If you want to see what things will look like on a tablet, along with some additional options, simply select the eye icon right next door.
