# Save a draft of a campaign

Crafting the perfect email might take some time. When editing your [Campaigns](https://support.ludus.com/campaigns) (**Marketing** --> **Campaigns --> Edit**), you can scroll down to the bottom of the settings page and click **Save as Draft**. Additionally, a Save Draft button is at the top of the page.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/JakC7kT2NLg-3q4uVgSkv_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-07t091712358.png?format=webp "Document image")

If you are in the [Email Design Tool](https://support.ludus.com/email-design-tool), you will want to click **Continue** in the upper right-hand corner, and then scroll down to **Save as Draft**
