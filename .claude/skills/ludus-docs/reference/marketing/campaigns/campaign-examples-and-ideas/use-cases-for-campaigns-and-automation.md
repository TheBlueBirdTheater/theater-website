# Use Cases for Campaigns and Automation

Within the Marketing feature, there are two ways to communicate with your patrons: Campaigns and Automations.

### **Campaigns**

These are standard messages you can schedule to send to your patrons. They are great for the following:

-   Season announcements

-   Show announcements

-   Coming Soon!

-   Now available to purchase!

-   Early Bird / Early Access announcements

-   Special deals or discounts

-   VIP Experiences

-   Friends and Family Offer

-   Countdown to opening night

-   General information about an event

_Suggested Article:_ [Campaigns](https://support.ludus.com/campaigns "Campaigns")

### **Automations**

These are messages scheduled to go out to purchasers of an event ticket, collection, or class on a specific date or time before or after event times.

These are great for the following:

-   Sending a reminder to ticket holders or purchasers of a class.

-   Can include the patron login link so patrons can retrieve their tickets.

-   Information about the event includes:

-   Specific parking messages

-   Local places to dine before or after the event.

-   Other events in the area are going on near the event, so people can plan to arrive early / avoid traffic

-   Pre- or Post-event Surveys or questionnaires.

-   General information prior to class.

_Suggested Article:_ [Automations](https://support.ludus.com/automations "Automations")
