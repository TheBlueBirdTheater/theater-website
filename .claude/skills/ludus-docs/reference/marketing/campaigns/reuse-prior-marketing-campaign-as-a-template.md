# Reuse prior Marketing Campaign as a template

Within Ludus, you can utilize prior email campaigns as a template for your next email blast! To do so, follow the steps below.

1.  Navigate to **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**.
    
2.  Click **\+ New Campaign**.
    
3.  Under the "Templates " section, click the **My Campaigns** tab and select the previous campaign you'd like to use as a template.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/BhoBdhyPfP_KrK2LbTD05_reuse-prior-marketing-campaign-as-a-template-archbee-document.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Click **Next** and begin editing your email!
    

You can delete templates that were created by accident or are no longer needed as originally intended. If you wish to delete a template, you can do so when you create a Campaign under "My Campaigns".

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/nVF5fA6ggmZOUjzmvjs4q_screenshot-2026-06-23-at-102708-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

You **cannot** delete a template that is attached to a live or draft campaign.
