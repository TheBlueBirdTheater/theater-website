# Enable click tracking

To capture what patrons are clicking within your email campaigns, click **Marketing** --> **Settings**. Then, under the section titled "Options," click the box labeled **Click Tracking**.

When you go to view a campaign, you can see the overall number of clicks next to the campaign name on the main listing.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/RbnbwYTX4nVUutF6jMrLK_screen-shot-2023-08-10-at-53750-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

If you'd like to see which specific patrons have clicked the email, click **View** next to the desired campaign, then scroll to the bottom of the page. There, you can choose the **Clicked** tab and see the patrons who have done so.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/E_dkNg9-npc9bbSHo5HXP_screen-shot-2023-08-10-at-53825-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

When click tracking is active, you can see the date and time that your patron opened the email by hovering over the Opened Status indicator to the right of their name on the Opened tab.
