# Share Direct Mailing List sign-up link

Patrons will always be prompted to join your individual Mailing List(s) at checkout. However, you might want to offer the ability to sign up for a Mailing List even when they aren't buying a ticket.

By default, a box will appear on the main ticketing page for your Ludus account. There, patrons can click subscribe and enroll in any list.

![Document image](https://app.archbee.com/api/optimize/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/FPB9ufju_KzgoVOAvqOzS-20250929-160523.png "Document image")

If you'd like to send a direct link out to patrons via social media or another means, navigate to **Marketing** --> **Mailing Lists**.

Under the section titled "Mailing List Sign Up Form: Share Link", click **Copy to Clipboard**.
