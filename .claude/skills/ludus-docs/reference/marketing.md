# Marketing

Welcome to the Marketing hub. This will be a centralized location where you can find any information you need about the tool.

The Marketing feature lets you create beautiful, custom email campaigns to send to all or some of your patrons.

## Video Overview:

Marketing is one of the Premium Features within Ludus. This will add .05 to your base Ludus fee. There are no monthly fees or per-email fees associated. To learn more about the cost of Ludus, check out our Pricing Table.

_Suggested Article_: [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table)

## What can this feature help with?

The Marketing feature in Ludus is the perfect way to reach your patrons, as well as specific groups (such as donors or potential VIPs).

## Why use this feature?

We have seen this feature make all the difference for groups that need to reach out to all of their patrons, whether there is a venue change or perhaps an announcement for the upcoming season.

## What makes this feature so special?

Ludus was built for the performing arts by people who care about them, and we want to make your life easier. The Marketing feature in Ludus has so many amazing extras, including:

-   Create Custom Campaigns (emails)

-   Set up Custom Audience (to send to)

-   Create Automated Messages (for specific events/event times)

-   Overall Patron Communication

-   And so much more!

## So how does this feature work?

When you enable the Marketing feature, you can immediately send emails to your patrons. If you are just getting started with Ludus, you can even [Import Patrons to Ludus](https://support.ludus.com/import-patrons-to-ludus) in a matter of minutes and start emailing them right away.

## How do I start using Marketing?

Campaigns are the hub for all of your individual Marketing email blasts.

To enable this feature (and then create, edit, send, and view performance for Campaigns), navigate to **Marketing** --> **Campaigns**.

From here, you will be prompted to enable the Marketing feature in your Ludus account.
