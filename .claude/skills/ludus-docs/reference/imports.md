# Imports

## What can imports help with?

The ability to import items into your account goes beyond just patrons. While you can add patrons and do bulk updates to patron records at any time, imports can be used to complete additional tasks beyond onboarding your account.

## Why use the ability to import?

If you have a lot of data you wish to carry over from a previous platform, the ability to import is a fantastic way to keep that data with you and in Ludus.

## What makes importing so special?

Imports, like stated above, are a fantastic way to keep your data with you, not just in spreadsheets, but in your Ludus account.

Additionally, the ability to import not just patrons gives you a well-rounded account.

## So how do imports work?

Some items can be imported on your own, while other items will need to be handled by the Customer Support team.

Be sure to read through [What can be imported?](https://support.ludus.com/what-can-be-imported) to know for sure what can be imported and where it should be imported.
