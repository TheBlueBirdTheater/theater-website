# Make a purchase from the Supplies Shop

If you decide you'd like to purchase additional equipment (not required) for your Ludus box office setup, you can do so easily from the Supplies Shop.

All items are subject to availability.

If your organization is tax-exempt, please contact Ludus Customer Support before making a purchase.

### To make a purchase:

1.  From the top navigation bar, click **More** --> **Supplies Shop**.
    
2.  Beneath the item you'd like to purchase, click **View Product**.
    
3.  Choose the quantity you'd like to purchase and click **Add to Cart**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/zB24bUKzJh2dDDZNBk4dB_screen-shot-2023-09-17-at-35211-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Continue to add items as needed. Once complete, click **Checkout** from the right-hand panel.
    
5.  Type in your contact email, delivery address, shipping method, and payment method in the boxes provided.
    
6.  Review the purchase and click **Pay now** to complete your order.
    

Upon checkout, you can pay with a card; however, if you need to pay via PO, please reach out to [Live Chat Support](https://support.ludus.com/live-chat-support) , and they can assist with this.
