# How to Receive a Sales Payout in AnywhereSeat

Once you start making sales on AnywhereSeat, you're able to request payouts.

# There are two ways you can receive a sales payout:

## Direct Deposits

Simply connect your bank account, and we'll transfer your requested payouts (via the Balances tab on your account). This service is powered by Stripe, our payment processor. (In some cases, you can also connect a debit card for instant payouts.)

## Paper Checks (U.S. Only)

Simply provide a "pay to" name and address, and we'll send checks to you via USPS.

You can request payouts as many times as you'd like under **More** -> **Balances**.

_There is a $2 payout fee for either option._
