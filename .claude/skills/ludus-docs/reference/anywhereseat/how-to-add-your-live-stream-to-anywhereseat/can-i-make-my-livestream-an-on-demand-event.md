# Can I make my livestream an on-demand event?

Depending on how you are livestreaming, yes, this is possible. You will need the recordings from your livestream, whether those are from Vimeo or you have an Embed URL from your streaming software.

When setting up a new event on your account, you will select Video. From here, you can input that information for an on-demand (or video) event.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-5qDGUY0gs6Aah5U_tUQMP-20250609-145741.png?format=webp "Document image")

You could also make this free or provide a discount for those who attended the Live event (if you don't wish to charge them again).
