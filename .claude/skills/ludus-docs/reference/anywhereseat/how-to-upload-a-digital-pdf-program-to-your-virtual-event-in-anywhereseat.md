# How to Upload a Digital PDF Program to Your Virtual Event in AnywhereSeat

With AnywhereSeat, you can upload a PDF of your program/playbill that will appear alongside your event. Patrons can scroll through your digital program while they're watching your show.

When adding a new event, you'll find a section called "Digital Program". Here, click the **Select PDF** button and select it from your computer. Once you save your event, the program will be uploaded and scrollable.

If you have an existing event, simply click the **Options** button next to the event and select **Edit** in the dropdown. Scroll down to the "Digital Program" section and click the **Select PDF** button.
