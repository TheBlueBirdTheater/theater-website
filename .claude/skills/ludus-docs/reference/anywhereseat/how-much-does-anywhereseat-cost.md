# How Much Does AnywhereSeat Cost?

Let's break down what it costs to use AnywhereSeat! (The short answer is _almost nothing!_)

# Convenience Fee

We pass a **5% + $0.75 / ticket convenience fee** onto your patron.

Example: on a $10 purchase, the patron pays $11.25. We take the $1.25, cover credit card processing fees, and **you're left with the full $10**.

_This means there are no credit card processing fees you have to pay on top of our convenience fee._

There are no fees for $0 purchases.

It's also possible for you to absorb the fees instead of passing them on to your patrons. So, on a $10 purchase, your patrons would pay $10, and you'd receive $8.75.

# Payout Fees

When you request your money via your **Balances** dashboard in AnywhereSeat, we charge a $2 per payout fee. For example, if you request a $250 payout, we'll send you $248. This covers the fees we're charged for sending payments via paper check and direct deposit.

# Example

Let's say you sell 50 $10 tickets.

That's $500 in earned sales for you since the convenience fee is passed on to patrons.

When you request a payout, you'll receive $498 via paper check or direct deposit. There are _no additional credit card processing fees_ you have to worry about paying!

# Are there any costs associated with live streaming, video hosting, or other online events?

Selling tickets to your event on AnywhereSeat is free, but depending on how you decide to do your event, you may be looking at additional outside expenses. Here's a quick guide:

**Live streaming**

We can embed Vimeo Live Streams if you have a Vimeo Premium account. It is best to look at your options, but the rule of thumb with live streaming technology is "you get what you pay for."

**Other events**

If you plan on doing a Zoom event, you can get away with their free Basic tier if you plan on having an event of 40 minutes or less with 100 or fewer patrons. If you want to upgrade to a higher Zoom tier with additional options, you'll want to upgrade to a paid version.
