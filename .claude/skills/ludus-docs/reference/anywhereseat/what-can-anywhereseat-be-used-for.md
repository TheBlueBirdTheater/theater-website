# What Can AnywhereSeat Be Used For?

There are a number of exciting uses for AnywhereSeat!

Here are some ideas:

-   Live or pre-recorded performances (theatre, music, dance, etc.)

-   Exclusive music videos

-   Limited-access videos for your fans

-   Virtual art shows and classes

-   Online workshop access (Zoom, Google Meet, and more)

-   Virtual fundraising events

-   Sing-alongs

-   Exclusive looks behind the scenes

We support many services, such as:

-   Live streaming

-   Pre-recorded video uploads

-   Zoom

-   Google Meet

-   Other embeddable/linkable services
