# Can I purchase tickets to a stream outside of the US?

_**All AnywhereSeat purchases are processed in USD.**_

While it might be possible for you to purchase a streaming ticket, there is a possibility (depending on the country you are trying to stream from) that you may not be able to access the stream or video on the platform.
