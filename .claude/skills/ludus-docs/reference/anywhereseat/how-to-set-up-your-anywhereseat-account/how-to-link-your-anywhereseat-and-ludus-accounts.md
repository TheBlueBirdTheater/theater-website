# How to Link Your AnywhereSeat and Ludus Accounts

## Link Ludus with AnywhereSeat

1.  On your Ludus admin account, navigate to the Ludus at the top left corner. In that dropdown, you will select the **AnywhereSeat** tab.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/ekvqzMngM_l44lQ5_j__n_screenshot-2024-06-24-at-112333-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Once selected, you can click the button to link your AnywhereSeat and Ludus accounts.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/uNsFrrjGAQ1spffuSGssu_screenshot-2024-06-24-at-112458-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  At this point, you can sign into your AnywhereSeat account and that will link both your AnywhereSeat and Ludus accounts.
    
4.  Once your accounts are linked, patrons will see Virtual Tickets and In-Person ticket selections available on your patron site.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/kX-STNM4D1lbdmHpjtGbi_screenshot-2024-06-24-at-112719-am.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    If you have any issues connecting, please reach out to Customer Support via the chat widget (located in the lower right corner), and they will be happy to assist you.
    

Any event created on Ludus will not automatically create an event on AnywhereSeat (and vice versa). If you have an event that will be in-person and virtual, you will need to create it on both Ludus and AnywhereSeat.
