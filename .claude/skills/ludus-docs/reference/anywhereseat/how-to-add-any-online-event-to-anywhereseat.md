# How to Add Any Online Event to AnywhereSeat

It's easy to add any online event to AnywhereSeat, such as a Zoom or Google Meet meeting.

## To Add An Event:

1\. Click the **Add Event** button 2. Choose your type of event: _Other _3\. Enter your _Event Name_, _Description_ (optional), and _Cover Photo_ (optional) 4. Select your online event provider -> and enter the _Meeting ID_. To find your ID:

-   **Zoom**: once your meeting is created, copy the Meeting ID -> go back to AnywhereSeat, and _paste_.

-   **Google Meet**: once your meeting is scheduled, select the portion behind _meet.google.com_/your-unique-ID -> **Copy** the ID -> go back to AnywhereSeat, and _paste._ 

-   **Other**: Once your meeting is scheduled, select the full URL. If you need additional help, let us know! **Copy** it, and then go back to AnywhereSeat to _paste it_

5\. Under _Times_, select the _date(s)_ and _time(s)_ of the event 6. Enter the price of your event ($0 if you'd like it to be free) 7. Finally, click **Add Event**

For your AnywhereSeat events, there is no way to set Triggers to go live/turn off sales (as there is in Ludus). You will want to handle this manually.

That's it! You're ready to start selling tickets to your online event.
