# Payment Plans

A payment plan allows patrons to split the total cost of their purchase into multiple scheduled payments instead of paying in full at checkout for your [Classes](https://support.ludus.com/classes). These plans can be customized to fit your class and patron base as needed and applied to class fees and any add-ons.

Payment Plans are 5% + $1.25/payment.

Be sure to check out [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table).

When the Payment Plan is set up for a class, it will appear as an option for the patron, though they are not required to pay in increments; they can pay the entire fee up front.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/gGV8aWE2z09Fwwey6dyvk_screenshot-2026-08-03-at-33249-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

When applying a discount to an order with a payment plan, the discount will be distributed evenly across all of the payments.
