# Chargebacks and Disputes

With Ludus, you do not need to worry about chargebacks and disputes. Ludus covers _all_ chargebacks fees and disputes issued by the credit card companies.

Ludus does not notify you of chargebacks as these are handled directly by us where we will submit evidence if needed — out of sight, out of mind.
