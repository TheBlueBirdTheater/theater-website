# Why do I have a charge from LUDUS*

If you're checking your card statement and notice a charge from LUDUS\*, you've come to the right place!

Ludus is an online ticket sales program used by hundreds of K-12 schools, community theaters, and other performing arts organizations that sell tickets.

You would have received this charge for tickets you purchased through a Ludus ticketing portal. Think of a local school performance (theatre, band, orchestra, choir) or community theatre you, or someone who shares your card, would have attended.

This includes tickets, donations, season passes, and other payments.

If you can't remember, we'd be more than happy to look up your order information to verify the charge and what it was for. Simply email support@ludus.com with your request and we'll get back to you ASAP.
