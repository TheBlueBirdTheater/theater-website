# Washington State ASB Compliance

Ludus works with multiple school districts in the state of Washington following ASB guidelines, making Ludus an ASB-compliant ticketing vendor.

## PCI-compliant and certified

-   Ludus is [PCI compliant and certified](https://support.ludus.com/pci-compliance "PCI compliant and certified"), undergoing an annual audit verifying our compliance and security practices. We have an SAQ-D on file we can provide if needed.

## Daily deposits for events, made _intact_ to Washington State banks

-   All funds are immediately deposited to your processing account within Ludus and sent to your bank intact (ticket price + fees) on a daily basis. Each deposit comes with a detailed report breaking down by event, tickets, etc.

## Daily receipt/sales reconciliation data

-   All receipts/sales are kept track of in real-time, so a report can be generated at any time to reconcile sales data for any period of time. There is no delay in reporting or reconciliation.

## Invoicing

-   Since deposits are made intact, Ludus will invoice on a monthly or quarterly basis the fees owed to Ludus. Monthly or quarterly is determined based on your preference.

## An annual contract is available which specifically lays out the terms and pricing

-   While Ludus by default is a no lock-in service, an annual or multi-year contract can be signed by both parties laying out the terms and pricing for the product. We work with a few schools that require this, so we can simply send a contract over for your review when the time comes.

## No reserves are held

-   Ludus does not reserve or keep any funds on hold!

## HB1660 fee waivers and discounts

-   Create discount codes, passcode-protected ticket prices, memberships that unlock special reduced prices, or do in-person admin sales.
