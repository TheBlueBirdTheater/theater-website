# How do I remove my account?

To delete or remove your main Ludus account or credentials for the Ludus Access Control or Ludus Mobile Terminal apps, please reach out to our live chat support at [ludus.com](http://ludus.com/ "ludus.com") or send an email with the subject line "Ludus Account Deletion" to [support@ludus.com](mailto:support@ludus.com "support@ludus.com")
