# Ludus Onboarding Videos

Ludus offers the assistance of Onboarding videos. These can be incredibly helpful when setting up your account, as they will bring you through some of the many options available in Ludus.

To find the Onboarding videos, go to the "**?**" (top right) -> and select **Onboarding Videos**.

This will bring up those videos and you can view and use those at your leisure!
