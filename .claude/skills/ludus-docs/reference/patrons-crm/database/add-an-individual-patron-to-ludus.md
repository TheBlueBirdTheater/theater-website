# Add an Individual Patron to Ludus

To add a single patron (outside of [Import Patrons to Ludus](https://support.ludus.com/import-patrons-to-ludus) ) go to **Patrons** (at the top of your account) -> **Dashboard** -> **\+ Add Patron**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/1-YhMgpPrjQ76sc8LRBrZ-20260603-163555.png?format=webp "Document image")

From here you can add:

-   The patron's **First and Last Name** (required)

-   The patron's **Email** (required)

-   The patron's **Street Address** (optional)

-   The patron's **Phone Number** (optional)

-   An associated **Company** (optional)

-   **Patron Tags** (option)

-   Tags on your account will show as options, or you can choose to add new Tags for that patron.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Q3FkxJyBgK3NpiJhW3XER-20260603-163613.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once finished, be sure to select Save. That patron will then be included on your account patron database.
