# Patron History

The patron history tab, located on a patron's profile is where you can see a list of items related to the patron.

This information can be helpful if a patron has called about an order, or if you have questions about who made a change to the account. Or even if they received a marketing email.

The following action items are reported on the patron's history.:

-   **Cart Activity**:

-   **Added tickets to a cart**: Includes the number of tickets.

-   **Abandoned Cart:** Generally following an "Added Tickets to a cart" message. Indicates the purchase was not completed and that an email was sent to bring them back to create and complete an order

-   **Abandoned Success**: Indicates that the patron used the link in the Abandoned Cart Email to complete an order.

-   **Placed Order**: Indicates a successful order of an event, collection, or class was placed and how (Online vs. Box Office)

-   **Placed Pass Order**: shows the passcode and pass name as well as how purchased (Box Office / Online)

-   **Transferred items**: Lists when an item is transferred by the patron with old and new order ID along with the email of the patron it was transferred to.

-   **Donated**: Showing amount and how processed (Online vs. Box Office)

-   **Patron Profile Activity**:

-   **Edited Patron info**: indicates that the patron information was updated and by which Admin.

-   **Relationship Created**

-   **Relationship Edited**

-   **Relationship Deleted**

-   **Patron Merged**

-   **Edited Own Info**

-   **Membership related**:

-   **Received Membership Renewal**

-   **Received "Payment Info Needed" email for membership renewal**

-   **Marketing Items**:

-   **Subscribed/Unsubscribed:** Will appear any time the status is changed, and state if an admin made the change

-   **Received Email**: shows when a marketing or automation email was received by the patron and the name of the email.

-   **Opened Email:** includes the email subject and time.

-   **Clicked Link in Email**

-   **Bounced**: Lists when an email bounces.
