# Database

The Patron Database will provide you with a list of patrons and their corresponding information. By default, it will list _**all**_ patrons in your database.

The Database allows you to:

-   Manage duplicate patron records

-   Import or Add Patrons. Suggested Article: [Import Patrons to Ludus](https://support.ludus.com/import-patrons-to-ludus "Import Patrons to Ludus")

-   Search for patrons

-   Display Patrons based on Tags

-   View All Patrons or New ones based on the last purchase or Donation. (Past week, Past 30 or 60 days, or past year)

-   Display Patrons by Event purchased.

-   Print your filtered database report with only the columns selected

-   Export your filtered report to CSV with all available columns

-   Edit Columns: add or remove columns from the report.

### Edit Columns

The Edit Columns Button allows you to select which columns you wish to include in your patrons' report and the order in which those columns appear.

To add columns to the report, click the checkbox next to what you wish to have present.

To remove columns, uncheck the boxes or click the Delete button next to the column on the Selected Columns list.

In the Selected Columns area, you can click and hold to drag and drop the columns into the order you would like the report displayed, with the top being the first column and the bottom the last.

Click Apply to update the report.

## View Specific Types of Patron Reports

Using the instructions below, you can pull different types of reports on Patrons.

-   **Global Patrons Report** - To pull an account-wide Patrons report, click **Patrons** (from the top navigation bar) --> **Database**

-   **Event Patrons Report** - To pull an event-specific Patrons report, click the **Events** tab from the top navigation bar. Then, click **Reports** --> **Patrons** next to the desired Event.

-   **Collection Patrons Report** - To pull a collection-specific Patrons report, click **Collections** --> **Standard** from the top navigation bar. Then, click **Reports** --> **Patrons** next to the desired Collection.

-   **Class Patrons Report** - To pull a class-specific Patrons report, click **Collections** --> **Classes** from the top navigation bar. Then, click **Reports** --> **Patrons** next to the desired Class.

If you are viewing the Database Report, you can use the All Events drop-down menu to easily navigate to the patrons list for one or more specific events, classes, or collections.

### Terms and Definitions on the Patron's Report

Each row in a Patron Report represents a different patron. The columns will display whatever data you enable at the bottom of the page. See below for more details on each.

-   **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Company**, **Phone Number**, **Address**, **City**, **State**, **Zip Code** - The general contact information for the patron.

-   **Total Tickets** - The total number of tickets the patron has purchased.

-   **Total Add-Ons** - The total number of add-ons the patron has purchased.

-   **Total Passes** - The total number of passes the patron has purchased.

-   **Total Donations** - The total number of individual donations a patron has made.

-   **First Purchase** - The date of the patron's first purchase.

-   **Last Purchase** - The date of the patron's most recent purchase.

-   **First Donation** - The date of the patron's first donation.

-   **Last Donation** - The date of the patron's most recent donation.

-   **First Seen** - The date the patron first entered your account. This could be from a patron upload or the first transaction they made, such as a ticket purchase or donation.

-   **Last Seen** - The date the patron last interacted with your Ludus site.

-   **Notes** - The internal notes for a patron.

-   **Accommodations** - Any accessibility details that have been added to the patron's profile.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Collect accessibility notes during checkout](https://support.ludus.com/collect-accessibility-notes-during-checkout)

-   **Tags** - Any patron tags that have been added to a patron's profile**.** [Add patron tags](https://support.ludus.com/add-patron-tags)

-   **Status** - The marketing email subscription status for the patron. Subscribed or Unsubscribed.

-   **Alumni Year** - The alumni year associated with the patron (if applicable).

-   **Household** - The household to which the patron belongs.

-   **Custom Properties** - This will include any Custom Properties that have been created on your account.

-   _Suggested Article:_ [Properties](https://support.ludus.com/properties)
