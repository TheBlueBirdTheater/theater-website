# Audience filters

There are many filters within Ludus that allow you to target the users that you want to reach. Check out the full list below.

### Tickets

-   **Tickets Purchased** - The number of individual tickets that have been purchased

-   **Spent on Tickets** - The total amount of money spent on tickets

### Donations

-   **Number of Donations** - The number of donations that have been made

-   **Total Donated** - The total amount of money donated

-   **Recurring Donation Amount** - The total amount in each recurring donation

-   **Frequency of Donations** - The frequency of a recurring donation (i.e. - monthly, yearly, etc...)

-   **Last Donation Date** - A specific date of when a patron last donated (i.e. - before May 5, 2026).

-   **Donation Category** - Which donation category a patron donated to.

-   **Donor Level** - Which level a donor is currently at.

-   **1st Time Donor** - If a patron is a first-time donor (within a date range).

-   **Lapsed Donor** - If a recurring donor has lapsed.

### Add-Ons

-   **Add-Ons purchased** - The number of Add-Ons that have been purchased

-   **Spent on Add-Ons** - The total amount of money spent on Add-Ons

### Passes

-   **Passes purchased** - The number of individual Passes that have been purchased

-   **Spent on Passes** - The amount of money spent on Passes

-   **Passes held** - Whether or not a specific pass has been purchased

### Events

-   **Total events attended** - The number of individual events attended

-   **Total event times attended** - The number of individual event times attended

-   **Events attended** - Whether or not a specific event was attended

### Patron Info

-   **Patron tags** \- whether a patron carries a specific tag.

-   _Suggested Article_: [Add patron tags](https://support.ludus.com/add-patron-tags)

### Marketing

-   **Mailing Lists** - Whether or not a patron is subscribed to a Mailing List

Filters based on a numerical value can be sorted by "greater than", "less than", "equals", or "does not equal".

Filters based on a yes or no binary (such as Events Attended) can sorted by "are", or "are not".
