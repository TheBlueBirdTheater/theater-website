# Unsubscribing from Emails

Patrons can choose to unsubscribe/opt out of emails that are sent through Ludus. This is broken into 3 sections, which they can opt out of:

-   **Event Announcements**

-   Event Messages

-   Event Time Messages

-   **Fundraiser Updates**

-   Campaign Updates

-   **Marketing Communication**

-   Marketing Campaign

-   Marketing Automation

### Unsubscribe from the Admin Panel

1.  Go to the **Search Bar** (magnifying glass) OR the **Patron** tab (at the top of your account).
    
2.  From here, you can enter and search for the patron to pull up their **Patron Profile**.
    
3.  Scroll to the bottom left portion of their profile and find **Email Subscriptions**:
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/bMUK4RjfH5KfAUPbOkNcA-20260330-155331.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  From here, you can unsubscribe them from all 3 email sections.
    
    By default, they will be subscribed to all 3 when they sign up for their account.
    

### Unsubscribe from Patron Site

1.  If a patron wishes to unsubscribe, they can go to your Ludus patron site (i.e., "mysubdomain".ludus.com) and click My Account in the top-right corner.
    
2.  From here, they can log in to their patron profile by entering their email and receiving their login link:
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/q_e_ODiMDyjDDTAX6BCp8-20260325-155134.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Once they select View my account, they will be able to scroll near the bottom to unsubscribe/subscribe to a mailing list or email communication they wish.
    

### Tags and Unsubscribing

While a patron will still have a Tag attached to their profile, they will NOT be included in the tagged audience when a Marketing email is sent if they choose to unsubscribe from those emails.

So if you have 25 patrons in a tagged audience and one opts out, only 24 patrons will receive that email.
