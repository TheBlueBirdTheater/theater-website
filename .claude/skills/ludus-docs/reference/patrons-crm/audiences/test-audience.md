# Test Audience

While you can always preview your email within the [Email Design Tool](https://support.ludus.com/email-design-tool), you might want to send out a test version before sending it to all of your patrons.

_Suggested Article_: [Send a test email](https://support.ludus.com/send-a-test-email).

To add email addresses to your recipient list for test emails, follow the steps below.

1.  Navigate to **Patrons** --> **Audiences**.
    
2.  Next to "Test Audience," click **View**/**Edit**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/j61z-HKqxNdSFKjjxDwmL_screenshot-2026-08-03-at-123318-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click **Add Emails**.
    
4.  Type in the email address(es) that you'd like to add to your test Audience. (_Note_: add a comma or hit enter between addresses)
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/WmjVKaj-sfJBbAks5a_dp_screenshot-2026-08-07-at-30522-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
5.  Click **Add** to save the address(es) to your list.
