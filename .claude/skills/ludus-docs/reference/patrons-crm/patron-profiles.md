# Patron Profiles

Every time a patron interacts with your Ludus — buys a ticket, registers for a class, makes a donation, reads an email, etc. — we build a profile on all of their activity and history.

The patron profile gives you a glimpse at an individual and allows you to edit their info, add private notes, create relationships with other patrons (spouse, parent, friend, arch-nemesis, etc.), attach custom tags (donor, VIP, volunteer, etc.), and more.

Managing patrons is an important aspect of Ludus, allowing you to set patron tags, add private notes, see patron activity, merge profiles, and more.

# Information available on the Patron Profile

Patron profiles include patron details on the left-side panel, as well as 3 pages to the right of the main display. These include the Overview, Orders, and History pages.

## Patron Details

The main details for a patron are listed on the left side panel of a patron profile. These items will always be visible when viewing a patron's profile.

There are two blocks of information here that can be edited. These blocks are separated by a divider. On top is the name field, and then below that is additional patron information. To edit, hover your mouse over the name or additional information, and an edit icon will appear; click it to open the editor.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/f0Vo_YqIjHJFYSZHt6OlE_screenshot-2026-06-26-at-34206-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

When you click the patron's email on the profile, it opens **Mail To** on the computer. This will not always open the Mail Client (depending on your browser). Additionally, you can always right-click and copy and paste for later use if easier too.

## The Overview Page

The overview page is where you will find important information about the patron, including a breakdown of spending.

### Active Memberships

All active memberships will appear in this area. Recurring memberships will show their status (Valid Until, Due, or Past Due).

Non-recurring memberships will display a message indicating how long they have been a member.

Clicking on a membership here will take you to the membership screen, where you can view payment information and renew. You can also view the amount received to date for the membership.

### Spending

Spending provides a breakdown of the spending totals for the patron, broken down by Tickets, Collections, Classes, Passes, Add-Ons, and Donations.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-k9fzCEL1j5nv9C4p5SDn8-20240927-151250.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Transactions

This area contains the dates when the patron was First Seen, as well as the dates of their First and Last Transactions.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/ZYdqFEbtTGo_h59dKFu8Y_ludus-manual-admin-2024-07-17t083758759.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Tags

Tags are an additional way to segment your patrons. You can add as many tags as you'd like to a patron. For example, you could add the tag "VIP" to important patrons or "Mega Donor" to patrons who have historically donated large amounts. These tags can be used in our [Marketing](https://support.ludus.com/marketing) feature to help sort your [Audiences](7Hl0rjmxRIgdirvYXOR5n)

To add a new tag, simply begin typing in the text box provided and hit Enter to save. To remove a tag, click the X next to the existing tag in blue.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/-KsErVXy023P_ssvg-EoI_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-115230-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Relationships

This area highlights any relationships the patron has with other patrons or students in your database. To add a new relationship, click the green **+Relationship** button. Search for the patron and choose the relationship.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fQTHIZgr5bpUokfswTiHH_screen-shot-2023-09-06-at-114735-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Mailing

If you are utilizing [Mailing Lists](https://support.ludus.com/mailing-lists) in our Marketing feature, their subscriptions will appear here.

## The Orders Page

Here you will find, just as the name implies, a list of all orders the patron has made, broken down by category.

The categories in order are:

-   **Tickets**: Including Collections

-   **Donations**

-   **Passes**

-   **Pass Renewals** (If you use this feature)

-   **Invoices** (if you use Invoices)

-   **Transfers**

-   **Gift Cards** (If active on your account)

-   **Class Registration** (if used on your account)

## The Activities Page

The Activities page is the home for Tasks related to the patron as well as a center to log activity with your patrons.

Here, any tasks that are created and assigned will appear, but you can also use this area to log things. As an example, if Peter Parker calls the box office and you would like to have a note of the discussion, you can go to the Activity page while you are talking to them and click **Log Activity**.

This will open a window in which you can enter information about the phone call. The date and time fields will auto-populate with the current date and time. You can then set the outcome of the call, as well as whether it was an incoming call or an outgoing call, and make any notes you would like to add.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-PviSSXkOcEve-zp6MHiOC-20240814-200631.png?format=webp "Document image")

When completed, simply click **Create** to add this call to the activity page.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-zKLznSfrHRT8yn40qGwFE-20240814-200821.png?format=webp "Document image")

On this page, you can log the following items:

-   **To-Do**

-   **Calls**

-   **Emails**

-   **Meetings**

-   **Gifts**

-   **Letters**

## The History Page

This page includes historical activity for the patron. This includes online purchases, admin panel sales, abandoned carts, subscription actions, and such.
