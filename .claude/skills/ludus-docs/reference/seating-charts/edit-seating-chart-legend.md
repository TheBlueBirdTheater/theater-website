# Seating Chart Legend

We understand that sometimes a generic label for seating type doesn't match your ticket price, and can cause confusion among patrons. Luckily, we've made this easy to change within the _Seating Chart Editor._

### Change Legend Labels

Select the **More** dropdown → choose **Seating Charts** → and click **Edit** beside your seating chart. Then, within the _Legend_, click the **Pencil** button (on the far right). Enter the new labels for your seating types, and be sure to **Save** when done. Once saved your updated Labels will appear on the patron and admin side for that specific seating chart!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/O59IiLmF12ujLJV3kDm9H_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-07t140708039.png?format=webp "Document image")

It is important not to use special characters such as "&" in your legend. Saving a legend with these types of characters will result in an error. If this happens, the chart can be recovered from the last saved version by using Recovery, located under the 3 dots on the seating chart page.
