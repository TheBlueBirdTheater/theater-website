# Changing the Color of Tiered Seating

If you need to adjust your tiered seating, it is possible to easily update seats to a different tier.

### Change the color of your seats

1.  Open the chart that you need to edit. (**More**, on the top navigation bar, then **Seat Charts ->** Click **Edit**.

2.  From the toolbar, click on the Chair Icon and select the color you want your seats to be from the drop-down.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/NNEQE_6NyMZ1xw4deCI4r-20260330-161440.png?format=webp "Document image")

3\. Click on the seats you wish to change to your selected color.

If you wish to remove a tier color and restore a seat to its normal Green color. You will want to select the color of the seat that it is currently showing as.

i.e., to restore a Blue seat to Green, select Blue. Then click on the seat to restore it.

4\. Repeat as needed to change the color.

If you need to change many seat colors, you can click on the first seat, then press and hold Shift, and click the last seat in the section or row to change. This will change everything within the section between the first and last seats.
