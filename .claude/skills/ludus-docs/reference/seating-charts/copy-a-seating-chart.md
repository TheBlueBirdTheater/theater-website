# Copy a Seating Chart

You may need to duplicate your seating chart to edit for a specific event. This can be useful if you want to use the same chart but add tiered seating or perhaps have a smaller section of seats available. You have the ability to quickly make a copy of your seating chart.

## Steps to Copy a Seating Chart

1.  Navigate to your seating chart by selecting **More** _**→**_ **Seating Charts.**

2.  Click '**Copy**' next to the seating chart you want to duplicate.

3.  Enter the '**Chart Name**' you would like the chart to have or you can use the name that is already entered in the space.

4.  Click '**Create Seating Chart**' to save a copy of your chart.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/QMgdWjkCIWvO3QKth7LgU_ludus-manual-admin-2024-06-10t082147634.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once a seating chart is copied you will have access to editing that chart and attaching it to any of the events on your account.
