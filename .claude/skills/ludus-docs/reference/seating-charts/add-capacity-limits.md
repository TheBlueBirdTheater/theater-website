# Capacity Limits

If you have capacity restrictions you need to follow, or perhaps you only want a certain number of seats available, you can easily accomplish this through the **Capacity Limit** function in Ludus!

## Add a Capacity Limit

1.  Go to **More** → **Seating Charts** in your top navigation.
    
2.  Click the **Options** button next to your seating chart and select **Capacity Limit** in the dropdown.
    
3.  Enter your capacity limit.
    
    For example, if you can only have 100 people in your space, enter 100.
    
4.  **Save Changes** when finished, and your **Capacity Limit** will be set!
    

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/mN-6ZhmimKpdSHmqsEqP9_image.png?format=webp "Document image")

This will override the actual seat count of your auditorium. Once your capacity limit is reached for a performance, that performance will be marked as sold out.
