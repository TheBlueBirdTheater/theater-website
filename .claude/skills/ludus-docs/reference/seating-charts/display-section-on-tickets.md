# Display Section on Ticket

If you would like the name of the seating chart section visible on your patrons' tickets instead of just the seat number, you can adjust that setting in your seating chart. This can be particularly helpful if you have a large auditorium or complex seating and you would like patrons to know precisely where their section is located!

## Display the Section name on tickets for patrons

1.  Select the **More** dropdown in the upper right-hand corner and click **Seating Charts**.

2.  Click the **Options** dropdown to the right of the seating chart you would like to edit and select **Settings**.

3.  Change the **Section on Ticket** to _Yes,_ and be sure to **Save Changes**!

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/b9Aet_soVASoNNr48XYx__image.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once saved, the section will be reflected on the patron's ticket!
