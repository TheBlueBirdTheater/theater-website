# Delete a Seating Chart

If your account has an outdated chart or if you made a duplicate of the wrong chart, it's simple to delete it in Ludus!

## Steps to Delete a Seating Chart

1.  Navigate to your seating chart by selecting **More** _**→**_ **Seating Charts.**

2.  Click **Edit** on the seating chart you wish to delete.

3.  In the dropdown, you will want to select "**Delete**" for the seating chart that needs to be removed.

4.  Once selected, a pop-up will prompt you to confirm that this deletion is correct. Click "**OK**" to approve deleting the chart.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/NMG57d9b-GU07OCe7VeZ6_fuqlzipmdr.png?format=webp "Document image")

Please be sure to verify that it is the correct chart before deleting, as it will remove the chart from your seating chart inventory completely (this is not archiving the chart).
