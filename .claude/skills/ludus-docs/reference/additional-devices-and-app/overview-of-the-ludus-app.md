# Overview of the Ludus App

## Overview

In addition to your Ludus Admin Panel, we have a mobile app available for download on iOS and Android devices. These are optional tools that make things even easier for both you and your patrons.

Patrons themselves **never** have to download an app to complete their purchase. These apps are solely for admins to operate the day-of box office functionality.

## What does the app do?

The Ludus app has three main functions:

-   **Ticket Scanning/Check-In** - Instead of needing to purchase any additional ticket scanners, you can turn any mobile device (such as a phone or tablet) into a top-tier ticket scanning tool. Utilize the camera on the device or our Check-In list to make verifying tickets a breeze and speed up your lines

-   **Connecting to Mobile Reader** - While there is no additional equipment needed to make a sale within Ludus, we do offer additional equipment to help speed up your in-person box office sales and increase the variety of ways your patrons can pay for a purchase. Our [Mobile Readers](https://support.ludus.com/mobile-readers) utilize the mobile app to create a secure connection for your in-person sales.

-   **Retail** - Retail is exclusive to the App, and through this function you can process sales for other items (listed in Retail). To learn more about this feature, check out [Concessions](https://support.ludus.com/concessions) or reach out to Customer Support with any questions.
