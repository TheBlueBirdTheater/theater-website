# Boca Printer

# Boca Printing

Having quality, professional tickets that you can give to patrons when they purchase tickets at your box office is always a nice option. Fortunately, Ludus makes thermal printing easy and fast!

If you need to purchase a thermal printer, here are the options we recommend:

-   **Boca Lemur-S** — high-grade, fast printing.

These machines are used and trusted by many professional theaters. You can purchase directly from Boca at [BocaSystems.com](https://bocasystems.com/ "BocaSystems.com"). (**Don't forget to ask for the Ludus discount to receive ~18% off.**)

To purchase thermal ticket stock from Ludus, you can do so under **More** _**→**_ **Supplies Shop**.

## Thermal Printing Is Not A Requirement

If you're unable to buy a thermal printer, no worries! By default, you can print tickets to normal 8.5 x 11 sheets of paper. We also have a "blank tickets" feature that allows you to pre-print any quantity of blank tickets with your event information if printing at your box office is not a viable option.

Aside from a computer with an internet connection and a web browser, there is no required equipment to use Ludus!
