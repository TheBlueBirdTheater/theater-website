# How do I clean my Boca Out?

If you are printing via a Boca printer, you may encounter an issue where multiple blank tickets print after actual tickets print. This is most likely due to the dust buildup from cutting the tickets. This build-up can cause the sensors to not print or cut tickets correctly.

### Options for Cleaning the Boca Sensor

1.  The Boca Manual - [**Boca Lemur Maintenance**](https://www.bocasystems.com/documents/LemurMaintenance.pdf "Boca Lemur Maintenance").
    
    The Boca Lemur Manual will go through steps and troubleshooting to clean and maintain your Boca Printer.
    
2.  Boca Cleaning Video
    
    This video will bring you through how to clean and the best procedure to clean it.
    

If you are in a time crunch and need it cleaned fast, an air can (like used to clean out a keyboard) can be used to quickly blow out any dust that might be causing issues with the sensor.
