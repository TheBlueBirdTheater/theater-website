# DYMO Printer

# DYMO Printing

DYMO printers are supported but not recommended if you're looking to purchase a new thermal printer.

Having quality, professional tickets to give to patrons when they purchase tickets at your box office is always a nice option. Fortunately, Ludus makes thermal printing easy and fast!

Ludus works with the DYMO 450 printer.

Newer models, such as the Dymo 550, are not compatible due to changes to the printer requiring that Dymo brand paper usage be enforced via RFID

If you'd like to enable thermal printing for your DYMO, navigate to **Settings** (⚙️ in your top navigation) **→ Advanced →** "DYMO Printing".

## Thermal Printing Is Not A Requirement

If you're unable to buy a thermal printer, no worries! By default, you can print tickets to normal 8.5 x 11 sheets of paper. We also have a "blank tickets" feature that allows you to pre-print any quantity of blank tickets with your event information if printing at your box office is not a viable option.

Remember, aside from a computer with an internet connection and web browser, there is no required equipment to use Ludus!
