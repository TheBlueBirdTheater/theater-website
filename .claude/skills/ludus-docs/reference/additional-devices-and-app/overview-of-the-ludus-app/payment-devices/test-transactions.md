# Test Transactions

You should not perform an actual transaction to test your terminals, as the Ludus fees are not refundable. To this end, we have a test procedure in place.

## Test your reader

Once you have paired and connected your mobile reader or terminal, you can use the following to create a test transaction to verify communication between the selling computer and the card reader.

The following directions will bring you through how to test your reader.

1.  Navigate to **More** on the top navigation bar, and select **Mobile Apps**

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Adk_WjTwrJDTcuP11dcsq_ludus-manual-admin-10.png?format=webp "Document image")

2\. Scroll down to the section labeled **Test Mobile Payment Terminals**

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/t2Q9oj4bBV46NsXGuI2ZT_ludus-manual-admin-12.png?format=webp "Document image")

3\. Click on **Test Payments.** This will send a $0.50 payment to your card reader.

Ensure that your reader appears below the blue "Send to Terminal App" button. Or click **Change** to switch to a different reader.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/GNVin98R2uw2GivxcLxGc_ludus-manual-admin-13.png?format=webp "Document image")

4\. You will see Waiting for payment on your selling computer, and in the **Terminal** or **Mobile Reader App,** you will see the amount come up to take payment.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/7iuHPXuJBs10GD-Una9bh_ludus-manual-admin-14.png?format=webp "Document image")

5\. Insert a card into the chip reader or use Apple/Google Pay to make a payment.

6\. The payment will be accepted, and you will see the confirmation on your selling computer.

Once the charge is accepted, it will automatically be refunded right away.

There is a limit of 2 test transactions. If you use both and then need more, you can reach out to customer support and ask for more to be added.
