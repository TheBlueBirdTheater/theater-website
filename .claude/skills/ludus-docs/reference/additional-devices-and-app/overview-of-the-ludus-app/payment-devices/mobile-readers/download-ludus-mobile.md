# Download Ludus Mobile

To use the Mobile Reader, you’ll need a mobile device (like a phone or tablet) with our app installed and a computer with the Ludus website open, where you’ll enter sales. Your mobile device acts as the communication device between the Mobile Reader and your computer or laptop. There are two apps depending on if you are using an Android or iOS device. Click the drop-down that corresponds with your device type below.

Our team is dedicated to ensuring top-notch functionality on our app. Consequently, there may be updates available for download on the app. If you are experiencing any difficulties, first ensure that you are using the most updated version of the app.

## iOS Devices (Ludus Mobile)

From the App Store on iOS devices, we have an app titled "Ludus Box Office" available for download. This app combines both the ticket scanning tool and mobile reader into one seamless app.

[Open in App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ludus-mobile-terminal/id1670171827)
