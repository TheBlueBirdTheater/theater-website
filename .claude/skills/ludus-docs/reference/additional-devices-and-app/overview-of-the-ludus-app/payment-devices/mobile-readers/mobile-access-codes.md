# Mobile Access Codes

## What are mobile access codes?

We recognize that many programs have volunteers and non-administrator staff working the box office and ticket sales. We also realize that remembering complex usernames, passwords, and organization subdomains is not always easy! Therefore, we have designed a secure login feature that will allow your various Ludus admins to access a temporary access code and account secret for a seamless and simple mobile login.

## How to find mobile access codes?

If your organization is actively using the mobile terminal, you will have an icon in your Ludus Admin dashboard located under **More**, on the top navigation bar that resembles a mobile phone in the upper right-hand corner. Click the icon.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/UruWJn3ysCN76i4VMwrKl_ludus-manual-admin-10.png?format=webp "Document image")

Then, scroll to the section titled "Logging in and Access Codes". Use these codes during the login process for your mobile app ([Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-mobile) or [Ludus Mobile Terminal (Android)](BP20OC8qVe2QJ9blVP7c8))

## FAQs

### When do the 24 Hour codes reset?

These codes will reset automatically every 24 hours. Anyone who is logged in using one of those 24-hour mobile access codes will not be automatically logged out when the code resets.
