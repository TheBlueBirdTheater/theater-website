# Pair the Mobile Reader with the App

Check out the corresponding drop-down menu depending on whether you are using an iOS or Android device.

We highly recommend pairing the reader while it is plugged in.

Once a mobile reader has been paired, it can be set aside. You do not need to click anything on the mobile app to process a sale with the Mobile Reader.

## Pair the Mobile Reader to an iOS Device

Once you have logged into the [Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-mobile) app, you are ready to pair. To do so:

1.  Before you begin to pair, make sure your reader is charged, turned on, and close to your mobile device.
    
2.  Click **Pair With Reader**.
    
3.  From the page of available readers, click **Connect** for the desired reader.
    

## Pair the Mobile Reader to an Android Device

Once you have logged into the [Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-mobile) app, you are ready to pair. To do so:

1.  Before you begin to pair, make sure your reader is fully charged, turned on, and close to your mobile device.
    
2.  Click **Pair With Reader**.
    
    You will be prompted to pair with the reader. Click **Pair & Connect** for the first reader to be added to your account. You do NOT need to allow access to your contacts and call history.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/W6WdyFZWQ_Rat9KqcYwiF_image-3.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    If you are pairing other readers, click **Pair** after the first reader is connected.
    
3.  From the page of available readers, click **Connect** for the desired reader.
