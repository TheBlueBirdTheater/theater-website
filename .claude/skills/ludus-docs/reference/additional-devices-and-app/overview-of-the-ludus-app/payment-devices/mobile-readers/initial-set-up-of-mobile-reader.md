# Initial Set Up of Mobile Reader

## Overview

This article will walk through everything you need to know to collect payments with a mobile reader. From unboxing to accepting payments in person, each step is outlined below. Some steps may include additional information you'd like to read up on. To dive deeper into a particular step and learn about the FAQs and troubleshooting, click the "_Suggested Article_" for the step.

# Instructions

1.  ### [](#unbox-your-device)Unbox your Device
    
    Once you have received your Mobile Reader from Ludus, you will want to unbox and charge the reader. There is a charging cable in the box with your reader.
    
    Charging your device is important for proper setup and functionality.
    
2.  ### [](#add-mobile-reader-to-your-admin-panel)Add Mobile Reader to your Admin Panel
    
    You will need to add your Mobile Reader(s) to your admin panel. This is done by clicking the **Settings wheel icon** in the upper right-hand corner and navigating to the **Payments** tab.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Add payment device](https://support.ludus.com/add-payment-device)
    
3.  ### [](#download-the-ludus-app-for-your-mobile-reader)Download the Ludus app for your Mobile Reader
    
    To use the Mobile Reader, you’ll need a mobile device (like a phone or tablet) with our app installed and a computer with the Ludus website pulled up, where you’ll enter sales. Your mobile device acts as the communication device between the Mobile Reader and your computer or laptop. There are two apps, depending on whether you are using an Android or iOS device.
    
    iOS & Android: "Ludus Box Office"
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Download Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/download-ludus-mobile)
    
4.  ### [](#log-in-to-the-app)Log in to the app
    
    Once downloaded, log in to the app using your subdomain, username, and password.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Log in to Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/log-in-to-ludus-mobile)
    
5.  ### [](#pair-the-reader-with-your-app)Pair the reader with your app
    
    You are now ready to create the connection between your mobile reader, mobile device, and computer. On the app, click **Pair With Reader**. Then, next to the desired reader, click **Connect**.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Pair the Mobile Reader with the App](https://support.ludus.com/pair-the-mobile-reader-with-the-app)
    
6.  ### [](#sell-tickets)Sell tickets!
    
    Once you have successfully paired the Mobile Reader, you are ready to start accepting payments from your admin panel. Simply create a new order like you normally would. Then, from the checkout screen, click **Credit** --> **Send to Terminal** from the pop-up menu.
    
    _Suggested Article_: [Sell tickets to an Event](https://support.ludus.com/sell-tickets-to-an-event)
