# Standard Terminal S710

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/SaiPZMp7qkLlGTCvEsX_6_screenshot-2026-08-18-at-92108-am.png?format=webp "Document image")

The Standard Terminal S710 is a smart, all-in-one payment terminal designed for both countertop and mobile use. Featuring a 5.5-inch touchscreen, the S710 accepts tap, chip, and swipe payments, including Apple Pay, Google Pay, and other digital wallets. It connects via Wi-Fi or built-in 4G/LTE cellular service, making it ideal for venues that need reliable connectivity both indoors and on the go.

The device runs on Android 10 and offers a long-lasting battery with up to 15 hours of active use.

The Standard Terminal S710 only works with Ludus — 3rd party payment terminals are not supported! Must be paired with a laptop, desktop, or tablet logged into your Ludus admin panel.
