# Reports Tab (Ludus Mobile)

The **Reports** tab (found at the bottom of the app), can be used to get a quick view of sales and reports specific to your events.

Users with Seller access will not be able to view Reports.

_Suggested Article:_ [User Types - Breakdown](https://support.ludus.com/user-types-breakdown)

# What does the Reports tab do?

Within the Reports tab, you can get a quick glimpse at how ticket sales are going for your event(s).

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/S1l7NUvX7xhH6itWxDG-P_img-1064.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

-   **Sales Stats** (overview of sales either over the past week or all sales)

-   **Tickets Sold**

-   **Total Sales**

-   **Total Gross** (sales)

-   **Sales Breakdown** (where tickets are being purchased)

-   **Seats Left** (total seats left for the entire event)

-   **Event Times** (breakdown of seats still available and those sold)
