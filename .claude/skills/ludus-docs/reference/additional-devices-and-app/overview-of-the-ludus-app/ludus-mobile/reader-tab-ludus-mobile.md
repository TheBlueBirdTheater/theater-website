# Reader Tab (Ludus Mobile)

The reader tab is the place where you can connect to one of your [Mobile Readers](https://support.ludus.com/mobile-readers). These are optional pieces of equipment used to take payment via card swipe, chip, insert, tap-to-pay, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

From the reader tab, you have two options: **Pair With Reader** and **Tutorial**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/jQojCjVILoq6M7jy1Wmyp_img-1065.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

### Pair With Reader

The **Pair With Reader** tab is a fast and simple way to connect with your Mobile Reader. Once your mobile reader is charged, turned on, and within range, simply tap **Pair With Reader** and click **Connect** on the subsequent page.

_Suggested Article_: [Pair the Mobile Reader with the App](https://support.ludus.com/pair-the-mobile-reader-with-the-app)

### Tutorial

If you'd like a visual walkthrough of the steps you need to take on your admin panel to send a payment to the Mobile Reader, click **Tutorial**.
