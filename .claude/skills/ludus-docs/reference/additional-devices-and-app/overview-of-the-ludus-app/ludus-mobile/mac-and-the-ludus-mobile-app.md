# Mac and the Ludus Mobile App

If you do not have access to a mobile device to use with your [Mobile Readers](https://support.ludus.com/mobile-readers) , you can also add the app to your MacBook.

If you have not yet set up your Ludus account for the Mobile Reader, please see [Initial Set Up of Mobile Reader](https://support.ludus.com/initial-set-up-of-mobile-reader) .

To add the app to your Mac:

1.  Go to the App Store (found on your device).
    
2.  Search for the [Ludus Mobile](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-mobile) App and select **Get** and then **Install** to download that app.
    
3.  After the App Store app is added to your Mac, you can open and sign in (as if you were on a mobile device).
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-1452Q66dXD0fbyTQqfFEY-20250219-150804.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Once logged in, you will be prompted to turn on Bluetooth and Location Settings. Both are needed for the app to work.
    
5.  From here, you will be able to:
    
    -   Pair your Mobile Reader
    
    -   View a Tutorial
    
    -   Select Menu (at the top) While the "**Scanner**" tab (at the top) is present, this is not accessible via your Mac.
    
    While the "**Scanner**" tab (at the top) is present, this is not accessible via your Mac.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-ptWKdxHPrUdXubijwTFDA-20250219-161912.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
6.  To pair your Reader, simply select "Pair Reader" to connect to your Mobile Reader.
    
    Once selected, you will see your Nearby Readers which are listed to connect to.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-JLw67O1MxS44IeSPIfztr-20250219-165348.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    Ensure that your Reader is fully charged and turned on when you go to pair it. See [Mobile Readers](https://support.ludus.com/mobile-readers) for more information on what it looks/sounds like when the reader is on.
    
7.  Once your Mobile Reader is paired, you will see the following screen where you can select to Collect Payments:
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-I8Imm71hyVZpd61AFjpo8-20250219-170420.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    Once selected, your Mobile Reader will be ready to accept transactions.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-RRpAg2RrI-rDKl5k_h48Q-20250219-170527.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
8.  Once your Mobile Reader is paired, you can go to **New Order** (or Box Office), next to the **Event** -> **Select** the items for purchase -> at the checkout screen, select **Credit** and **Send to Terminal App**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-mTnTaDeyvRUlmayblYV5f-20250219-170638.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
9.  When that purchase is sent to the "**Terminal App**" you will see that pop-up on your App Screen.
    
    At this point, you can tap, swipe, or insert the chip to collect the payment.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-pcjnkOz1M6hTZtF_c6kRA-20250219-170816.png?format=webp "Document image")
