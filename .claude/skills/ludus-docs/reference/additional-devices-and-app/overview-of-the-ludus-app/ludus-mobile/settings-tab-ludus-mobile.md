# Settings Tab (Ludus Mobile)

In the Ludus Box Office app, the tabs at the bottom of the screen allow you to navigate through the different functions the app has. To view settings and general info, use the **Settings** tab.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/0keYKGK7XcP1jjpZ2rBZg_img-1066.jpg?format=webp "Document image")

# What does the Settings tab do?

Within the **Settings**, you are able to access a few things:

-   **Bluetooth Settings** (set up your Mobile Reader)

-   _Suggested Article:_ [Pair the Mobile Reader with the App](https://support.ludus.com/pair-the-mobile-reader-with-the-app)

-   **About Us** (links to Ludus.com and provides additional information)

-   **Privacy Policy** (the Ludus Privacy Policy)

-   **Terms of Service** (Ludus Terms of Service)

-   **Log Out** (log out of the app)
