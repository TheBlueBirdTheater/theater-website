# Donation Categories

Donation Categories allow patrons to route their donations to different areas within your organization. For example, a theatre group has a pool of funds for summer programming, a pool for lighting and sound equipment, and one for general funds. They could set up different Donation categories and patrons will able to choose where they would like their donation to go.
