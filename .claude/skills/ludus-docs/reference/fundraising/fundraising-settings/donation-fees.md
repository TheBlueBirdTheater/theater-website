# Donation Fees

Donations in Ludus do not carry the standard 5% + $0.75 fee that other sales do.

Fees for donations are charged as follows:

-   3.5% + $0.50 per item for donations over $3.00.

-   $0.50 for any donation of $3.00 or under.

-   As always, fees are only charged for credit card transactions, and there are no fees for recording Cash or Check donations in Ludus.

-   Donations made by the Fundraising pages allow the patron to elect to cover the processing fees.

-   Donations made as an add-on to an order will absorb the fees, and there is no option to cover them.

Fees for donations are not passed on as required by legislation. Instead, your patrons have the opportunity to opt in to covering the fees.

For a full breakdown of all Ludus fees, please visit the [Ludus Pricing Table](https://support.ludus.com/ludus-pricing-table "Ludus Pricing Table").
