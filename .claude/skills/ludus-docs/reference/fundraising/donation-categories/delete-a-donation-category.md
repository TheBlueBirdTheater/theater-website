# Delete a Donation Category

To delete a current donation category:

1.  Navigate to your categories by clicking **Fundraising** --> **Categories** form the top navigation bar.
    
2.  Next to the desired category, click **Delete**.
    

If a category is deleted, all previous donations with that category selected will remain listed on your donation reports.
