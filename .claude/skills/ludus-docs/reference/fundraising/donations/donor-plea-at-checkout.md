# Donor Plea at Checkout

A donor plea is a way to ask for donations from individuals or organizations that will appear on the checkout screen when events, collections, classes, or passes are purchased.

The donor plea can be created within the **Fundraising** section on the Settings page. Your message can be crafted to read exactly how you want it to appear to your patrons. This can be a simple message, but it can also include images or video if you would like. The goal of the plea is to inspire potential donors to contribute to your programs by letting them know how they can help support or partner with your program.

### Adding the Donor Plea at Checkout

1.  Click **Fundraising** on the top navigation bar and then select **Settings**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-odTQUygSUPL991Jd2p_xD-20240910-125850.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  On the Settings page, scroll down to the Donor Plea at Checkout field to enter your message. You can use formatted text, images, and video if desired.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/y_yS_sVZWor_q35Q4f09J-AE1iHL0l0F17wEHVjdJOb-20240910-130108.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the Settings page**.**
    

Your donor plea will now appear at checkout, provided **Allow Donations** is set to yes on the Fundraising Settings page.
