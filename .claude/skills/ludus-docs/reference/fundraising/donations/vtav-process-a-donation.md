# Process a donation

Whether you have a patron call in or send in a check/cash, you might need to manually enter that donation from your admin panel. Luckily, this is super simple to do within Ludus.

### Process an Admin Donation

1.  Navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Donations**
    
2.  Click **New Donation**
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/MRLsKH4Bx02fK_fBoZt8R-20260408-144949.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Choose which type of payment you will process (Use other for any custom payment methods you have set up.
    
    Additionally, if the patron wishes to set up a recurring donation you can set that up as:
    
    -   One-Time
    
    -   Monthly
    
    -   Quarterly
    
    -   Yearly
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fNq9KE1lZMAHSE09jHEMv-20260610-145407.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    _Suggested Articles:_
    
    [Add Other payment options](https://support.ludus.com/add-other-payment-options)
    
    [Recurring Donations](https://support.ludus.com/recurring-donations)
    
4.  Fill out any patron information and click **Proceed to Payment or Collect** if it is cash, check, etc.
    
    The memo field, if used, will appear in the patron's email confirmation.
    

As an admin, you can also choose whether to send a confirmation email or to allow the patron to cover processing fees (for credit transactions only). To determine, select the checkboxes at the bottom of the form.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/UeEAx6ZQ3xuc4Z_I2gBMz-20251218-211936.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Process a Donation from the Patron Site

When patrons process a donation, under the donations tab, they will simply select the donation amount (whether under **Make a Donation**, **Individual Donor Levels**, or a **Fundraiser**).

When making a donation, they can also easily choose whether to give once, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/pS5qgX5eypHkeVCs4HrtG_screenshot-2026-06-22-at-20103-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Depending on what a patron chooses, they will then be brought to the cart and then to the checkout for their donation.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/rQ5Rwhr5uowLtubV57zWF_screenshot-2026-06-22-at-20429-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Patrons can also update their donation frequency and amount in the cart.
