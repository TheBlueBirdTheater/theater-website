# Share a direct link to donate

Whether it's on social media, in a marketing email, or on posters throughout town, sharing a direct link to your donation page is a great way to reach a larger audience and maximize donations. To find a shareable link and ready-made QR code, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Donations** --> **Share**.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/DBMh4Yle-_1LWTiE_kRHa-20260417-123959.png?format=webp "Document image")

Click **Copy to Clipboard for the URL**. To download the QR code, click the image itself, and it will automatically download.
