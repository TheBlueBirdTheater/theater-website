# Recurring Donations

When your account admin or patrons make a donation, they can set up a recurring donation (monthly, quarterly, or annual).

Additionally, a reminder email will be sent 7 days prior to the renewal.

**Admin View:**

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fNq9KE1lZMAHSE09jHEMv-20260610-145407.png?format=webp "Document image")

**Patron View:**

Patrons will receive an email each time their donation recurs. Each confirmation email will include a direct link to manage their donation (cancel or update payment information). Patrons CANNOT change the amount of the donation.

[Cancel Recurring Donations](https://support.ludus.com/cancel-recurring-donations)
