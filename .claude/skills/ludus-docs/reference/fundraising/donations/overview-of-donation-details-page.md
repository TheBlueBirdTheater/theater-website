# Overview of Donation details page

The donation details page allows you to view all of the data associated with a particular donation. This article will give you a breakdown of all of the data that is available to view.

### Donation Details

-   **Donated** - The amount of the donation.

-   **Received** - The amount of revenue after any fees that were absorbed (only applicable to credit transactions)

-   **Level** - The Donor Level to which this donation qualifies.

-   **Category** - The donation category to which this donation was made.

-   **Memo** - The message that was attached to the donation itself.

-   **Donation Date** - The date and time the donation was made.

### Recurring Info

If the donation is recurring, this box will appear. Otherwise, nothing will display.

_Suggested Article_: [Recurring Donations](https://support.ludus.com/recurring-donations)

-   **Frequency** - The rate at which their donation reoccurs (Monthly or Annually).

-   **Total Charges** - The number of times this donation has been processed.

-   **Total Donated** - The cumulative amount donated.

-   **Status** - Whether the donation is active or canceled.

-   **Manage Link** - The link at which the patron can cancel their donation.

### Donor Details

-   **Full Name** - The name of the patron that is associated with the donation.

-   **Email** - The email of the patron that is associated with the donation.

-   **Phone** - The phone number of the patron that is associated with the donation.

-   **Address** - The address of the patron that is associated with the donation.

### Form Submission

If you have a Form attached to your donations, all responses will appear on the donation details page and in the reporting.

_Suggested Article_: [Forms](https://support.ludus.com/forms)

You can view any questions asked at the time of donation and edit responses as needed.
