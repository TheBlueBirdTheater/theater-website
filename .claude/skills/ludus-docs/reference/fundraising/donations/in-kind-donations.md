# In-Kind Donations

In-Kind donations are donations of goods or services that are not standard monetary donations. Account admins can process these types of donations on the fundraising pages.

### Recording an In-kind donation

1.  Navigate to **Fundraising** and then select **Donations**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/qRL_eN65GsoKoP34eLrwO-20260417-123904.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Click **New Donation**
    
3.  Select the In-kind option.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/5NAE3OEXQjgmZ0mQANOm9-20251218-212123.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Enter the Fair Market Value of the In-Kind Donation as well as a Memo/Description, in addition to all the information you would like to have on the donation, and click the button to Collect (Record) the donation.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/x9JM05mOvggDNJgGuFFoD-20251218-212223.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    The In-Kind donation has now been recorded. You will be returned to the Donations screen and will see the donation listed there with the In-Kind label applied.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/y3KrB1DoSmyw7jXFfPsLH-20260407-191446.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

### About In-Kind Donations

-   In-Kind donations are not included in the Summary, Revenue, or Itemized reports.

-   They are shown on the donations page with their own total directly below the collected amount.

-   The Donation report includes filters for "In-Kind" donations and totals.
