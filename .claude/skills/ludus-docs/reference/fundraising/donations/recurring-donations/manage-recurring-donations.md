# Manage Recurring Donations

To easily manage and monitor your recurring donations, you can go to **Fundraising** (at the top of your account) -> **Donations**.

At the top of this page and the donations dashboard, you will see the option to select **Recurring Donations.**

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/1H87RiPxGaCsGYoRRBJuF_screenshot-2026-06-25-at-33041-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once selected, it will take you to all your patrons with recurring donations and allow you to view and monitor them.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/jqNl8UGKbVS-DtLPjFUk-_screenshot-2026-06-25-at-13855-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

If you do not have any recurring donations, you can still access this page, though no patron data will be available.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/n4VMhvPv8GB7WzzY9KIuL_screenshot-2026-06-25-at-33226-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
