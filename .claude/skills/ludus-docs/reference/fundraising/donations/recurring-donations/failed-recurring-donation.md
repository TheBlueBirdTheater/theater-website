# Failed Recurring Donation

If a patron has a card used for their recurring donation that is no longer active or usable, they will receive 3 emails from Ludus when it attempts to charge that card. These emails will notify the patron that their charge has failed and prompt them to update their information.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-WjTiTq--PUjxXXQrk4zjR-20250813-141640.png?format=webp "Document image")

Each email will say how many attempts are left (2 or 1).

On the admin side, it will also show you exactly what's happening and whether the recurring donation is failing.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-KWtx7rMjs3B4PAMlN3s8_-20250813-141813.png?format=webp "Document image")

Once the final attempt is made and it is unsuccessful, the donation will be canceled. This will appear on the admin side (under their donation), and the patron will also receive an email notification.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-64bIVqCUpsfl5eUiGAxbU-20250813-141918.png?format=webp "Document image")

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/-Wjcg2-gpBfUKvBZQaMyV-7Q6XbuAemWjzFPOliIx4F-20250813-141943.png?format=webp "Document image")

You can reactivate the recurring donation on the admin panel if needed.
