# Enable corporate donations

There might be circumstances where you want a separate list of pre-set donation amounts and [Donor Levels](https://support.ludus.com/donor-levels) on your Donation tab. To accomplish this, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Settings**. Scroll to the section titled "Corporate Donations". Then, click **On** to enable corporate donations. This will create a separate tab under Donations on the patron side where they can toggle between Individual and Corporate donations.
