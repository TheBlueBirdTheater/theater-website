# Hide Donor Level from the public

You might want to keep a specific Donor Level hidden from the general public. To do so, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Levels**.

Then, click **Edit** next to the desired level. Toward the bottom of the page, there is a section titled "Display to Public".

Uncheck this box and click **Save Changes**. Now this level will not be displayed on the patron side.
