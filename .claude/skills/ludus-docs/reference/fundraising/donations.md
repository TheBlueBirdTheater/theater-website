# Donations

In addition to selling tickets, you can allow your patrons to make donations at checkout or separately via the **Donations** tab in your ticketing portal. This is a great way to receive extra money from supportive patrons.

Allowing donations is always at your discretion and is a functionality that you can turn on/off at any time.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/iBFb_3UZR7F7HJ1qpjHJG-20260417-123508.png?format=webp "Document image")

### Data Under the Donations Tab**:**

-   The **Total Collected** under donations for your account.

-   How many **Total Donations** have been collected.

-   **Average Donation Amount** that has been given.

-   Any **Scheduled Donations** (for payout).

-   A graph of **Donations Over Time.**

-   A look at the **Donor Breakdown** (between **New Donors** and **Returning Donors**).

You can choose which comparisons you wish to compare in the data. For example, the last 7 days compared to the previous 7 days.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Zp-YRWgej2ORrYy1KhY1Z-20260417-123617.png?format=webp "Document image")

### **All Donations (Under the Donations Tab):**

**This section includes and shows -**

-   The Donor Name

-   The Type of donation (Cash, Card, In-Kind, etc...)

-   The date of the donation

-   The Donor Level (if applicable)

-   The frequency of the donation

-   The amount of the donation

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/y3KrB1DoSmyw7jXFfPsLH-20260407-191446.png?format=webp "Document image")

_Additionally_, you will be able to access the [Donations Report](https://support.ludus.com/donations-report) , [Share a direct link to donate](https://support.ludus.com/share-a-direct-link-to-donate) , and [Process a donation](https://support.ludus.com/vTav-process-a-donation) .
