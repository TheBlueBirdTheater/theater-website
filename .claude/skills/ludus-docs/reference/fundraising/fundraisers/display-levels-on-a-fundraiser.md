# Display Levels on a Fundraiser

If you are utilizing Donor Levels, you might wish to use them in conjunction with your Fundraiser. To toggle this on and off, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Fundraisers** --> **Edit** (next to the desired Fundraiser) and scroll down to "Additional Options". Next to "Donation Levels", check or uncheck the box that says "Display Donation Levels on fundraising page".

Then, your levels will appear on the main page for your Fundraiser as seen below.
