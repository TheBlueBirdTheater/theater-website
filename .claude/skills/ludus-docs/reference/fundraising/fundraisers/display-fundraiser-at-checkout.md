# Display Fundraiser at checkout

In addition to listing your Fundraiser on your Donation page, patrons can donate to it during checkout. This option is enabled by default.

To toggle this on and off, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Fundraisers** --> **Edit** (next to the desired Fundraiser) and scroll down to "Additional Options".

Next to "Ticket Buying", check or uncheck the box that says "Promote during ticket buying process".

When a patron donates at checkout and selects a category, they will see your Fundraiser listed.
