# Require comments on Fundraiser donations

Fundraisers allow donors to leave comments on your wall. You may request that patrons leave a comment at the time of donation.

To do so, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Fundraisers** --> **Edit** (next to the desired Fundraiser) and scroll down to "Additional Options". Next to "Require Comments", check the "Require Comments" box.
