# Delete a Fundraiser Update

After you publish a Fundraiser, you may realize a typo or even have additional information you wish to add. These can easily be updated through the Fundraiser tab.

1.  Go to **Fundraising** (at the top of your account) -> **Fundraisers**.
    
2.  From here, navigate to the **Fundraiser** you need, and select **Updates**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/KxRWTzX3mgs0FNW_gN8RJ-20260401-193453.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  Once here, go to the top-right corner of the update you wish to delete, then select **Delete**.
    
    You will be prompted to verify that yes, you do wish to delete that Update.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/rh-IMxgl9ethiGsT6_0Sx-20260407-164207.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/EWYcb-GLBZGZ7WM9XCMxX-20260407-164221.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  Once **Yes, Delete** is selected, that Update will be removed from your admin site and the patron site.
