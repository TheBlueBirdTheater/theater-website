# Edit a Fundraiser Update

After you publish a Fundraiser, you may realize a typo or even have additional information you wish to add. These can easily be updated through the Fundraiser tab.

1.  Go to **Fundraising** (at the top of your account) -> **Fundraisers**.
    
2.  From here, navigate to the **Fundraiser** you need, and select **Updates**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/KxRWTzX3mgs0FNW_gN8RJ-20260401-193453.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
3.  From here, you can select **Edit** next to the Update you wish to adjust.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/j8NVs-OSw6gVvitlBCH1O-20260401-193558.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
4.  You can then make the necessary edits and **save** those for your patrons to view under the Fundraiser on the patron site.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/_MbbGVEOjqSaPm0JTc7-r-20260401-193718.png?format=webp "Document image")
