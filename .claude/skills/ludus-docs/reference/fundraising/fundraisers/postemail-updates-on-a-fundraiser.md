# Post/Email updates on a Fundraiser

Fundraisers within Ludus are meant to be an interactive way to receive well wishes from your donors and share progress with updates. These updates will appear on your Fundraiser's main page. To post an update to your campaign, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Fundraisers** --> **Updates** (next to the desired Fundraiser).

Once there, click **New Update**. Insert a subject line and then, in the large text box, place any text, images, or videos. Choose whether you would like to post the update to the main page, email the update to donors for the fundraiser, or both by checking the boxes at the bottom. Hit **Submit** to finalize.
