# Share a Fundraiser

In the ultra-connected world, there are many locations you might want to share your Fundraiser. Whether it's digital with a link or in print with a QR code, here's where to go.

Navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Fundraisers** --> **Share**. To copy the Public or Private links, click **Copy to clipboard**. To download the QR Code, simply click the code, and the image will download.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/n7M3lsd-zuLoIq3v6QlWl_screenshot-2026-06-22-at-15535-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

It is important to share all Links as they are shown on your Share page. Adding the "www" prefix to a link in Ludus will prevent your link from working.
