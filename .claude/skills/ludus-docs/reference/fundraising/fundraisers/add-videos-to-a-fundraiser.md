# Add videos to a Fundraiser

Videos are an engaging way to inform patrons about the fundraiser or provide exciting updates about the progress being made.

To add a general video about the fundraiser, navigate to **Fundraising** --> **Fundraisers** --> **Edit** (next to the desired Fundraiser). Then scroll down to the description titled "Description". Next, click the three vertical dots on the right-hand side of the toolbar.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/9bPp3YgXd4C09F3ZDfI39_screen-shot-2023-07-18-at-45326-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

You will then see a dropdown menu. Click the video icon and insert the URL to the video.

![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/fLIwqAK5LjK2VADdmC6ZG_screen-shot-2023-07-18-at-45527-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")

You might also want to post video updates to your Fundraiser. To do so, I recommend checking out the article about posting updates to your Fundraiser.

_Suggested Article_: [Post/Email updates on a Fundraiser](https://support.ludus.com/postemail-updates-on-a-fundraiser)
