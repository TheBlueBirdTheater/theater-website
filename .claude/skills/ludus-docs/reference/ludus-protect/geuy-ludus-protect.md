# Ludus Protect

Ludus Protect offers your patrons an optional protection add-on at checkout. If something unexpected happens and they cannot attend, they can request a refund through Ludus Protect; your revenue stays with you, our team handles the request, and **Ludus covers the ticket cost**. It is completely free for your organization.

**Ludus Protect** is only available through the Patron checkout process and cannot be added after the order is completed.

Please read through the [Terms and Conditions.](https://hello.ludus.com/ludus-protect "Terms and Conditions.")

_**Additionally, to receive a refund, patrons must follow the link in their confirmation email and submit the refund request if their order has Ludus Protect.**_

## What does it cover?

### **Ludus provides refunds if...**

You cannot attend your event due to any of the reasons below, and have provided the necessary evidence requested below: • Illness / Injury (not including Covid-19) • Pre-existing Medical Condition • Death of Immediate Family • Pregnancy Complications • Public Transport Failure • Flight disruption • Mechanical Breakdown • Adverse Weather • Home Emergency • Jury Service • Court Summons • Armed Forces & Emergency Services Recall

If your event is canceled or postponed by the organizer, the organizer is responsible for refunds. Ludus's customer support team will be able to assist you in finding the correct person to reach out to, but Ludus is prohibited from issuing the refund.

_We may consider other emergency circumstances at our discretion._

Ludus Protect extends to the Add-Ons in the order.

### **General Conditions of Refund**

-   Any reason for a refund must not have been foreseeable at the time you made the booking for the event.

-   We do not refund if you made your booking by mistake, or if it is no longer wanted or needed.

-   If the event is canceled or postponed to a date you cannot attend, please contact the venue or event organizer directly.

-   You must make all arrangements to attend the event, including arranging any necessary travel or documents and allowing suitable travel time.

-   You will be asked to provide supporting evidence at your own expense.

-   Your refund will be made to the same method used to purchase tickets.

-   The maximum refund value will not exceed $1,000 USD or local currency equivalent.

-   Claims must be submitted no later than 14 days following the occurrence of the event.

### Covid-19

Due to the uncertainty of COVID-19, Ludus does not provide refunds for missing a show due to COVID-19.

For a full list of what is covered and what is not, please see our [Terms and Conditions.](https://hello.ludus.com/ludus-protect "Terms and Conditions.")

## What does Ludus Protect Cost?

If a patron elects to purchase Ludus Protect for their order, the cost is passed on to them. The fee for Ludus Protect is a percentage of the overall order price, depending on the ticket price.

**There is no additional cost to your group/organization to offer Ludus Protect**.

Ludus Protect will cover up to $1,000 per order, but will not exceed that amount. For example, if an order was for $50 and your request is approved, you will be refunded $50.

## What does purchasing Ludus Protect look like for a patron?

Once a patron adds tickets to their cart, they will be prompted to buy or decline Ludus Protect during the checkout experience.

## Request a Refund

To apply for a Refund, click the link in your confirmation email. If you do not have your confirmation email, please contact our support team, and they will be able to assist. 

1.  Navigate to the confirmation email in your inbox and scroll down to the bottom, where it says "Refundable Purchase". From here, you will select the **HERE** link (seen below).
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/HwuJ3Fgd6UQlXugLgIQwa_screen-shot-2024-02-14-at-32517-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
2.  Once selected, you will be brought through the process of filing your claim for refund protection.
    
    To start, you will **select** the ticket(s) needing to be refunded and click **Continue**.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/Epl0u3tLg4H-2X-HjlILx_screen-shot-2024-01-23-at-120644-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    Next, you will **fill out** your information and select the reason you are requesting a refund.
    
    Please provide as much supporting evidence as possible while filling out this information and selecting the reason for the refund.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/4N__Df5lS1tAXL1ePDSBA_screen-shot-2024-01-23-at-120836-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    Finally, you will **submit the Application** and be brought to the **Confirmation** page.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/YQjz2_EIjOgYeotKxbiEX_screen-shot-2024-01-23-at-121018-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    
    Additionally, you will receive an email confirmation of the refund application. From this email, you can **view the** application status.
    
    ![Document image](https://images.archbee.com/hZcZIA5As4EsukOh0OMVE/DLL0aBJ4Md2vyFeew6lvH_screen-shot-2024-01-23-at-121340-pm.png?format=webp "Document image")
    

More about Ludus Protect can be found [here](https://hello.ludus.com/ludus-protect "here").

Follow-up on refund claims will occur within 7 days of the application. If you have any questions regarding your claim, you are welcome to reach out to us [via email](https://finance@ludus.com "via email").

## Can a refund be requested for fewer tickets than were ordered initially?

Yes! Suppose one person in the party has a situation that falls under Ludus Protect. In that case, they can request a refund for that person or multiple people, depending on the number of individuals who need to be refunded. This WILL NOT refund and release all of the tickets, only the amount requested.

## How do I know if the patron has been refunded?

If you have the patron's order details, you can search the order/information for the claim on your account.

### **To search:**

1.  Go to your **Ludus account**

2.  Type in the reference number or name of the patron in the **Search Bar/Magnifying Glass**

3.  **Select** the order

4.  **Scroll down** to the tickets, and there will be a shield next to the tickets with information on the refund/if it went through.

The patron will receive an approval or rejection email once the team goes through their claim/submission. If the patron has any questions, they can reach the team by directly responding to their approval or rejection email.

Follow-up on refund claims will occur within 7 days of the application. If you have any questions regarding your claim, you are welcome to reach out to us [via email](https://finance@ludus.com "via email").
